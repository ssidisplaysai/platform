# GECP-0001 Genesis Enterprise Capability Program

Project: Genesis Enterprise Operating System  
Program: GECP-0001 Genesis Enterprise Capability Program  
Mode: Program Charter  
Status: Program Initiation  
Governing Baseline: Genesis Platform v1.0, GAF-0001 Genesis Constitutional Foundation v1.0  
Date: 2026-07-29

## Mission
Formally establish the Genesis Enterprise Capability Program.

This program SHALL build enterprise business capabilities on top of the certified Genesis Platform.
The platform SHALL be treated as the stable foundation.
Future work SHALL maximize enterprise capability, deterministic automation, reusable business knowledge, and enterprise intelligence.
This program SHALL NOT redesign the constitutional platform except where future certified architectural assessments explicitly require.

## Background
Genesis Platform Engineering Program is complete and the platform is architecture-certified with:
1. Runtime Authority
2. Dependency Authority
3. Platform Bootstrap Authority
4. Public Platform APIs
5. Registry Authority
6. Kernel Authority

## Vision
Genesis shall become an Enterprise Operating System capable of understanding, operating, automating, and improving an entire business.

Genesis SHALL operate on a canonical Business Genome representing the enterprise rather than isolated documents or applications.

## Strategic Objectives
The Enterprise Capability Program SHALL develop:
1. Business Genome
2. Enterprise Agents
3. Marketing Intelligence
4. Operations Intelligence
5. Manufacturing Intelligence
6. Financial Intelligence
7. Enterprise Automation
8. Enterprise Applications
9. Knowledge Services
10. Decision Support

## Workstreams
### Workstream I Business Genome
Mission: Create the deterministic enterprise knowledge layer that becomes the authoritative memory of the organization.

### Workstream II Enterprise Agents
Mission: Build specialized enterprise agents that consume the Business Genome and Genesis Platform.

### Workstream III Marketing Platform
Mission: Complete deterministic marketing planning, publishing, SEO, analytics, and optimization.

### Workstream IV Enterprise Applications
Mission: Develop applications that consume Genesis services rather than duplicating platform capability.

### Workstream V Enterprise Intelligence
Mission: Deliver explainable enterprise reasoning using canonical business knowledge.

## Guiding Principles
1. Enterprise capabilities SHALL consume Genesis Platform.
2. Capabilities SHALL NOT duplicate platform services.
3. Business knowledge SHALL originate from canonical evidence.
4. Evidence SHALL remain traceable.
5. Applications SHALL consume public platform contracts.
6. Enterprise intelligence SHALL operate on canonical business objects rather than raw documents.
7. The Business Genome SHALL become the enterprise source of truth.

## Program Success Definition
Genesis shall become capable of operating an entire enterprise using deterministic business knowledge rather than isolated software modules.
