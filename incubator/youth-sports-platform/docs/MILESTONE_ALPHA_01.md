# Alpha 01 - Usable Softball Family Platform

Goal: a real cloud-hosted product that can be used alongside GameChanger during actual softball activity.

## User flows

1. Adult creates account
2. Adult creates family
3. Adult adds athlete
4. Adult creates or joins softball team
5. Team roster is visible
6. Coach/manager creates practice/game/tournament
7. Family sees unified Today/Calendar view
8. Family RSVPs
9. Family opens venue/diamond and directions
10. Family checks in to event
11. Team receives announcement/update
12. Schedule change triggers push notification
13. Game Center displays opponent, inning, score, status
14. Pitcher appearance/stat line can be recorded
15. Photos can be uploaded to the game

## Mobile navigation

- Today
- Teams
- Games
- Places
- Me

## Out of scope for Alpha 01

- full GameChanger replacement
- payments
- recruiting
- live video streaming
- AI highlights
- advanced pitch tracking hardware

Those should be enabled by the architecture but not block first usable release.
