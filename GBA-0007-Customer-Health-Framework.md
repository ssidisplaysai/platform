# GBA-0007 Customer Health Framework

## Health Dimensions

1. Product adoption score.
2. Renewal history score.
3. Support interaction score.
4. Engagement score.
5. Satisfaction score.
6. Executive escalation score.
7. Financial standing score.
8. Operational delivery score.

## Health Classification

1. Risk LOW: overall >= 80.
2. Risk MEDIUM: overall >= 65 and < 80.
3. Risk HIGH: overall >= 50 and < 65.
4. Risk CRITICAL: overall < 50.

## Aggregate Agent Health

1. HEALTHY: no at-risk customers, renewal risk, escalations, or onboarding delays.
2. DEGRADED: limited risk concentration.
3. BLOCKED: multi-signal risk concentration requiring coordinated intervention.

## Output Surfaces

1. /api/gba/customer-success/customer-health
2. /api/gba/customer-success/health
3. /glw/customer-success-agent/customer-health
4. /glw/customer-success-agent/health
