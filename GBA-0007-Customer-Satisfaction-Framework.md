# GBA-0007 Customer Satisfaction Framework

## Satisfaction Signals

1. CSAT score.
2. NPS score.
3. Sentiment trend.
4. Survey summary.
5. Highlighted feedback themes.

## Support Signals

1. Open issues.
2. Escalations.
3. Resolution progress percent.
4. SLA performance percent.
5. Communication summary.

## Framework Outcome

1. Satisfaction and support signals are combined with health and renewal posture.
2. Escalation-heavy accounts are elevated into recommendation and alert surfaces.
3. Dashboard surfaces include customerSatisfactionTrends and supportActivitySummary metrics.
