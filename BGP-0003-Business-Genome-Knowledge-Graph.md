# BGP-0003 Business Genome Enterprise Knowledge Graph & Semantic Relationship Architecture

Project: Genesis Enterprise Operating System  
Program: Business Genome Program

Mode: Architecture + Semantic Model  
Status: FOUNDATION  
Governing Baseline: Genesis Platform v1.0, GAF-0001, GECP-0001, BGP-0001, BGP-0002

## Mission
Define the semantic relationship architecture for the Genesis Business Genome.

## Scope and Boundary
This package defines relationship semantics only.

Out of scope:
1. Graph storage implementation
2. Graph database selection
3. Inference engine implementation
4. API implementation

## Relationship Principles
1. Relationships SHALL be first-class enterprise objects.
2. Relationships SHALL possess deterministic identity.
3. Relationships SHALL support governance, provenance, confidence, lifecycle, version history, explainability, and temporal evolution.

## Graph Principles
1. The graph SHALL describe knowledge rather than operational transactions.
2. Operational systems remain systems of record.
3. Genesis remains the system of enterprise understanding.
