## 1. Executive Summary
This package certifies the Business Genome Foundation v1.0 as the frozen semantic baseline for all future Business Genome engineering.

The foundation is complete, internally consistent, and now active as the governing baseline for follow-on engineering packages.

This package is baseline certification only and introduces no implementation mutation.

## 2. Frozen Foundation
The Business Genome Foundation v1.0 consists of:
- BGP-0001 Business Genome Canonical Object Model
- BGP-0002 Business Genome Evidence Model and Source Architecture
- BGP-0003 Business Genome Enterprise Knowledge Graph and Semantic Relationship Architecture

## 3. Certified Boundary
Certified foundation boundary:
1. Canonical business objects are defined by BGP-0001.
2. Evidence, source authority, provenance, traceability, and confidence are defined by BGP-0002.
3. Semantic relationships, graph governance, temporal structure, inference classes, and validation are defined by BGP-0003.

## 4. Freeze Terms
- Future engineering SHALL extend this foundation rather than redefine it.
- New engineering packages SHALL preserve the frozen object, evidence, and relationship contracts unless superseded by governed architectural decisions.
- No redesign of frozen contracts is authorized under this certificate.

## 5. Engineering Transition
Beginning immediately after this freeze:
- BGP-0004 through BGP-0009 are reserved for engineering packages that consume the frozen foundation.
- Any new authority requirements SHALL be raised through governed package design, not by altering the frozen baseline.
