# Genesis Enterprise Capability Workstreams

Program: GECP-0001 Genesis Enterprise Capability Program  
Status: Program Initiation

## Workstream I Business Genome
Mission: Create the deterministic enterprise knowledge layer that becomes the authoritative memory of the organization.

Scope:
1. Canonical business objects
2. Evidence-linked knowledge graph
3. Deterministic provenance and replay

## Workstream II Enterprise Agents
Mission: Build specialized enterprise agents that consume the Business Genome and Genesis Platform.

Scope:
1. Role-specialized agents
2. Capability-driven orchestration
3. Deterministic decision/action traceability

## Workstream III Marketing Platform
Mission: Complete deterministic marketing planning, publishing, SEO, analytics, and optimization.

Scope:
1. Planning and publishing workflows
2. SEO and performance intelligence
3. Closed-loop optimization guidance

## Workstream IV Enterprise Applications
Mission: Develop applications that consume Genesis services rather than duplicating platform capability.

Scope:
1. Platform-consumer applications
2. Contract-first integration
3. Shared capability reuse

## Workstream V Enterprise Intelligence
Mission: Deliver explainable enterprise reasoning using canonical business knowledge.

Scope:
1. Explainable recommendations
2. Decision support models
3. Cross-capability intelligence synthesis

## Workstream Governance Rules
1. All workstreams SHALL consume platform authorities and contracts.
2. Workstreams SHALL NOT duplicate platform runtime/bootstrap/registry/kernel services.
3. Evidence traceability SHALL be maintained end-to-end.
4. Architecture expansion SHALL require certified assessment when ambiguity is unresolved under current baseline.
