## Genesis Scaling Architecture

GLW is primarily canonical generation plus geographic expansion. ProjectorEnclosure requires a legacy-first campaign model:

1. **Read-only inventory acquisition**: paginate pages/posts/custom collections, read Yoast, public HTTP, redirects, media, and links.
2. **Durable classification repository**: deterministic inventory IDs, source ID/collection/hash, version/timestamp, disposition, ownership, product mapping, actions, risk, safety, opportunity score, dependencies.
3. **Authority graph**: canonical topic owners, product authorities, redirect edges, conflict evidence, and protected certified owners.
4. **Batch compiler**: transform approved candidates into bounded operations with exact hashes, anchors, mutation limits, QA, concurrency locks, and stop conditions.
5. **Shared Genesis services**: reuse target preflight, Product Intelligence, SEO authority, research provider, image generation, contextual-media authority, WordPress transport, redirect authority, strict QA, campaign persistence, and rollback evidence.
6. **ProjectorEnclosure-specific policies**: legacy claim taxonomy, product-role gating, page/post collection resolution, existing-media hierarchy, consolidation graph, niche scoring, and workbook lineage.
7. **Campaign execution**: persist wave/batch state, candidate leases, before/after evidence, public acceptance, failure code, rollback result, and generated-media cleanup.
8. **Geography gate**: enable state/city generation only after ownership, product authority, links, claims, and canonical parents are stable.
