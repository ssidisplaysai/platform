## Claim-Authority Finding

The current literal policy reports 8 findings on California, 10 on AZ/CO/CT/FL/GA/NC/NY, and 12 on Illinois. Most are false positives caused by class keywords in negated, cautionary, interrogative, or procurement-planning prose, including “not to select technical specifications by guesswork,” “request written confirmation,” “if touch ... is being considered,” and price-comparison caveats. The climate findings direct readers to official weather authority rather than asserting climate facts. Illinois adds two generic requirement phrases that need policy review.

This is a systemic `CLAIM_CLASSIFIER_FALSE_POSITIVE` / `STALE_QA_POLICY` defect. The policy must distinguish positive factual assertions from negation, questions, conditional planning, requests for confirmation, and authority citations. It must not be weakened to permit unsupported positive claims. Existing content should then be rerun read-only through the corrected policy before any HTML repair decision.
