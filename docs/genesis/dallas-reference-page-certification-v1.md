## Proven reusable rules

1. Product truth can anchor generated contextual imagery.
2. Local context improves familiarity without false proximity.
3. Market intelligence can determine which applications deserve emphasis.
4. Application emphasis must emerge from evidence rather than arbitrary page design.
5. Local authority links can add real usefulness.
6. Multiple semantic media roles materially improve page composition.
7. Rich composition produces a more credible commercial page than undifferentiated long-form editorial output.
8. Market intelligence must be able to reject weak opportunities.
9. Owner visual review remains valuable before publication.
10. Preview, authenticated draft, and public render require separate certification.

These rules are reusable only when their source identities, product authority, claims, and evidence remain current.

## Publication attempt and revocation

The exact owner-reviewed WordPress object `13084` was published at `https://projectorenclosure.com/fan-cooled-projector-enclosures/texas/dallas/`. Its approved content, SEO identity, featured media, semantic media, hierarchy, and claims were preserved through authenticated post-publish readback and independent public verification.

Public capture is separate from authenticated draft capture. Independent browser validation found two visible H1 elements: the Cerato theme page title and the approved composition H1. It also confirmed a measured 107-pixel theme-shell overhang at the 1024 breakpoint. These violate the requested public gate even though the approved `post_content` itself remains exact.

The reference certification was therefore revoked append-only and object `13084` was returned to the exact approved draft. Dallas was returned to `draft_ready`; Austin remained `reference_complete`; Houston and San Antonio remained `queued`. No new dispatch, execution, or dispatch date was created. The failed public captures and all rollback events remain durable evidence.
