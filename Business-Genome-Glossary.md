# Business Genome Glossary

Program: BGP-0001  
Status: FOUNDATION

## Core Terms
1. Canonical Object: An implementation-independent enterprise concept represented in normalized form.
2. Canonical Identifier: Deterministic identity of a canonical object.
3. Evidence Reference: Pointer to source material that supports a business fact.
4. Confidence State: Maturity stage of trust for a canonical object.
5. Validation Status: Governance assertion about canonical object conformance.
6. Relationship Taxonomy: Approved set of relationship types used in the Business Genome.
7. Lifecycle State: Operational maturity state of object instance or object type.
8. Supersession: Governance transition from prior version to successor version.
9. Lineage: End-to-end ancestry and derivation trail.
10. Explainability: Ability to trace canonical facts back to evidence and decisions.

## Domain Terms
1. Organization Domain: Enterprise structural entities and hierarchies.
2. Manufacturing Domain: Production resources and execution entities.
3. Product Domain: Commercial and technical product definitions.
4. Knowledge Domain: Policies, standards, and enterprise documentation.
5. Evidence Domain: Provenance, extraction, and validation records.
6. Operations Domain: Workflows, tasks, and decisions.
7. Compliance Domain: Regulatory and audit entities.
8. Technology Domain: Application and infrastructure entities.
9. Metrics Domain: KPI and objective measurement entities.
