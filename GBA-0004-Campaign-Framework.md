# GBA-0004 Campaign Framework

## Model
Campaign plans are immutable planning artifacts with lineage, audience, budget, channel focus, and projected outcomes.

## Behavior
1. Each campaign plan is created through the marketing runtime.
2. Each plan records an immutable lineage checksum.
3. Each plan can append history and timeline events.

## Operational Rule
Campaign planning is advisory and governed. It is not the publishing or scheduling kernel.
