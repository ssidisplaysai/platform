# Genesis Founder Journal

Version: 1.0  
Status: FROZEN PROCESS

## Purpose
The Genesis Founder Journal is the permanent historical record of how Genesis learned to think.

It preserves the evolution of founder understanding.

It documents discoveries, not brainstorming.

It records the moments that permanently changed the direction, philosophy, purpose, or identity of Genesis.

The Founder Journal exists before standards, before architecture, and before product implementation.

It is the birthplace of enduring ideas.

## Archivist Role
The Archivist of Genesis does not invent.

The Archivist does not improve.

The Archivist does not mature ideas prematurely.

The Archivist faithfully preserves the evolution of founder thinking with historical accuracy, contextual preservation, and intellectual continuity.

## Founder Journal Rule #1
The Founder Journal does not preserve everything the founder says.

It preserves the moments that permanently changed how Genesis thinks.

## Founder Discovery Test
Before creating any Founder Journal entry, answer:
1. What did we discover today that we did not understand yesterday?
2. Why does this discovery matter?
3. Does this permanently influence how Genesis thinks?
4. Will this discovery still matter ten years from now?
5. Is this a genuine discovery rather than a temporary idea?
6. Does this deserve to become part of Genesis's permanent historical record?

If the answer to Questions 5 or 6 is No, do not create a Founder Journal entry.

## Entry Template
Each Founder Journal entry contains:
- Journal Number
- Title
- Date
- Context
- Discovery
- Founder Realization
- Why This Matters
- Impact on Genesis
- Questions Still Unanswered
- Future Possibilities
- Possible Future Artifact(s)
- Status

## Status Values
Allowed status values:
- INCUBATING
- EXPLORING
- MATURING
- READY FOR WORKING PAPER

Disallowed starting states:
- APPROVED
- CERTIFIED
- CANONICAL
- STANDARD
- FROZEN

## Lifecycle
Conversation

-> Founder Journal

-> Working Paper

-> Founder Review

-> Official Artifact

-> Published

-> Frozen

Ideas earn permanence.

They never begin there.

## Long-Term Purpose
The Genesis Founder Journal exists so that future generations of Genesis can understand not only what Genesis became, but how it became Genesis.

It preserves the evolution of thought before ideas become institutional knowledge.

It protects the intellectual history of Genesis.
