# Relationship Boundaries

The Relationship Runtime may resolve only what is supported by governed evidence and approved upstream contracts.

It must handle:
- relationship candidate creation from governed inputs;
- canonical relationship identity derivation;
- relationship classification;
- directionality assignment;
- cardinality representation;
- confidence preservation;
- entity linkage;
- replay linkage;
- immutable provenance retention;
- append-only lineage retention;
- contradictory relationship preservation without silent resolution;
- unresolved relationship states when evidence is insufficient;
- supersedence and retirement.

Contradictory relationships must remain deterministic under identical inputs, must retain provenance, and must not be resolved by the Relationship Runtime. Resolution authority remains downstream.

No guessing is permitted. If evidence is insufficient, the relationship must remain unresolved.
