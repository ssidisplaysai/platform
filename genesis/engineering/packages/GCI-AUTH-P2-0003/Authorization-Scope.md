# Authorization Scope

## Authorized Scope
The Relationship Runtime may deterministically:
- consume approved upstream contracts and records;
- create governed relationship candidates;
- assign canonical relationship identity;
- classify relationship type;
- set relationship directionality and cardinality;
- preserve confidence, provenance, and lineage;
- link to replay and entity references;
- version relationship records;
- model supersedence and retirement;
- maintain deterministic registry behavior.

## Unchanged Scope
The authorization does not alter any upstream runtime contract and does not add new rights to other Phase 2 semantic runtimes.

## Explicit Exclusions
This authorization excludes Business Rule Runtime, Business Genome Assembly Runtime, persistence, scheduling, orchestration, deployment, AI or LLM inference, heuristics, probabilistic reasoning, OCR, queues, workers, and crawlers.
