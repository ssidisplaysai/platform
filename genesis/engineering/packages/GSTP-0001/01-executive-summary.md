# Executive Summary

This package establishes the constitutional and architectural foundation for the Genesis STONER Platform as a Genesis-consuming platform and defines preliminary constitutional placement for the STONER Partner Network.

## Outcomes
- Repository discovery completed with evidence-backed path references.
- Ownership boundaries documented across Genesis, GSTP, SPN, external providers, and human operators.
- Mandatory commercial invariants documented as architecture constraints, not legal conclusions.
- Preliminary bounded contexts, lifecycle models, attribution constitution, and ledger constitution documented.
- Capability reuse matrix completed against existing Genesis constitutional and runtime capability surfaces.
- Program roadmaps for GSTP and SPN drafted with explicit non-scope boundaries.
- Decision backlog and risk registers created with review ownership tagging.

## Critical Finding
Identifier authority is resolved: GSP remains occupied by Genesis Specification Governance, and GSTP is assigned to Genesis STONER Platform. Package naming, references, and roadmap identifiers were normalized accordingly.

## Non-Implementation Confirmation
No implementation was introduced for QR features, links, attribution engines, commission engines, payouts, storefronts, databases, APIs, workflows, or UI.
