# GSTP Constitutional Boundary

## In Scope
- Constitutional placement of Genesis STONER Platform.
- Constitutional placement of SPN as first subordinate application.
- Ownership boundaries and capability responsibilities.
- Preliminary lifecycle, attribution, ledger, inactivity constitutional models.

## Out of Scope
- Feature implementation.
- Runtime implementation.
- API, workflow, scheduler, UI, database, migration, and external provider implementation.

## Hierarchy
- Genesis
- Genesis STONER Platform
- STONER Partner Network
- Future STONER applications

## Mandatory Commercial Invariants
1. Compensation linked to verified product sales.
2. Recruitment alone does not generate compensation.
3. Enrollment alone does not generate compensation.
4. No purchase required for compensation access.
5. No forced inventory maintenance for eligibility.
6. Rank/status not purchasable.
7. Personal purchases cannot deceptively qualify status.
8. Returns/refunds/fraud/cancellation/chargeback affect eligibility.
9. Commission calculations deterministic.
10. Commission calculations explainable.
11. Rule versions explicit.
12. Historical calculations auditable.
13. Ledger records not silently rewritten.
14. Corrections represented as reversal/adjustment/replacement entries.
15. Sponsor lineage distinct from compensation lineage.
16. Sponsor lineage may exceed compensation depth.
17. Compensation depth policy-controlled.
18. Earnings claims evidence-governed.
19. Inactivity does not erase identity or ledger history.
20. Administrative actions permissioned and audited.
21. Privacy boundaries prevent inappropriate exposure.
22. Customer payment details excluded from partner analytics.
23. Reactivation preserves identity continuity.
24. Partner IDs never recycled.
25. QR destinations changeable without asset reprint.
26. Raw attribution evidence preserved separately from final decision.
27. Architecture supports future legal/accounting/tax/fraud/privacy review.
