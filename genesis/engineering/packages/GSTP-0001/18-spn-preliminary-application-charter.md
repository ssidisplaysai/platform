# SPN Preliminary Application Charter

## Mission
Enable governed partner-network sales attribution and compensation workflows for STONER business channels while preserving deterministic, auditable, and privacy-bounded operations.

## Users
- Individual ambassadors
- Affiliate marketers
- Content creators
- Store partners
- Retailers
- Online resellers
- Event vendors
- Regional representatives
- Distributors
- Internal STONER sales representatives

## Responsibilities
- Partner classifications and programs.
- Sponsor and sub-partner relationships.
- QR and short-link assignment policy.
- Attribution policy execution (future implementation).
- Compensation policy execution (future implementation).
- Partner lifecycle and inactivity governance.
- Partner ledger visibility.
- Dispute and compliance flows.

## Prohibited Responsibilities
- Commerce checkout ownership.
- Payment processor ownership.
- Tax authority ownership.
- Replacing Genesis constitutional capabilities.

## Relationships
- To GSTP: SPN is subordinate to platform-level policy and governance.
- To Genesis: consumes identity, authority, lifecycle, validation, audit, and publication models.
- To Commerce: receives eligible sales/order events through governed integration boundaries.
- To Payments/Accounting/Tax: consumes external settlement and compliance context via explicit boundaries.

## Initial Launch Boundary
Constitutional architecture only in this package. No implementation.

## Future Implementation Packages
SPN-0002 through SPN-0017 as defined in roadmap.
