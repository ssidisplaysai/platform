# GSTP Platform Charter

## Mission
Establish a Genesis-native, Genesis-consuming platform for the STONER business ecosystem that coordinates shared STONER policies, shared identity boundaries, and application-level integration governance.

## Constitutional Status
- Draft charter for architecture foundation package.
- Not approved.
- Not registered as canonical program artifact; registration workflow remains pending governance approval.

## Relationship to Genesis
GSTP consumes Genesis constitutional, registry, identity, lifecycle, eventing, workflow, audit, lineage, publication, validation, and certification capabilities where they already exist.

GSTP does not replace Genesis and does not redefine Genesis-owned constitutional meaning.

## Business Scope
- Shared STONER platform concerns spanning multiple future applications.
- Initial constitutional placement of SPN only.
- Future domains include Passport, Rewards, Collectibles, Commerce, Inventory, Retail, Stores, Manufacturing, Events, Community.

## Shared Responsibilities
- STONER platform vocabulary.
- STONER platform policy overlays where Genesis allows extension.
- STONER cross-application governance extensions.
- STONER cross-application integration boundaries.
- STONER privacy policy direction for app-level data boundaries.

## Prohibited Responsibilities
- Redefinition of Genesis constitutional authorities.
- Runtime duplication of Genesis registry or identity services.
- Implementation of SPN feature services in this package.

## Non-Goals
- No commerce engine implementation.
- No partner compensation implementation.
- No payout implementation.
- No UI or API implementation.

## Principles
- Deterministic policy interpretation.
- Explicit lineage and traceability.
- Append-only ledger principles for financial evidence surfaces.
- Strict authority separation.
- No silent overrides.

## Governance Model
- Architectural decisions escalate to architecture authority.
- Business policy decisions escalate to product authority.
- Legal/accounting/privacy/security-sensitive decisions remain open until reviewed.

## Dependency Policy
- Prefer Genesis constitutional services and runtime components first.
- External providers only through explicit boundary contexts.

## Integration Policy
- All application integrations must preserve Genesis invariants.
- Cross-application integrations must preserve tenant and privacy boundaries.

## Data Ownership Direction
- Genesis owns constitutional governance primitives.
- GSTP owns STONER platform-level semantics and policies.
- SPN owns SPN domain behavior and policies.

## Privacy and Security Direction
- Principle of least exposure.
- Strict separation between partner analytics and customer payment data.
- Administrative actions must be permissioned and auditable.

## Versioning and Certification Direction
- Rule and policy versions must be explicit.
- Historical records must remain auditable and non-silent.
- Certification expectations align to Genesis review and governance lifecycle models.
