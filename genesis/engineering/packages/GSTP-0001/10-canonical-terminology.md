# Canonical Terminology

## Platform and Application Terms
- Genesis: constitutional and runtime authority platform.
- Genesis STONER Platform: Genesis-consuming STONER program platform.
- STONER application: subordinate application under GSTP.
- STONER Partner Network: first planned STONER application.

## Partner Terms
- Partner: a participating seller actor under SPN policy.
- Partner Account: identity and lifecycle container for a partner.
- Partner Classification: configurable class attached to partner account.
- Partner Program: policy bundle assigning eligibility and rules.
- Seller: broad commercial participant term.
- Affiliate: partner class for referral-based selling context.
- Ambassador: partner class for advocacy and selling context.
- Store Partner: physical storefront partner class.
- Retailer: partner class representing retail business actor.
- Reseller: partner class representing resale actor.
- Distributor: partner class for distribution role.

## Relationship and Lineage Terms
- Sponsor: partner responsible for sponsorship relationship.
- Sponsored Partner: partner linked through sponsorship relationship.
- Sponsor Lineage: historical sponsor relationship graph.
- Compensation Lineage: graph used to determine compensation routing.
- Direct Seller: partner with direct sale relationship.
- Override Recipient: partner eligible for override compensation.

## Sale and Attribution Terms
- Qualified Retail Sale: sale that meets policy eligibility.
- Eligible Sales Value: value basis used for compensation rules.
- Attribution: governed process linking conversion to partner context.
- Attribution Evidence: raw evidence supporting attribution.
- Attribution Candidate: candidate attribution outcome before resolution.
- Attribution Decision: resolved attribution outcome.
- Attribution Window: policy-defined attribution time boundary.

## Link and Campaign Terms
- QR Asset: physical or digital QR representation.
- QR Assignment: governed mapping of QR asset to destination context.
- Short Link: managed short redirect reference.
- Redirect: governed destination routing outcome.
- Campaign: marketing initiative identity context.
- Conversion: event interpreted as conversion under policy.

## Compensation and Ledger Terms
- Commission: compensation result from eligible sales value and rule.
- Commission Rule: policy logic for commission determination.
- Commission Rule Version: explicit immutable version of rule logic.
- Commission Entry: ledger entry representing commission outcome.
- Adjustment: additive or corrective ledger entry.
- Reversal: offset entry negating prior entry effects.
- Cash Payout: monetary payout outcome.
- Merchandise Credit: product/merchandise credit outcome.
- Inventory Credit: inventory account credit outcome.
- Partner Ledger: partner-visible ledger scope.
- Administrative Ledger: admin audit ledger scope.

## Lifecycle and Governance Terms
- Account Status: lifecycle state of partner account.
- Qualified Activity: policy-defined activity preserving active status.
- Inactivity: threshold state before or at inactivation.
- Suspension: temporary restricted state.
- Archival: preserved non-active state with history retention.
- Reactivation: governed return to active pathway preserving identity.

## Territorial and Compliance Terms
- Territory: geographic or market boundary assignment.
- Exclusivity: exclusivity policy boundary.
- Dispute: formally raised challenge against decisions/outcomes.
- Earnings Claim: externally communicated earnings statement.
- Fraud Signal: indicator requiring fraud/risk review.
- Partner Health Score: policy-defined partner performance/risk signal.

## Vocabulary Restriction
The terms downline, upline, and MLM are not canonical application terms. They are reserved only for legal, regulatory, migration, or comparative discussion contexts.
