# Dependency Map

## Upstream Dependencies
- Genesis Constitution and constitutional publication governance set.
- Existing Genesis specification governance artifact with identifier GSP-0001.
- Genesis architecture and engineering package standards.

## Internal Package Dependencies
- Discovery report feeds capability inventory.
- Capability inventory feeds reuse matrix.
- Reuse matrix feeds roadmap and decision backlog.
- Decision backlog and risk registers feed readiness assessment.

## External Dependency Categories
- Payment processing
- Payout providers
- Tax reporting
- Identity verification
- Fraud systems
- Ecommerce platforms
- Shipping systems
- Messaging providers

## Dependency Invariants
- Dependency direction must remain subordinate to Genesis authority boundaries.
- No dependency may redefine constitutional authority.
- External dependency cannot become hidden source of constitutional truth.
