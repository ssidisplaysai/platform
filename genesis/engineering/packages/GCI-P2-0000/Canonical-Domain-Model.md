# Canonical Domain Model

## Core Definitions
- Business Identity: deterministic canonical identifier for a business semantic object.
- Business Entity: canonical semantic representation of a business subject.
- Business Relationship: directional semantic link between entities.
- Business Rule: deterministic evaluable semantic policy or behavioral constraint.
- Ownership: explicit accountable authority over an entity, relationship, rule, or decision.
- Responsibility: assigned operational duty bound to an owner.
- Capability: reusable business ability delivered by people, process, systems, and controls.
- Process: governed sequence of business activities producing defined outcomes.
- Asset: business-valued resource under ownership and lifecycle control.
- Evidence: immutable governed source material.
- Observation: interpreted evidence statement without final canonical promotion.
- Fact: canonical accepted observation under defined confidence and policy thresholds.
- Inference: deterministic derived statement produced from governed facts and rules.
- Constraint: non-negotiable boundary condition that limits semantic state transitions.
- Policy: formal governance rule defining permitted and prohibited semantic outcomes.
- Dependency: directional requirement relationship affecting semantic validity.
- Lineage: full causal chain from source evidence to current semantic state.
- Provenance: source origin, custody, and transformation metadata.
- Confidence: quantitative semantic certainty measure.
- Trust: policy-governed reliability score for a source, process, or semantic artifact.
- Version: immutable snapshot of semantic state at a point in governed evolution.
- Supersedence: explicit declaration that a newer version replaces active status of an older version.
- Conflict: incompatible candidate semantic states requiring deterministic resolution.
- Business Genome: canonical integrated semantic graph of business identity, structure, behavior, governance, and lineage.
