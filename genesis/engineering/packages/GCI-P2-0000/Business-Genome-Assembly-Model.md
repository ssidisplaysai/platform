# Business Genome Assembly Model

## Assembly Objective
Compile canonical semantic layers into a single Business Genome artifact.

## Assembly Inputs
- canonical entities
- canonical relationships
- deterministic rule outcomes
- ownership and responsibility mappings
- causality chains
- lineage/provenance references

## Assembly Outputs
- Business Genome version
- genome identity and fingerprint
- completeness and integrity status

## Assembly Invariants
- deterministic assembly algorithm
- append-only version lifecycle
- full lineage closure across all contributing artifacts
- no mutation of source semantic records
