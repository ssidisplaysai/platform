# Confidence and Trust Model

## Confidence
Confidence is the quantitative certainty assigned to a semantic claim based on deterministic evidence interpretation and policy thresholds.

## Trust
Trust is the policy-governed reliability score assigned to a source, pathway, or semantic producer.

## Model Rules
- Confidence and trust are explicit fields, not implicit assumptions.
- Confidence and trust changes are versioned.
- Rule outcomes may be constrained by minimum trust thresholds.

## Invariants
- deterministic score computation
- transparent scoring factors
- lineage reference for every score
