# Business Semantic Lifecycle

## Lifecycle Stages
Observation Declared

↓

Observation Interpreted (IBR)

↓

Entity Bound

↓

Relationship Bound

↓

Rule Evaluated

↓

Genome Assembled

↓

Version Published

↓

Superseded (if newer canonical state exists)

## Lifecycle Guarantees
- Each stage is deterministic.
- Each transition is explicitly recorded.
- Each published semantic version remains traceable and replayable.
- Supersedence preserves full historical lineage.
