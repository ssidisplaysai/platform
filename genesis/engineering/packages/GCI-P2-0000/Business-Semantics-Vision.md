# Business Semantics Vision

Genesis shall transform deterministic runtime artifacts into deterministic business understanding.

The target outcome is a canonical Business Genome that captures entities, relationships, rules, ownership, causality, confidence, trust, and lineage as governed semantic state.

The architecture exists to ensure:
- business interpretation is reproducible
- semantic identity is canonical
- semantic evolution is versioned
- governance remains first-class

The Business Genome is not a marketing narrative. It is a deterministic compiled representation of business reality under constitutional controls.
