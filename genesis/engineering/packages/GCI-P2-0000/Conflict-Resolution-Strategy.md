# Conflict Resolution Strategy

## Conflict Types
- identity conflict
- attribute conflict
- relationship direction conflict
- rule outcome conflict
- ownership conflict

## Resolution Framework
1. Detect conflict deterministically.
2. Classify conflict type.
3. Apply policy-ordered resolution rules.
4. Record decision, confidence, and trust impacts.
5. Publish superseding canonical state.

## Invariants
- conflict resolution is deterministic
- conflict decisions are traceable
- unresolved critical conflicts prevent canonical promotion
