# Certification Strategy

## Certification Philosophy
Every Phase 2 runtime shall be independently certified before integration.

## Certification Domains
- determinism
- immutability
- replayability
- traceability
- boundary enforcement
- dependency validation
- lineage validation
- identity validation

## Certification Outcomes
- CERTIFIED
- CERTIFIED_WITH_CONDITIONS
- BLOCKED

## Gate Rule
No runtime integration proceeds without successful independent certification evidence.
