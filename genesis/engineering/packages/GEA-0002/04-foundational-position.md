# 04 Foundational Position

## Program Separation

GEA-0001 responsibilities:
1. Atlas ontology and topology specification.
2. Entity and relationship class definition.
3. Navigation, query, ownership, lifecycle, validation, certification, impact, and boundary semantics.

GEA-0002 responsibilities:
1. Evidence discovery architecture.
2. Parsing and extraction architecture.
3. Relationship instantiation architecture.
4. Ownership, lineage, and traceability generation architecture.
5. Validation and certification-readiness architecture.
6. Deterministic Atlas emission architecture.

## Governance Position
1. GEA-0002 consumes and preserves GEA-0001.
2. GEA-0002 does not replace GEA-0001.
3. Completion of GEA-0002 does not freeze GEA-0001.
4. Reconsideration of GEA-0001 status requires formal governance review.
