# Business Genome Assembly Boundaries

## Authorized Assembly Role
Assemble previously validated runtime outputs into canonical Business Genome artifacts deterministically and immutably.

## Authorized Inputs
- immutable evidence runtime objects
- immutable evidence validation outcomes
- immutable manifests
- immutable replay records
- immutable IBR observations
- immutable canonical entities
- immutable canonical relationships
- immutable business-rule evaluations and lineage

## Authorized Outputs
- canonical Business Genome identity
- immutable genome record
- append-only lineage transitions
- supersedence and retirement transitions
- unresolved and contradictory-preservation artifacts

## Forbidden Assembly Behavior
- inventing or inferring missing semantics
- resolving contradictions by non-deterministic policy
- resolving identity or relationship conflicts beyond existing canonical upstream outputs
- evaluating rules or recalculating upstream rule results
- mutating upstream records
