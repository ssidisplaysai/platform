# Genesis Service Certification Model

## Certification Dimensions
Every enterprise service SHALL support:
- Architecture Certification
- Security Certification
- Performance Certification
- Operational Certification
- Compatibility Certification

## Certification Preconditions
1. Service registration is complete.
2. Owner and capability boundaries are explicit.
3. Dependencies are declared and acyclic.
4. Contracts are published and versioned.
5. Observability and health contracts are declared.

## Certification Outputs
Each certification package must produce:
- Scope and service inventory
- Validation matrix and command evidence
- Findings register with dispositions
- Certification recommendation

## Disposition Outcomes
- APPROVED
- APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS
- NOT APPROVED
