# 01 Review Charter

## Objective
Assess whether Genesis constitutional architecture is sufficiently complete, coherent, traceable, and internally consistent for independent enterprise audit submission.

## Review Principle
The enterprise constitutional audit should not be used to discover avoidable internal defects.

## Review Boundaries
- Architecture and governance artifacts only.
- No substantive remediation inside this package.
- Objective editorial corrections are allowed but not required.

## Pillar Model
The review treats Genesis as one integrated constitutional platform across five pillars:
1. Runtime Foundation
2. Kernel Framework
3. Constitutional Services
4. Business Genome Foundation
5. Canonical Data Model

## Required Readiness Decision
Exactly one final disposition:
- ARCHITECTURE READY
- ARCHITECTURE NOT READY

## Decision Method
- Gather direct authoritative evidence.
- Classify findings by severity.
- Apply readiness rules without optimism bias.
- Preserve historical dispositions as immutable records.

## Scope Completion Statement
All 12 review areas are covered in this package and mapped to deliverables 04 through 15.
