# 18 Terminology Consistency Review

## Reviewed Term Sets
- Vision and framework terms.
- Constitutional services governance terms.
- Runtime freeze terms.
- Business genome compiler terms.

## Consistency Results
- Determinism/traceability/lineage/provenance terms are consistently used.
- Architecture-only and implementation-independent boundaries are consistently asserted.
- Freeze semantics are consistently treated as normative restrictions.

## Drift Controls
- Application vocabulary remains outside framework authority.
- New terms require explicit definition in GEAF artifacts before reuse.
