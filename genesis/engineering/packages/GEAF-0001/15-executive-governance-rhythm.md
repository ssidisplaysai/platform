# 15 Executive Governance Rhythm

## Cadence
1. Evidence intake checkpoint.
2. Pattern extraction checkpoint.
3. Boundary compliance checkpoint.
4. Independent architecture review checkpoint.
5. Approval and publication checkpoint.

## Decisions Required Per Cadence
- Confirm evidence sufficiency.
- Confirm partition correctness.
- Confirm no-implementation conformance.
- Confirm no application logic absorption.

## Escalation
Any boundary breach triggers correction workflow before progression.
