# 13 Inheritance And Dependency Map

## Inherits Architectural Law From
1. GAV-0001 (vision-level first principles and invariants)
2. GAF-0001 (layer/domain/family/dependency law)
3. GCSA-PROGRAM-0001 (program governance architecture pattern)
4. GCSA-0001 (service boundary architecture pattern)
5. AFR-0004 (runtime freeze constraints)
6. BGC-0001 and GAR-0016 (deterministic transformation and provenance patterns)

## Dependency Rules
1. GEAF cannot override upstream constitutional authority.
2. GEAF can classify and reuse patterns but cannot recast application-specific meaning.
3. GEAF recommendations must remain architecture-only.

## Non-Dependencies
GEAF does not depend on implementation repositories, runtime internals, or application deployments for framework validity.
