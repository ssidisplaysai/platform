# 09 AI Architectural Guidance

## Allowed AI Use
- Extract explicit architecture statements.
- Classify statements into universal/Genesis/application partitions.
- Detect conflicts between boundaries and recommendations.

## Prohibited AI Use
- Inventing architecture not supported by evidence.
- Rewriting constitutional authority semantics.
- Proposing implementation/runtime behavior under framework scope.
- Absorbing STONER business logic into Genesis framework law.

## Required AI Output Checks
1. Every architectural claim links to an evidence source.
2. Every recommendation passes boundary constraints.
3. Every new term is validated against terminology governance.
