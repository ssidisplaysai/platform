# Genesis Commerce Platform Catalog Readiness Policy

## Readiness Intent
Readiness determines whether a product is eligible for bounded publication-oriented workflows.

## Blocking Conditions
1. Product disabled.
2. Lifecycle state incompatible.
3. Missing name, slug, sku, or product type.
4. Missing categories.
5. Missing primary site assignment.
6. Missing/disabled assignment for primary site.
7. Missing required descriptions.
8. Missing specifications.
9. Missing primary image reference.
10. Missing manufacturer reference.
11. Primary site unavailable or disabled.
12. Site publication status incompatible.
13. Hidden visibility state.
14. Missing required profile references.
15. Missing required user permission.

## Warning Conditions
1. Catalog status review_required yields warning context while readiness remains blocked until all blockers are resolved.
