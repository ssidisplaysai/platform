# Genesis Executive Recommendations

## Executive Position
Genesis has achieved substantial architectural and domain certification progress, but enterprise-wide governance closure is not yet sufficient for Version 1.0 declaration.

## Recommendations
1. Declare interim state: READY FOR BETA (not Version 1.0).
2. Charter a short-lived Governance Closure Sprint focused only on GD-001 to GD-004.
3. Freeze non-critical new feature intake until branch convergence reaches agreed threshold.
4. Require each program family to publish normalized lifecycle and certification status.
5. Approve a single V1.0 gate checklist and attach accountable owners per gate.
6. Execute a final independent governance revalidation before declaration vote.

## Decision Guardrails
- Do not override governance evidence with runtime-only health signals.
- Do not declare V1.0 while catalog integrity remains unresolved.
- Do not broaden release claims beyond domains with certified evidence.

## Executive Risk Statement
Declaring Version 1.0 before governance closure introduces high reputational and operational governance risk.

## Executive Opportunity Statement
A disciplined final closeout will create a reusable enterprise operating model for future Genesis releases.
