# Business Rule Theory

## Purpose
Define constitutional theory for deterministic business rule evaluation.

## Theory Statement
A business rule is a governed constitutional knowledge assertion that evaluates evidence-derived facts and relationship-derived context to produce deterministic outcomes.

## First Principles
1. Evidence Derivation
- Rules SHALL evaluate facts derived from governed evidence.
- Rules SHALL NOT create new evidence.

2. Determinism
- Identical facts, relationships, rule versions, and compiler versions SHALL produce identical outcomes.

3. Constitutional Governance
- Rules SHALL be governed, versioned, and certifiable.

4. Non-Invention
- Rules SHALL NOT infer facts beyond governed input scope.

5. Auditability
- Every rule decision SHALL retain rationale and provenance links.

## Rule Knowledge Contract
Business rules encode constitutional knowledge constraints, obligations, and decisions over canonical enterprise state.

## Rule Composition Theory
Composed rules SHALL preserve deterministic ordering, explicit dependencies, and governed conflict resolution.

## Rule Authority
Rule authority SHALL be established by governance-approved source, version lineage, and certification status.
