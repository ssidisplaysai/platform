# Genesis Manufacturing Quality Model

## Quality Authority Scope
Quality owns:
1. Inspection results
2. Defects
3. Corrective actions
4. Non-conformance records
5. Machine calibration references

## Quality Principles
1. Quality records are immutable.
2. Every defect is traceable.
3. Every inspection is attributable.
4. Corrective actions preserve causation lineage.

## Quality Process Integration
1. Quality verification gates production completion.
2. Defect severity and disposition influence release and closure decisions.
3. Non-conformance records link to work order, job, operation, and batch context.

## Quality Governance Outcomes
1. Deterministic compliance evidence.
2. End-to-end traceability for audit and root-cause analysis.
3. Reliable quality metrics for enterprise intelligence.
