# Genesis Manufacturing Authority Model

## Manufacturing Owns
1. Work Orders
2. Production Jobs
3. Operations
4. Work Centers
5. Machines
6. Production Routing
7. Bills of Process
8. Production Scheduling
9. Material Consumption Records
10. Quality Records
11. Production History
12. Labor Tracking
13. Manufacturing Events
14. Manufacturing KPIs

## Manufacturing Does Not Own
1. Customers
2. Quotes
3. Sales Orders
4. Invoices
5. Payments
6. Inventory quantities
7. Purchasing
8. Shipping

## Authority Enforcement Rules
1. Manufacturing references Commerce identifiers; it does not author commercial commitments.
2. Manufacturing requests inventory actions; it does not hold stock authority.
3. Manufacturing publishes manufacturing events; it does not publish finance or shipping authority events.
4. Cross-domain interaction is contract-based only.

## Boundary Integrity Verdict
Manufacturing authority is unique and non-overlapping with Commerce, Inventory, Finance, and Shipping authorities.
