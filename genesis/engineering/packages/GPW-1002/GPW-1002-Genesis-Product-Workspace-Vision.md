## Hero Question
Which products create the greatest enterprise value, and why?

Every screen, interaction, recommendation, visualization, and decision model shall reinforce this question.

## Workspace Rationale
Products are enterprise capabilities with connected effects across revenue, margin, customer trust, manufacturing reliability, and operational load.

The Product Workspace exists to convert product information into decision-quality understanding.

## Executive Narrative
The Product Workspace follows the canonical narrative:

Orientation

-> Confidence

-> Decision

-> Context

-> Action

-> Continuity

-> Inquiry

Narrative purpose in Product Workspace:
- Orientation: Clarify current portfolio posture and strategic product landscape.
- Confidence: Surface product health, value posture, and trust indicators.
- Decision: Present highest-consequence product decisions.
- Context: Show the evidence, dependencies, and changes shaping product outcomes.
- Action: Recommend accountable investment, recovery, or retirement actions.
- Continuity: Preserve lifecycle, timeline, and decision history.
- Inquiry: Allow executive investigation of product value and risk questions.

## Product Pulse
Product Pulse should answer:
- Which products are strongest right now?
- Which products are weakening?
- Which value signals are improving or declining?
- How confident are we in each product posture?

Required pulse dimensions:
- Current state
- Trend direction
- Confidence level
- Value impact signal

## Product Portfolio Overview
Portfolio overview should reveal:
- Value concentration by product group
- Strategic exposure to declining segments
- Portfolio balance across growth, core, and sunset products
- Enterprise dependency concentration risk

## Product Health
Product Health combines:
- Value posture
- Profitability posture
- Lifecycle posture
- Knowledge maturity posture
- Dependency risk posture

Product health question:
Can this product continue creating enterprise value under current conditions?

- Manufacturing complexity
- Finance exposure
- Sales dependencies
- Operations dependencies
- Knowledge maturity
- Mission control priorities

Product Workspace must connect to Enterprise Atlas with:
- Related capabilities
- Dependencies
- Upstream impacts
- Downstream impacts
- Shared enterprise context

Required integrations:

- Approval lineage for recommendation trust
- Confidence rationale sourced from governed knowledge artifacts

- Top Decision
- Decision Owner
- Business Impact
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Expected Outcome
- Risk of Inaction
- Related Enterprise Capabilities

Decision policy:
Rank decisions by enterprise consequence, not by recency.

## Recommendation Model
Every recommendation shall include:
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Business impact
- Expected outcome
- Risk of inaction
- Related enterprise capabilities

Recommendation principle:
Move from portfolio awareness to accountable strategic commitment.

## Confidence Model
Confidence model components:
- Evidence quality
- Data freshness
- Assumption load
- Dependency stability
- Approval maturity
- Cross-capability agreement strength

Confidence bands:
- High: Action-ready recommendation with low unresolved uncertainty
- Medium: Actionable recommendation with explicit caveats
- Low: Not decision-ready without additional validation

## Product Timeline
Product Timeline captures material portfolio events:
- Product launches and phase transitions
- Value inflection points
- Profitability shifts
- Risk escalations
- Executive decisions and outcomes

Timeline rule:
Narrative significance over event volume.

## Product Search
Product Search prompt philosophy:
"Which product decision matters most right now, and why?"

Search must support:
- Question-led portfolio inquiry
- Product-to-enterprise impact discovery
- Comparative decision framing
- Transition from question to explainable recommendation

## Explainability Center
Explainability Center must answer:
- Why this recommendation exists
- How we know
- What evidence supports it
- What assumptions exist
- How confident we are
- What enterprise capabilities are affected

Non-conformance rule:
Recommendations without explainability are not strategic-decision ready.

## TTU Target
Time to Understanding target:
- Executives should understand portfolio posture and top priorities in under 90 seconds.
- For a focused product, value and risk posture should be interpretable in under 30 seconds.

## TTD Target (Time To Decision)
Time To Decision target:
- Executives should identify the next highest-value product decision within 3 minutes.
- High-confidence recommendations should support commitment readiness in one review cycle.
