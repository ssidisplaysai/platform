# Genesis Release Office

## Mission
Operate the official governance authority for Genesis release progression and promotion decisions, beginning with Version 1.0 readiness closure under GRP-0001.

## Responsibilities
1. Preserve authoritative assessment baselines and decision context.
2. Define and maintain promotion gates and gate evidence standards.
3. Coordinate cross-domain release readiness reviews.
4. Maintain release dashboard and backlog visibility.
5. Govern executive decision packets for promotion outcomes.
6. Record auditable promotion approvals and outcomes.

## Authority
The Release Office is authorized to:
- Define release governance controls and gate contracts.
- Require evidence for gate progression.
- Escalate unresolved blockers to executive leadership.
- Recommend promotion state transitions.

The Release Office is not authorized to:
- Override constitutional governance decisions.
- Override certification authority decisions.
- Declare Version 1.0 without required approvals.

## Release Lifecycle
1. Baseline Assessment Freeze
2. Governance Gate Execution
3. Certification Gate Execution
4. Integration Gate Execution
5. Production Validation Gate Execution
6. Executive Approval Gate Execution
7. Promotion Decision and Recording

## Promotion Authority
Promotion decisions require successful gate completion and executive approval.
Allowed states:
- READY FOR GENESIS 1.0
- READY FOR RELEASE CANDIDATE
- READY FOR BETA
- NOT READY

## Governance Relationship
The Release Office consumes constitutional governance artifacts and enforces release-specific controls aligned with governance policy.

## Certification Relationship
The Release Office depends on certification authorities for completeness and exception closure decisions; it does not alter certification outcomes.

## Audit Relationship
The Release Office maintains auditable release records, evidence links, approvals, and decision history for internal and external assurance reviews.

## Future Version Ownership
The Release Office owns governance orchestration for all future Genesis version promotions, including maintenance of gate standards, readiness dashboards, and executive decision protocols.

## Founding Statement
GRP-0001 formally establishes the Genesis Release Office as the governance control center for promotion to Version 1.0 and beyond.
