# Operating Model

## Department Contributions

| Department | Purpose | Responsibilities | Success Metrics | Primary Relationships | Dependencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leadership | direction and stewardship | strategy, governance, prioritization | strategic objective attainment | all departments | foundation governance |
| Marketing | discovery and narrative | campaigns, positioning, engagement | awareness quality, conversion quality | community, commerce, media | product, analytics |
| Retail | channel execution | store operations, customer experience | store trust score, retail performance | commerce, inventory, support | product, logistics |
| Manufacturing | quality and provenance | lot controls, quality assurance | defect rate, provenance completeness | product, inventory, finance | suppliers, compliance |
| Commerce | transaction integrity | order and policy governance | conversion quality, fulfillment reliability | retail, finance, support | inventory, payments |
| Partner Network | ecosystem expansion | partner lifecycle and policy adherence | partner retention quality | rewards, commerce, support | identity, governance |
| Passport | participation identity | participation milestones and lifecycle | participation depth index | rewards, community, events | identity, analytics |
| Rewards | value exchange | earn/redeem policy and balances | redemption quality, reward trust | passport, finance, partner | commerce, policy |
| Community | belonging and contribution | member engagement and contribution loops | engagement quality index | events, marketing, support | media, governance |
| Events | experiential depth | event lifecycle and participation continuity | attendance quality, repeat participation | community, passport | operations, partners |
| Customer Support | trust recovery | resolution and guidance | first-contact resolution quality | all customer-facing teams | knowledge, policy |
| Finance | economic integrity | reconciliation, liability, controls | reconciliation accuracy | commerce, rewards, leadership | policy, systems |
| Legal | compliance and risk | policy constraints, contract guardrails | compliance incident rate | leadership, governance | privacy, operations |
| Analytics | decision intelligence | KPI stewardship, insight generation | metric integrity score | leadership, AI, operations | data ownership |
| AI | augmentation and prediction | forecasting, recommendations, assistive tooling | model utility and safety scores | analytics, operations, marketing | governance, data |
| Technology | enabling infrastructure planning | architecture alignment and implementation planning | architecture conformance rate | all departments | governance, architecture |
