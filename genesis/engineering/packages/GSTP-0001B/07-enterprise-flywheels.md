# Enterprise Flywheels

## Customer Flywheel
- Inputs: trust, product quality, clear onboarding.
- Outputs: repeat participation, advocacy.
- Feedback loop: better participation data improves relevance.
- Compounding effect: lower churn, higher relationship capital.

## Collector Flywheel
- Inputs: authenticity, rarity context, provenance.
- Outputs: collection depth, community influence.
- Feedback loop: collector activity improves curation quality.
- Compounding effect: higher long-term value density.

## Retail Flywheel
- Inputs: inventory reliability, staff readiness, local relevance.
- Outputs: conversion quality, loyalty.
- Feedback loop: local insights improve assortment and events.
- Compounding effect: stronger channel durability.

## Partner Flywheel
- Inputs: fair policy, enablement, transparency.
- Outputs: partner retention, network quality.
- Feedback loop: qualified partner outcomes improve onboarding criteria.
- Compounding effect: sustainable channel expansion.

## Manufacturing Flywheel
- Inputs: standards, QA discipline, lot traceability.
- Outputs: product trust, fewer defects.
- Feedback loop: defect learning improves process.
- Compounding effect: brand reliability.

## Community Flywheel
- Inputs: meaningful participation, recognition, events.
- Outputs: belonging, contribution quality.
- Feedback loop: engaged members create better community value.
- Compounding effect: social resilience and retention.

## AI Flywheel
- Inputs: clean data, governance, human oversight.
- Outputs: better forecasts and recommendations.
- Feedback loop: outcome evaluation improves model utility.
- Compounding effect: operational intelligence maturity.
