# Business Operating System

## System Model
Vision drives objectives.
Objectives drive capability priorities.
Capabilities drive architecture ownership.
Architecture drives application boundaries.
Applications drive engineering scope.
Engineering drives operations and customer outcomes.
Outcomes inform vision refinement through governance.

## Operating Coherence Requirements
- Shared definitions across departments.
- Explicit ownership and dependency contracts.
- Review cadence linking strategy to execution evidence.
- Governance controls that block drift from foundation principles.

## Success Condition
The enterprise operates as one coherent system where strategy, architecture, and execution remain aligned over time.
