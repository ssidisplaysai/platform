# Enterprise Metrics

## Ecosystem Health Metrics
- Relationship Health Index
- Trust Index
- Participation Depth Index
- Community Contribution Quality Index
- Partner Success Index
- Retail Performance Quality Index
- Manufacturing Quality Conformance
- Customer Experience Confidence Score
- Passport Activity Index
- Collector Completion Index
- QR Engagement Quality Index
- AI Adoption and Utility Index

## Executive Dashboard Metrics
- Strategic objective attainment score
- Risk exposure by category
- Cross-functional execution reliability
- Governance conformance score
- Lifecycle progression and retention quality

## Metric Design Rules
- Metrics must reinforce trust, not just growth.
- Metrics must map to a clearly owned domain.
- Metrics must be reviewable in governance cadence.
