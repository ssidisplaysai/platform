# Cross-Functional Collaboration Model

## Collaboration Loops
- Leadership <-> Analytics: objective tracking and strategic adjustment.
- Marketing <-> Community <-> Events: participation growth.
- Manufacturing <-> Inventory <-> Commerce: quality and fulfillment integrity.
- Partner Network <-> Rewards <-> Finance: incentive and settlement coherence.
- Technology <-> Governance: architecture conformance and change control.

## Interface Rules
- Every handoff requires owner, dependency, and success metric.
- Cross-functional conflicts escalate through governance cadence.
