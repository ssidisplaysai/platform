# Executive Governance Rhythm

## Governance Cadence
- Weekly: operating metric pulse and risk watch.
- Monthly: objective scorecard and dependency review.
- Quarterly: strategic adjustment and architecture conformance review.
- Annual: foundation alignment check and roadmap recalibration.

## Decision Layers
- Strategic decisions: executive and governance authority.
- Architectural decisions: architecture authority and review gate.
- Operational decisions: department owners within governed boundaries.
