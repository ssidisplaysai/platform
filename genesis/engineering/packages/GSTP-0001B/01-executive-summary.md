# Executive Summary

GSTP-0001B provides the definitive enterprise playbook for STONER, connecting vision, strategic objectives, operating model, lifecycle journeys, enterprise metrics, readiness sequencing, and future roadmap under Foundation governance.

## Outcomes
- Executive blueprint established.
- Enterprise strategy and long-horizon differentiation documented.
- Measurable objectives and KPI framework defined.
- Department operating model and interdependencies mapped.
- Multi-actor experience maps documented.
- Flywheel compounding logic documented.
- Enterprise health metrics defined beyond revenue.
- Implementation readiness and sequencing documented without implementation initiation.

## Boundary Confirmation
This package is business operating architecture only and introduces no software implementation.
