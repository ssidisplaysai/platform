# Experience Maps

## Persona Journeys

### First-Time Customer
- Goals: confidence, clarity, trustworthy first purchase.
- Touchpoints: discovery, catalog, checkout, onboarding.
- System interactions: product identity, commerce, support.
- Opportunities: reduce trust friction, improve onboarding.
- Success measures: first-purchase confidence score, repeat intent.

### Returning Customer
- Goals: continuity, recognition, value progression.
- Touchpoints: personalized offers, passport, rewards.
- System interactions: identity, rewards, history.
- Opportunities: increase participation depth.
- Success measures: repeat participation index.

### Collector
- Goals: authenticity, rarity context, provenance confidence.
- Touchpoints: collectible releases, provenance records.
- System interactions: product, collectibles, passport.
- Opportunities: improve completion and discovery quality.
- Success measures: collector completion and trust indices.

### Partner
- Goals: fair growth, transparent policy, sustainability.
- Touchpoints: onboarding, enablement, attribution visibility.
- System interactions: partner network, rewards, finance.
- Opportunities: reduce ambiguity and disputes.
- Success measures: qualified partner retention.

### Retail Store
- Goals: local trust and conversion quality.
- Touchpoints: in-store experience, events, support.
- System interactions: retail, inventory, commerce.
- Opportunities: tighten store-community loops.
- Success measures: store trust and performance scores.

### Distributor
- Goals: reliable demand and supply coherence.
- Touchpoints: inventory planning, logistics collaboration.
- System interactions: inventory, manufacturing, commerce.
- Opportunities: reduce variance and shortages.
- Success measures: channel fulfillment reliability.

### Manufacturer
- Goals: consistent quality and traceable output.
- Touchpoints: lot creation, QA, release.
- System interactions: manufacturing, product, inventory.
- Opportunities: quality loop acceleration.
- Success measures: conformance and traceability completeness.

### Employee
- Goals: clarity, alignment, accountable execution.
- Touchpoints: planning, handoffs, governance gates.
- System interactions: operating model and review gates.
- Opportunities: reduce coordination friction.
- Success measures: execution reliability index.

### Executive
- Goals: strategic coherence, risk visibility, scalable growth.
- Touchpoints: dashboard reviews, governance decisions.
- System interactions: analytics, governance, objectives.
- Opportunities: faster quality decisions.
- Success measures: objective attainment and risk posture.
