## Inheritance Assertions
1. GSTP-M0001 defines governing philosophy intent.
2. GSTP-0001 inherits philosophy and defines constitutional boundaries.
3. GSTP-0001A inherits constitution and partitions enterprise domains and contexts.
4. GSTP-F0001 inherits and enforces governance and review controls.
5. GSTP-0001B inherits architecture and defines business operating translation.
6. GSTP-0002 inherits playbook and defines canonical enterprise semantics.
7. GSTP-0003 and GSTP-0004 must inherit completed foundation authority before application constitutions begin.
8. Engineering inherits application constitutions and cannot redefine upstream meaning.
