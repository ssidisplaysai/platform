# Foundation Collection Terminology

- Foundation Collection: authoritative grouping of foundational philosophy, constitution, and enterprise architecture artifacts.
- Constitutional Hierarchy: ordered governance chain from philosophy through engineering.
- Inheritance: downstream levels adopting upstream constraints without semantic override.
- Non-Override Rule: prohibition on redefining upstream governing meaning.
- Conformance Gate: review control that enforces inheritance and boundary integrity.
- Certification Outcome: declared architecture certification status after evidence review.
- Implementation Readiness: architecture and governance determination of safe execution timing.
