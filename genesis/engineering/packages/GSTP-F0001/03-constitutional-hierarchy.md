# Constitutional Hierarchy

## Permanent Levels
- Level 0: Foundational Philosophy (GSTP-M0001)
- Level 1: Enterprise Constitution (GSTP-0001)
- Level 2: Enterprise Architecture (GSTP-0001A)
- Level 3: Foundation Governance (GSTP-F0001 completed)
- Level 4: Enterprise Playbook (GSTP-0001B completed)
- Level 5: Enterprise Semantic Contracts (GSTP-0002 completed)
- Level 6: Enterprise Service Contracts (GSTP-0003 not started)
- Level 7: Enterprise Policy Contracts (GSTP-0004 not started)
- Level 8: Application Constitutions (not started: Partner Network, Passport, Commerce, Inventory, Manufacturing, Retail, Marketing, Community, Rewards, Analytics, AI)
- Level 9: Engineering (runtime, applications, services, APIs, UI, infrastructure)

## Inheritance Rule
Every level must inherit intent and constraints from the level above.

## Non-Override Rule
Lower levels may specialize but may not redefine higher-level governing meaning.
