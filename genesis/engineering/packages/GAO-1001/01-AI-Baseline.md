# 01 AI Baseline

Baseline:
- Certified platform release: GPR-1.5
- All previously certified platform boundaries remain frozen.

Foundation intent:
- Introduce a Genesis AI orchestration layer that coordinates AI execution.
- Preserve business logic, Workflow, Scheduling, Messaging, Authentication, and Authorization boundaries.

Platform responsibilities:
- AI provider abstraction
- Model registry
- Agent registry
- Prompt registry and rendering
- Tool registry and execution
- Execution planning and routing
- Memory abstraction
- Safety policy enforcement
- Audit and metrics
- Mission Control observability
