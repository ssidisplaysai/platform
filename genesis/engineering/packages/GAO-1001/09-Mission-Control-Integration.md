# 09 Mission Control Integration

Mission Control integration:
- Health endpoint surfaces provider, model, agent, prompt, tool, memory, audit, and metrics posture.
- Metrics endpoint exposes execution counts, token usage, cost, latency, retries, and fallback behavior.
- AI observability is designed for Mission Control compatibility.

Integration posture:
- The AI orchestration layer is observable without taking ownership of Mission Control routing or identity boundaries.
