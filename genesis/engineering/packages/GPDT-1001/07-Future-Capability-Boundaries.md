# 07 Future Capability Boundaries

1. Product bundles.
2. Product kits.
3. Configurable products.
4. Attribute sets.
5. Option sets.
6. Multi-brand catalogs.
7. Localized product content.
8. Digital products.
9. Subscription product definitions.
10. Product versioning.
11. Replacement and supersession relationships.
12. Cross-sell relationships.
13. Upsell relationships.
14. Compatibility rules.
15. Product enrichment.
16. Supplier product references.
17. Channel-specific product presentations.
18. AI-assisted enrichment.

Boundary constraints for future capabilities:

1. No canonical ownership transfer outside Product domain.
2. No execution authority transfer into Product for Inventory, Manufacturing, Commerce, CRM, or Finance operations.
3. AI-assisted enrichment remains recommendation-based and subject to Product authority.
4. Mission Control remains observational only.
5. Compiler and Business Genome constitutional authority remains intact.

Adoption rule:

- Any future capability must pass the conformance checklist and preserve all non-ownership boundaries defined in this package.
