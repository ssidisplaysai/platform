# Semantic Conflict Resolution Model

## Process
1. Detect conflict through validation or governance review.
2. Route to primary owner and impacted secondary consumers.
3. Evaluate against constitutional and architecture inheritance constraints.
4. Propose canonical resolution and migration mapping.
5. Approve through Semantic Governance Council.
6. Publish versioned semantic update with traceability evidence.

## Escalation
Unresolved conflicts escalate to executive governance authority.
