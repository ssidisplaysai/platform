# Terminology Governance

## Alias Policy
- Aliases exist for discoverability and migration only.
- Canonical term must be present anywhere alias is used in authoritative records.

## Deprecation Policy
- Deprecated terms require replacement mapping and effective date.
- Deprecated terms cannot be reactivated without governance approval.

## Conflict Resolution Policy
- Conflicts are logged as semantic authority disputes.
- Primary owner proposes resolution.
- Semantic Governance Council approves final canonical decision.

## Future Terminology Process
1. Proposal submission with business rationale.
2. Duplicate and conflict detection.
3. Ownership assignment confirmation.
4. Cross-domain review.
5. Approval and versioned publication.

## Reserved Words
Identity, Passport, Product, SKU, Order, Invoice, Payment, Commission, Policy, Audit Record, Ledger Entry, Executive Metric, Business Objective, Capability, Shared Service, AI Agent.

## Forbidden Terminology
- Multiple terms claiming equal canonical authority for one concept.
- Ambiguous ownership terms such as universal owner or shared canonical owner.
- Runtime-specific names presented as business semantic authorities.
