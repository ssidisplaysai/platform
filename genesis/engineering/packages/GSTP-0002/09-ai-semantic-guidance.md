# AI Semantic Guidance

## AI Semantic Contract
AI systems must interpret enterprise semantics through GSTP-0002 canonical definitions only.

## Mandatory Controls
- Ownership Lock: AI must not invent or alter semantic ownership.
- Meaning Lock: AI must not create duplicate canonical meanings.
- Terminology Lock: AI must resolve aliases to canonical terms.
- Inheritance Lock: AI must preserve foundation and enterprise architecture intent.
- Policy Lock: AI interpretations must remain within approved policy semantics.

## Prevented Failure Modes
- Hallucinated ownership
- Duplicate semantic authorities
- Conflicting terminology
- Unauthorized reinterpretation

## AI Output Requirement
Every semantic statement emitted by AI should include canonical term, owner, and semantic version reference when used in authoritative decisions.
