# Executive Summary

GSTP-0002 establishes the authoritative semantic contract library for the STONER Enterprise.

## Scope Outcome
- One canonical semantic definition per enterprise concept.
- One owning business domain per enterprise concept.
- Explicit ownership, lifecycle, relationships, invariants, prohibited interpretations, and extension guidance.
- Terminology governance for aliases, deprecations, reserved terms, and conflict resolution.
- AI interpretation constraints preventing unauthorized semantic reinterpretation.

## Governance Position
This package inherits and preserves foundational authority from GSTP-M0001, GSTP-0001, GSTP-0001A, GSTP-F0001, and GSTP-0001B.
No foundation philosophy, constitutional boundary, or enterprise architecture intent is redefined.
