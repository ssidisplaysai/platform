# Semantic Business Rules

Semantic business rules define meaning constraints only.
No implementation, runtime, API, database, or UI behavior is specified.

## Rule Set
1. Canonical term precedence: canonical term overrides aliases in all authoritative documents.
2. Ownership exclusivity: each concept has one primary semantic owner.
3. Lifecycle integrity: concept state changes must preserve origin and identity lineage.
4. Relationship legitimacy: parent and child relationships must comply with relationship matrix.
5. Traceability: rewards, commissions, invoices, payments, ledger entries, and audit records require auditable semantic origin.
6. Objective alignment: executive and operational metrics must map to business objectives and owned capabilities.
7. Authorization grounding: authentication and authorization semantics must remain policy-anchored.
8. AI constraint: AI outputs are advisory interpretations bounded by canonical definitions only.
9. Deprecation discipline: deprecated terms remain traceable and cannot become active authorities.
10. Reserved term protection: reserved words cannot be reassigned to alternate meanings.
