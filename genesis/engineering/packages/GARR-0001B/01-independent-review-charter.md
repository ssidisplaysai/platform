# 01 Independent Review Charter

## Review Objective
Conduct an independent constitutional revalidation of GARR-0001 MAJOR blockers FR-001 through FR-004 after completion of GARR-0001A.

## Independence Constraints
1. Review-only package.
2. No remediation and no implementation work.
3. No architectural redesign unless a critical defect is discovered.

## Review Method
1. Reconstruct original GARR-0001 finding intent from authoritative records.
2. Inspect GARR-0001A remediation artifacts and evidence.
3. Validate remediated artifacts directly in governance sources.
4. Execute independent command-level validation checks.
5. Issue per-finding disposition: VALIDATED CLOSED or REOPENED.

## Review Scope
- Included findings: FR-001, FR-002, FR-003, FR-004.
- Excluded from closure decision: FR-005 editorial item (non-blocking).

## Decision Authority
Readiness disposition in this package is a gate recommendation for GEA-0002 preparation only and does not initiate GEA-0002 automatically.
