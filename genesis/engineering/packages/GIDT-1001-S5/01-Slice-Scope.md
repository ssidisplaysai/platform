# 01 Slice Scope

Slice 5 implements reservation and allocation commitments on top of Slice 1-4 foundation and movement capabilities.

Implemented:
- Reservation lifecycle operations.
- Allocation lifecycle operations.
- Deterministic reservation-to-allocation conversion.
- Query surfaces for reservations and allocations.
- Runtime service registration tokens and hooks.

Not implemented:
- Persistence and external APIs.
- Lot/serial/expiration behavior.
- Receiving/picking/packing workflows.
