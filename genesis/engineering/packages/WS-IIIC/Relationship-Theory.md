# Relationship Theory

## Purpose
Define the constitutional theory of enterprise relationships as evidence-governed connections between canonical entities.

## Theory Statement
A relationship exists only when governed evidence demonstrates a valid connection between a source canonical entity and a target canonical entity under an approved relationship rule.

## First Principles
1. Evidence Primacy
- Relationship existence SHALL be derived from evidence.
- Assertion without evidence SHALL be invalid.

2. Determinism
- For identical evidence, rule set version, compiler version, and canonical inputs, relationship outcomes SHALL be identical.

3. Non-Invention
- Genesis SHALL NOT infer or fabricate relationships beyond governed evidence.

4. Auditability
- Every decision SHALL contain rationale and traceable provenance.

5. Temporal Validity
- Relationships SHALL carry governed validity windows and lifecycle state.

## Relationship Identity
A relationship SHALL be uniquely identified by governed relationship identity fields that include:
- Relationship identifier
- Source entity identifier
- Target entity identifier
- Relationship type identifier
- Effective time window
- Governing version identifiers

## Contradiction Handling
When supporting and contradicting evidence coexist:
1. The contradiction SHALL be preserved.
2. Authority weighting SHALL be applied by approved rules.
3. Decision rationale SHALL explicitly explain the outcome.
4. Replay SHALL reproduce the same conflict resolution result.

## Relationship Truth Contract
Relationship truth in WS-IIIC is constitutional truth, meaning:
- Governed by approved rules
- Derived from governed evidence
- Reproducible by replay
- Certifiable by independent attestation
