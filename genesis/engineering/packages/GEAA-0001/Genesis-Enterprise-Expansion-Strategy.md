# Genesis Enterprise Expansion Strategy

## Expansion Constraints
New applications must:
1. Declare authoritative ownership scope.
2. Register contracts.
3. Declare dependencies.
4. Register permissions.
5. Register health and telemetry.
6. Register governance metadata.

## Additive Evolution Rule
New applications are additive and must not require ownership reassignment in existing applications unless governed by an explicit constitutional decision package.

## Expansion Review Gates
1. Ownership uniqueness check.
2. Boundary determinism check.
3. Dependency acyclicity check.
4. Shared service independence check.
5. Governance metadata completeness check.
