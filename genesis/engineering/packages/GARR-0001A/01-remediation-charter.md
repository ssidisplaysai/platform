# 01 Remediation Charter

## Objective
Close all open MAJOR findings from GARR-0001 (FR-001 through FR-004) through additive constitutional architecture and governance updates.

## Scope Boundaries
1. Allowed: architecture documents, decision records, manifest registrations, and governance synchronization.
2. Not allowed: runtime code, application code, compiler implementation changes, or release/freeze actions.

## Remediation Principles
1. Smallest constitutionally valid change.
2. Explicit source-to-change traceability.
3. Historical preservation of GARR-0001 outputs.
4. Finding-level closure evidence plus regression checks.

## Success Criteria
1. FR-001 closed with first-class Constitutional Services registration and ownership authority.
2. FR-002 closed with explicit dependency-direction authority and ambiguity supersession.
3. FR-003 closed with lifecycle/status normalization authority across governance surfaces.
4. FR-004 closed with explicit ADR approval closure across decision artifacts and manifest.
