# Implementation Risk Management

## Purpose
Define constitutional risk controls for implementation program execution.

## Risk Categories
- Architectural Drift Risk
- Determinism Regression Risk
- Replay Failure Risk
- Provenance Integrity Risk
- Certification Delay Risk
- Conformance Coverage Risk

## Risk Controls
- Risks MUST be tracked with severity, owner, mitigation, and target closure gate.
- High-severity unresolved risks SHALL block phase promotion.
- Medium-severity risks MAY allow conditional gate passage only with governance approval.
- Low-severity risks MAY be deferred with approved closure plan.
