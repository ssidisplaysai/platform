# 15 Mission Control and AI Boundaries

## Mission Control Boundary

Mission Control may observe:
- manufacturing health
- work-order counts
- execution states
- production throughput
- failures
- downtime
- yield
- runtime readiness

Mission Control may not:
- start work orders
- stop work orders
- consume material
- change routing
- record production output
- alter machine assignment
- mutate manufacturing state

## AI Boundary

AI may recommend, analyze, predict, assist planning, suggest scheduling, and flag anomalies.

AI may not own Manufacturing canonical truth. Approved Manufacturing services remain authoritative.
