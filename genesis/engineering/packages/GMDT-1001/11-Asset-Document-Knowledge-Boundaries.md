# 11 Asset Document Knowledge Boundaries

## Asset Boundary

Manufacturing may reference machines, tools, fixtures, equipment, and inspection devices.

Asset Platform retains canonical asset identity and custody where applicable.

Manufacturing owns execution assignment and operational state only.

## Document Boundary

Manufacturing may reference work instructions, drawings, inspection documents, certificates, and production records.

Document Platform retains document custody, revision authority, and canonical artifact authority.

## Knowledge Boundary

Manufacturing may reference procedures, operating knowledge, troubleshooting guidance, safety instructions, and process knowledge.

Knowledge Platform retains semantic authority.
