# 16 Future Capability Boundaries

The following capabilities may eventually be extracted to dedicated platforms:
- Quality Management
- Maintenance
- Scheduling
- Workforce Management
- Procurement
- Supplier Management
- PLM
- MES specialization

Current boundary rule:
- do not create these platforms in GMDT-1001
- document extraction seams only
- preserve Manufacturing execution authority until approved extraction

Potential extraction seams defined now:
- quality hold and inspection orchestration may migrate to a future Quality platform
- machine maintenance planning may migrate to a future Maintenance platform
- generalized scheduling mechanics may migrate to a future Scheduling platform
- workforce identity and workforce policy may migrate to a future Workforce platform
- supplier-facing material procurement may migrate to future Procurement or Supplier platforms
- product lifecycle engineering authority may remain external under a future PLM platform
