# Identity Resolution Boundaries

The Entity Runtime may resolve only what is supported by governed evidence.

It must handle:
- legal names;
- trade names;
- historical names;
- abbreviations;
- localized names;
- translated names;
- brand names;
- aliases;
- duplicate candidates;
- near-duplicate candidates;
- conflicting observations;
- insufficient evidence;
- unknown identity;
- retirement;
- supersedence.

No guessing is permitted. If evidence is insufficient, the identity must remain unresolved.
