# Strategic Principles

1. Every interaction creates value.
2. Every product has identity.
3. Every customer owns a journey.
4. Every reward has provenance.
5. Every QR has purpose.
6. Every application has a bounded context.
7. Shared services remain shared.
8. Customer trust outweighs short-term growth.
9. Long-term collectors are more valuable than short-term buyers.
10. Quality exceeds quantity.
11. Policy authority is explicit and reviewable.
12. Data ownership is singular and accountable.
13. Event ownership is singular and auditable.
14. AI augments human authority; it does not silently replace it.
15. Global scale must preserve local compliance and cultural relevance.
