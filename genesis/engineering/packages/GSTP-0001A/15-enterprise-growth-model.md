# Enterprise Growth Model

## Reinforcing Growth Flywheel
1. Customers discover and purchase.
2. Customers become collectors through identity and provenance.
3. Collectors become community participants through shared engagement.
4. Community participants become partners through trusted pathways.
5. Partners become retailers through capability and governance maturity.
6. Retailers become distributors through operational depth.
7. Distributors expand availability, reinforcing discovery and purchase.

## Flywheel Reinforcement Effects
- Identity depth increases trust and retention.
- Participation artifacts increase switching costs and belonging.
- Partner and channel growth amplifies distribution and storytelling.
- Analytics and AI improve relevance, planning, and operational quality.
