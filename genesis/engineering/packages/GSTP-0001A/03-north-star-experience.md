# North Star Experience

## Journey Stages
1. Discover: prospect encounters story, product, or community signal.
2. First Purchase: customer enters ecosystem with clear value and trust signals.
3. Product Registration: product identity links customer and product lifecycle.
4. Passport: identity-backed participation ledger begins.
5. Rewards: meaningful value exchange for participation and loyalty.
6. Collecting: product and experience collections reinforce brand affinity.
7. Events: physical and digital participation expands identity and belonging.
8. Community: peer identity and shared narrative strengthen retention.
9. Partner: eligible participants enter referral, advocacy, or sales roles.
10. Retail: selected participants or organizations become operators.
11. Distributor: ecosystem participants scale supply and access.
12. Legacy: long-term participants become mentors, curators, and ecosystem anchors.

## Why Each Stage Exists
- To move from transaction to relationship.
- To increase participant identity depth over time.
- To create mutually reinforcing customer, partner, and brand value.
- To sustain long-term growth without sacrificing trust.
