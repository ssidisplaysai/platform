# AI Capability Model

## AI Capability Areas
- Partner coaching and enablement
- Sales forecasting
- Inventory forecasting
- Marketing optimization
- Fraud and abuse detection
- Product and experience recommendations
- Territory recommendations
- Collector intelligence
- Community moderation support
- Knowledge assistants
- Executive analytics copilots
- Agent orchestration and guardrails

## AI Placement
- Core ownership: AI Context.
- Shared dependencies: Analytics, Identity, Governance, Events.
- Policy authority remains in Administration and Governance Context.

## AI Guardrails
- AI can recommend, not unilaterally govern constitutional policy.
- Sensitive decisions require human authority paths.
- Model outputs must remain auditable and explainable where required.
