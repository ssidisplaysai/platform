# Dependency Map

## Upstream Dependencies
- Genesis Foundation registry and certificate artifacts.
- Genesis constitutional services and publication governance artifacts.
- Existing GSTP-0001 package conventions and baseline authority record.

## Internal Dependency Flow
- Discovery informs domains.
- Domains inform capabilities.
- Capabilities inform bounded contexts and application map.
- Context and ownership model inform events, integrations, AI, and roadmap.
- Decision and risk artifacts inform next-package sequencing.

## External Dependency Categories
- Payments, tax, shipping, POS, ERP, CRM, identity verification, messaging, BI.

## Dependency Invariants
- External dependencies cannot redefine enterprise constitutional authority.
- Enterprise domains remain primary semantic owners.
