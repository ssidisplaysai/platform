# Executive Summary

GSTP-0001A defines the enterprise domain architecture for the full STONER ecosystem as a multi-application platform over a 10+ year horizon.

## Outcomes
- Long-term enterprise vision documented for global, multi-channel growth.
- North star journey defined from first discovery to lifelong participation.
- Enterprise business domains and capability model established.
- Bounded contexts and application partitioning proposed.
- Shared platform services, ownership model, and enterprise event model defined.
- QR strategy, AI strategy, and integration strategy documented at architecture level.
- Growth model and strategic principles documented as durable constitutional guidance.
- Decision backlog, risks, and roadmap created for downstream package sequencing.

## Boundary Confirmation
This package is architecture only. It introduces no software implementation.
