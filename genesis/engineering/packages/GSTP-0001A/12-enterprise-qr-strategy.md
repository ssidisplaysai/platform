# Enterprise QR Strategy

## Strategic Position
QR is a shared platform capability that links physical and digital participation across the ecosystem.

## Intended Future Consumers
- Partner Network
- Passport
- Authentication
- Product Registration
- Events
- Rewards
- Retail
- Manufacturing
- Marketing
- Community
- Limited Drops
- Collector Ecosystem
- Warranty
- Support

## Architectural Rules
- QR capabilities are reusable and policy-governed.
- QR does not own commerce, rewards, or identity truth.
- QR interactions are evidence-bearing and traceable.
- QR strategy is architecture only in this package.

## Non-Scope
No QR implementation design, algorithms, storage, or runtime contracts are defined here.
