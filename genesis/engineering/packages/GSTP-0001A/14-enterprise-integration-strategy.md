# Enterprise Integration Strategy

## Integration Domains
- Payments
- Tax
- Shipping
- CRM
- ERP
- POS
- Warehousing
- Marketing platforms
- Email
- SMS
- Authentication providers
- Identity verification
- Financial systems
- Business intelligence platforms

## Strategy Principles
- Integration contracts are explicit and versioned.
- External systems never become hidden constitutional truth sources.
- Provider abstraction boundaries protect long-term portability.
- Privacy, security, and accounting controls are defined before execution packages.

## Integration Ownership
- Integration Gateway Service provides shared transport and policy controls.
- Domain contexts own semantic mapping and acceptance rules.
