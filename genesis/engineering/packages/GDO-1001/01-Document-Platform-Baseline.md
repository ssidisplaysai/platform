# 01 Document Platform Baseline

Implemented baseline at GDO-1001:

- Canonical document identity and type model
- Structured content and metadata ownership
- Template registration and rendering
- Revision history and current revision tracking
- Approval transitions and history
- Signature lifecycle (sign/revoke)
- Lifecycle transitions
- Document relationships
- Asset references (consumer-only)
- Generation service with asset output references
- Audit logging, metrics, and health
