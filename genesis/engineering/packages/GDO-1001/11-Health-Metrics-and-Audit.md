# 11 Health Metrics and Audit

Health checks:

- persistence
- registry
- templates
- revisions
- approvals
- signatures
- lifecycle
- relationships
- assetReferences

Metrics include:

- lifecycle counts
- revision and template counts
- approval/signature counts
- relationship and asset-reference counts
- generation output counts
- recovery/corruption counters
- audit event totals

Audit model:

- append-only audit records for document, template, revision, approval, signature, lifecycle, relationship, generation, and asset-reference events
