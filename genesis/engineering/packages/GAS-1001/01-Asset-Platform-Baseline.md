# 01 Asset Platform Baseline

Implemented baseline at GAS-1001:

- Canonical asset identity and type system
- Metadata and tag management
- Provider abstraction with default filesystem provider
- Version history and checksum tracking
- Integrity verification API
- Relationships and collections services
- Lifecycle operations: archive, soft delete, restore
- Retention policy and legal hold enforcement
- Audit logging and metrics snapshotting
- Health reporting and GOP observability routes
