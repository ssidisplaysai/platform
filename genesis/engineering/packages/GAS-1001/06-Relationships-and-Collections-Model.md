# 06 Relationships and Collections Model

Relationship model:

- Directed relationship between two assets in same tenant
- Supported relationship types:
  - DERIVED_FROM
  - DEPENDS_ON
  - RELATED
  - THUMBNAIL_OF
  - VARIANT_OF
- Self relationships rejected

Collection model:

- Named tenant-scoped collections
- Asset membership tracking both in collection and on asset
- Collection tags and metadata supported
