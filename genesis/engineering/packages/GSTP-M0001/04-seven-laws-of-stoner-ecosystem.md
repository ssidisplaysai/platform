# The Seven Laws of the STONER Ecosystem

1. Relationships Over Transactions
Business decisions prioritize long-term trust above short-term conversion.

2. Every Product Has Identity
Each product must be traceable within customer and ecosystem narratives.

3. Participation Creates Value
Value increases when participants contribute, not only when they buy.

4. Community Is an Asset
Community health is strategic capital and must be protected.

5. Technology Should Disappear
Technology should feel seamless and serve human outcomes, not demand attention.

6. Recognition Must Be Earned
Recognition reflects contribution quality, integrity, and stewardship.

7. Leave the Ecosystem Better
Every participant and every package should improve trust, quality, and clarity.
