# The North Star

## Permanent Mission
Strengthen trusted relationships between people and products through participation, identity, and shared value.

## Long-Term Purpose
Create a multi-generational ecosystem where belonging, authenticity, and stewardship compound over time.

## Customer Promise
You will be seen, respected, and rewarded for meaningful participation.

## Partner Promise
You will operate within transparent rules that protect trust and shared value.

## Employee Promise
You will build systems that preserve human meaning and long-term integrity.
