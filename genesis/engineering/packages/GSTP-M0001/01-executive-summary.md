# Executive Summary

GSTP-M0001 defines the permanent philosophical foundation of STONER. It establishes why the ecosystem exists, what values it must preserve, and how philosophy governs architecture and implementation over a multi-decade horizon.

## Outcomes
- Ecosystem purpose, vision, and north star defined.
- Seven laws and trust principles established as enduring guidance.
- Relationship, collector, and recognition philosophies documented.
- Enterprise flywheel and legacy pathway documented.
- Foundational terminology, decisions, risks, and governance defined.
- Explicit enforcement model provided so architecture and implementation remain subordinate to philosophy.

## Boundary Confirmation
This package introduces no software implementation.
