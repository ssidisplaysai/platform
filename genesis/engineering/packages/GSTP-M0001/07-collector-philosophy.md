# Collector Philosophy

## Why Collecting Matters
Collecting transforms ownership into narrative and long-term engagement.

## Why Rarity Matters
Rarity creates meaning when paired with authenticity and fair access.

## Why Authenticity Matters
Authenticity protects customer trust, brand integrity, and community confidence.

## Why Ownership Matters
Ownership ties identity to responsibility and ecosystem stewardship.

## Why Provenance Matters
Provenance preserves truth across time, enabling trust in every transfer and recognition path.
