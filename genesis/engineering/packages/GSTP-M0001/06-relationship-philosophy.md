# Relationship Philosophy

## Customer Relationships
Trust is built by consistent value, product authenticity, and transparent recognition.

## Partner Relationships
Partnership is stewardship, not extraction; partner growth must reinforce customer trust.

## Retail Relationships
Retail extends brand experience and must preserve quality, identity, and service integrity.

## Community Relationships
Community participation is co-creation; the ecosystem must reward contribution and care.

## Product Relationships
Products are identity artifacts that connect customers to stories, moments, and belonging.

## Manufacturing Relationships
Manufacturing is a trust surface; quality and provenance are non-negotiable.
