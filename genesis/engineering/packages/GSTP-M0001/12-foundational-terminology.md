# Foundational Terminology

- Ecosystem: the full network of participants, products, and relationships.
- Relationship Capital: accumulated trust and participation value.
- Product Identity: canonical identity of a product across lifecycle.
- Participation: intentional actions that strengthen ecosystem value.
- Recognition: evidence-backed acknowledgment of contribution.
- Provenance: traceable lineage of ownership and meaning.
- Stewardship: responsible action that improves ecosystem health.
- Trust Surface: any interaction that can increase or decrease trust.
- Legacy Path: progression from customer to long-term ecosystem leader.
- Philosophical Integrity: preservation of foundational intent over time.
