# The STONER Ecosystem Manifest

## Why STONER Exists
STONER exists to create enduring human relationships around meaningful products, shared participation, and trusted community.

## Why Products Matter
Products are relationship anchors. They carry identity, memory, and participation potential beyond a transaction.

## Why Community Matters
Community turns individual moments into shared momentum. It creates belonging, accountability, and cultural continuity.

## Why Participation Matters
Participation transforms passive customers into active contributors who strengthen the ecosystem.

## Why Ownership Matters
Ownership creates responsibility, pride, and stewardship. It links value to identity and long-term trust.

## Why Technology Serves Relationships
Technology is infrastructure for trust and participation. It must never replace human meaning or purpose.
