# 02 Capability Philosophy

## Foundational Philosophy
1. Capabilities define enduring responsibilities, not implementation mechanisms.
2. Capability authority is constitutional and governance-bound.
3. Capability ownership is explicit, singular, and traceable.
4. Capability dependencies are acyclic and authority-aligned.
5. Capability evolution is governed and non-breaking by default.

## Capability Scope Doctrine
- Genesis capabilities describe platform and governance responsibilities.
- Application capabilities describe enterprise-specific business behavior.
- Genesis may enable business execution but shall not own application business semantics.

## Capability Construction Rules
Every capability definition must include:
- Purpose
- Mission
- Responsibilities
- Ownership
- Owned/consumed/produced artifacts
- Inputs/outputs
- Dependencies
- Interfaces
- Constitutional authority
- Governance requirements
- Validation requirements
- Certification requirements
- Boundaries
- Future evolution guidance
