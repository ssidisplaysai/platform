# 21 Architectural Constraints

## Mandatory Constraints
1. Capability architecture is implementation-independent.
2. Runtime and constitutional freezes remain authoritative.
3. No capability overlap across Genesis vs application ownership.
4. No ambiguity in business capability ownership.
5. No redesign of certified baselines without governance supersession.

## Prohibited Outcomes
1. Genesis owning application business logic.
2. Governance bypass via automation.
3. Runtime redesign through capability expansion.
