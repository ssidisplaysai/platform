# 19 Capability Evolution Strategy

## Evolution Principles
1. Additive-first evolution.
2. Non-breaking semantics by default.
3. Explicit supersession for breaking changes.
4. Authority and dependency review before promotion.

## Evolution Gates
1. Governance gate.
2. Boundary gate.
3. Validation gate.
4. Certification gate.

## Anti-Drift Rule
No capability evolution may transfer application business ownership into Genesis.
