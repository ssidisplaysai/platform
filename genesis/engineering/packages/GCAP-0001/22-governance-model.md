# 22 Governance Model

## Governance Layers
1. Constitutional authority layer.
2. Framework and program architecture layer.
3. Capability governance layer.
4. Validation/certification layer.

## Governance Mechanisms
1. Review and approval gates.
2. Dependency legality checks.
3. Boundary conformance checks.
4. Lifecycle transition controls.
5. Freeze and supersession controls.

## Governance Decision Policy
Any capability ownership conflict is resolved in favor of constitutional boundary preservation.
