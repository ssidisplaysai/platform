# 01 Executive Summary

GCAP-0001 establishes the canonical capability architecture for Genesis OS as a constitutional enterprise platform.

Key outcomes:
1. A complete inventory of current Genesis enterprise capabilities.
2. Classification of capabilities as foundational, supporting, optional, or application-exclusive.
3. Explicit identification of capabilities that must never be owned by Genesis.
4. A non-overlapping partition model separating universal enterprise capability, Genesis capability, application capability, and business capability.

Primary determination:
- Genesis owns enterprise platform capabilities and constitutional governance capabilities.
- Applications own business logic and domain-specific behavior.

Readiness:
- Capability coverage: complete.
- Boundary integrity: pass.
- Governance alignment: pass.
- No-implementation conformance: pass.
