# Genesis Business Genome Integration

## Business Genome Boundary
Business Genome consumes evidence for enterprise-wide traceability and governance.

## Published Evidence
- Production
- Planning
- Execution
- Engineering
- Routing
- Revision
- Quality References
- Operational Metrics

## Rules
- Evidence is published as governed metadata.
- Publishing does not transfer operational ownership.
- Business Genome receives lineage-preserving, versioned evidence only.
