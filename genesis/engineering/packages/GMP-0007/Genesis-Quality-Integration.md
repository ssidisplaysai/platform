# Genesis Quality Integration

## Quality Boundary
Quality remains the authority for inspection, deviation, and corrective-action records.

## Published From Manufacturing
- Inspection Requested
- Inspection Required
- Deviation Recorded

## Consumed By Manufacturing
- Inspection Passed
- Inspection Failed
- Corrective Action Created

## Rules
- Manufacturing shall not own inspection authority.
- Quality outcomes are consumed as authoritative external facts.
- Integration remains deterministic and auditable.
