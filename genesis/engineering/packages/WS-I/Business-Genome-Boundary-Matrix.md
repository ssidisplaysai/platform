# Business Genome Boundary Matrix

## Purpose
Define explicit authority boundaries for canonical model domains and prohibit overlap.

## Boundary Matrix
| Domain | Canonical Authority | Allowed Responsibilities | Prohibited Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Identity Governance | identity namespace, identity derivation rules | business policy ownership |
| Organization | Enterprise Governance | organizational authority and structure | evidence integrity hashing rules |
| Product and Service | Commerce/Service Governance | product and service canonical definitions | employee identity authority |
| Customer and Vendor | Commerce/Supply Governance | external party authority contracts | role permission governance |
| Employee and Role | HR + Governance | workforce identity and role contracts | product canonical ownership |
| Asset and Facility | Operations Governance | physical/digital asset and facility authority | policy constitutional authority |
| Document/Policy/Procedure/Process/Capability | Governance + Operations | normative and procedural contracts | provenance hashing algorithms |
| Evidence | Evidence Governance | evidence contract and integrity controls | canonical object authority reassignment |
| Provenance | Audit + Governance | lineage and traceability chain | evidence source modification |
| Versioning | Governance | transition policy and release state controls | runtime execution behavior |
| Extension Contracts | Architecture Governance | extension safety and compatibility rules | core canonical type mutation |

## Boundary Enforcement Rules
1. One domain cannot authoritatively redefine another domain's primary identity rules.
2. Cross-domain dependencies require explicit relationship and ownership references.
3. Boundary exceptions require constitutional amendment record.
