# WS-I Canonical Business Model

## Purpose
Define the constitutional canonical model that every future Business Genome subsystem must consume without duplicating authority.

## Canonical Model Principles
1. Deterministic identity for every canonical object.
2. Immutable evidence and provenance lineage for every canonical claim.
3. Version-governed lifecycle transitions.
4. Explicit ownership and accountability boundaries.
5. Extension through governed contracts only.
6. No implied authority and no hidden state transitions.

## Canonical Model Scope
The model defines contracts for:
- business identity
- organizations
- products
- services
- customers
- vendors
- employees
- roles
- assets
- facilities
- documents
- policies
- procedures
- processes
- capabilities
- relationships
- evidence
- provenance
- versioning
- ownership
- extension contracts
- invariants
- lifecycle states

## Architectural Layers
1. Identity Layer
- Immutable identity and namespace rules.

2. Entity Layer
- Canonical business entity contracts.

3. Relationship Layer
- Directed, typed, lineage-carrying relationships.

4. Evidence and Provenance Layer
- Evidence origin, validation status, and lineage trace.

5. Governance Layer
- Ownership, versioning, invariants, and lifecycle controls.

## WS-I Completion Criteria
WS-I architecture is complete when:
1. Canonical contracts are deterministic and unambiguous.
2. Ownership and boundary matrices contain no overlap.
3. Invariants and lifecycle rules are complete and auditable.
4. Certification checklist is fully satisfied.
