# WS-IIIE Business Genome Assembly Engine

## Purpose
Define the constitutional architecture for deterministic assembly of the canonical Business Genome from certified compiler products.

## Boundary
The Assembly Engine SHALL assemble certified outputs.
The Assembly Engine SHALL NOT create evidence.
The Assembly Engine SHALL NOT resolve identity.
The Assembly Engine SHALL NOT evaluate rules.

## Constitutional Invariants
1. Assembly SHALL be deterministic for identical governed inputs.
2. Assembly outputs SHALL be replayable and reproducible.
3. Assembly records SHALL be immutable and append-only.
4. Assembly lineage SHALL be auditable end-to-end.
5. Assembly, publication, and certification states SHALL be version-governed.
6. Assembly conformance SHALL be independently certifiable.

## Primary Architecture Domains
WS-IIIE SHALL govern:
- Genome Assembly
- Fact Assembly
- Entity Assembly
- Relationship Assembly
- Knowledge Assembly
- Snapshot Generation
- Delta Generation
- Cross-reference Integrity
- Genome Validation
- Genome Versioning
- Genome Publication
- Genome Replay
- Genome Certification

## Assembly Inputs
WS-IIIE SHALL support deterministic handling of:
- Canonical Entities
- Canonical Relationships
- Validated Facts
- Business Rule Results
- Evidence References
- Identity Ledger
- Relationship Ledger
- Rule Ledger
- Compiler Manifest
- Replay Manifest

## Assembly Outputs
WS-IIIE SHALL support deterministic generation of:
- Business Genome Snapshot
- Business Genome Delta
- Canonical Entity Graph
- Canonical Relationship Graph
- Knowledge Graph
- Evidence Trace Graph
- Identity Ledger
- Relationship Ledger
- Rule Ledger
- Assembly Manifest
- Publication Manifest
- Certification Record

## Out of Scope
No implementation, runtime, services, APIs, database, optimization, AI, or code artifacts are defined in this package.
