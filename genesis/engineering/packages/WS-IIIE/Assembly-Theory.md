# Assembly Theory

## Purpose
Define constitutional theory for deterministic Business Genome assembly.

## Theory Statement
Business Genome assembly is the governed construction of canonical business knowledge from certified compiler outputs, preserving lineage, integrity, and determinism.

## First Principles
1. Non-Invention
- Assembly SHALL NOT introduce knowledge not present in governed inputs.

2. Deterministic Construction
- Identical certified inputs and version context SHALL produce identical assembled outputs.

3. Provenance Continuity
- Every assembled artifact SHALL preserve traceability to evidence, entities, relationships, rules, and manifests.

4. Integrity Preservation
- Referential and cross-reference integrity SHALL be preserved across assembled artifacts.

5. Certification Accountability
- Every assembled genome SHALL be certifiable by independent review.

## Assembly Truth Contract
Assembled genome truth is constitutional truth:
- Derived from certified upstream products
- Governed by approved assembly contracts
- Reproducible through replay
- Independently certifiable
