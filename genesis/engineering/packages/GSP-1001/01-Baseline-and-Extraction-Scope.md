# 01 Baseline and Extraction Scope

Baseline inputs:

1. Knowledge Platform: certified and published baseline.
2. Product Platform: certified and published baseline.

Extraction intent:

1. Runtime composition scaffolding
2. Lifecycle and singleton-host primitives
3. Provider and service registration infrastructure
4. Persistence coordination and schema validation helpers
5. Health/metrics/audit observability shells
6. Mission Control observation fan-out infrastructure
7. Deterministic and normalization utilities
8. Invariant engine and common validators
9. Shared testing utilities

Explicit exclusions:

1. No platform-specific domain entities.
2. No platform-specific service behavior.
3. No platform business ownership transfer.
