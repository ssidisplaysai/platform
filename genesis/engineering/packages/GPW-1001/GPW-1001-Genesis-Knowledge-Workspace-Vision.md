## Mission
Design the Genesis Knowledge Workspace using the approved Genesis Workspace Blueprint.

This is the first production workspace to inherit:
- Genesis Experience Manifesto
- GES-0001
- Genesis Workspace Blueprint
- Executive Narrative
- Executive Decision Philosophy

The Knowledge Workspace is not a document library.

It is not a search engine.

It is not a wiki.

Its purpose is to answer one question better than any software in the world:

"How do we know this is true?"

## Primary Question
How do we know this is true?

Every screen, interaction, recommendation, and visualization must reinforce this question.

## Workspace Identity
### Purpose
Transform organizational knowledge into governed enterprise understanding.

### Mission
Make evidence discoverable, explainable, trustworthy, and connected so high-consequence decisions can be made with confidence.

### Primary Persona
Executive Leadership

### Secondary Personas
- Knowledge Managers
- Governance Officers
- Platform Engineers
- Business Analysts
- Compliance Teams
- Product Teams

### Expected Decisions
- Whether a claim is decision-ready
- Whether evidence is sufficient for approval
- Whether confidence level supports commitment
- Whether a knowledge object requires revalidation
- Whether dependent business decisions should proceed, pause, or escalate

### Business Outcomes
- Higher executive decision confidence
- Reduced uncertainty in governance-critical decisions
- Faster detection of weak or stale knowledge
- Improved traceability from claim to source
- Fewer downstream decisions made on unverified assumptions

## Executive Narrative
The Knowledge Workspace follows the canonical narrative:

Orientation

-> Confidence

-> Decision

-> Context

-> Action

-> Continuity

-> Inquiry

Narrative purpose in Knowledge Workspace:
- Orientation: Clarify what claim, decision domain, and evidence context are active.
- Confidence: Surface current knowledge health and confidence posture.
- Decision: Present the highest-consequence knowledge decisions requiring executive judgment.
- Context: Show what changed, who changed it, and what assumptions are active.
- Action: Recommend governed next actions tied to ownership and expected outcomes.
- Continuity: Preserve timeline history, approval history, and lifecycle movement.
- Inquiry: Allow directed questioning to deepen understanding before commitment.

## Workspace Sections
### Executive Summary
One-screen orientation of current knowledge posture, critical claims, and confidence status.

### Knowledge Pulse
Live posture of knowledge health by domain using state, trend, confidence, and explain signals.

### Decision Layer
Ranked list of top knowledge decisions by enterprise consequence.

### Context Layer
Material context behind each claim, recommendation, and confidence score.

### Relationship Layer
Connected map of claims, evidence, owners, approvals, and business dependencies.

### Recommended Actions
Governed next actions with explicit ownership and risk framing.

### Knowledge Timeline
Narrative timeline of material changes, approvals, reversals, and confidence shifts.

### Knowledge Search
Inquiry-first search centered on enterprise understanding, not keyword retrieval alone.

### Explainability Center
Unified surface that answers why a recommendation exists and how trust is justified.

## Decision Layer
Each decision item shall include:
- Decision title
- Decision owner
- Business impact
- Evidence summary
- Confidence score and rationale
- Expected outcome
- Risk of inaction

Decision ranking policy:
- Order by enterprise consequence and dependency impact.
- Recency alone is non-authoritative.

## Knowledge Pulse
Knowledge Pulse must answer:
- Which domains are healthy?
- Which domains are at risk?
- Why are they in that state?
- How confident are we in current posture?

Required pulse dimensions:
- Current state
- Trend direction
- Confidence level
- Explainability completeness

## Evidence Explorer
Evidence Explorer provides:
- Evidence-to-claim traceability
- Source provenance visibility
- Coverage completeness indicators
- Contradiction and gap identification

Purpose:
Allow executives and governance roles to inspect trust before committing decisions.

## Knowledge Timeline
Timeline records material knowledge events:
- Claim creation
- Evidence additions and removals
- Approval transitions
- Confidence changes
- Dependent decision impacts

Timeline rule:
Narrative quality over event volume.

## Relationship Layer
Relationship Layer shows:
- Claim-to-evidence relationships
- Claim-to-decision dependencies
- Cross-capability impacts
- Upstream and downstream effects

Purpose:
Prevent isolated interpretation and preserve enterprise coherence.

## Enterprise Atlas Integration
Knowledge Workspace shall integrate with Enterprise Atlas as a first-class dependency surface.

Integration requirements:
- Related capabilities linked to each knowledge decision
- Upstream signals from source systems and governance inputs
- Downstream impacts on product, finance, manufacturing, customers, and mission control
- Shared enterprise context for all critical claims

## Knowledge Search
Search prompt philosophy:
"What do we need to trust before we decide?"

Search must support:
- Question-led inquiry
- Claim validation pathways
- Confidence comparison across alternatives
- Rapid transition from question to explainable recommendation

## Explainability Center
Every recommendation must answer:
- Why this recommendation exists
- How we know it is justified
- What evidence supports it
- What assumptions remain
- How confident we are

Non-conformance rule:
Recommendations without explainability are not decision-ready.

## Recommendation Model
Recommendation schema:
- Recommendation statement
- Why now
- Expected enterprise value
- Owner
- Supporting evidence set
- Confidence and assumptions
- Risk if deferred

Recommendation principle:
Move from understanding to accountable action.

## Confidence Model
Confidence model components:
- Evidence quality
- Evidence freshness
- Source reliability
- Assumption load
- Approval maturity
- Contradiction presence

Confidence bands:
- High: Decision-ready with strong traceability
- Medium: Actionable with explicit caveats
- Low: Not decision-ready without additional validation

## Source Lineage
Source lineage must expose:
- Original source
- Transformations
- Validation checkpoints
- Ownership transitions
- Current authority status

Lineage purpose:
Trust through transparency and auditability.

## Approval History
Approval history must include:
- Who approved
- What was approved
- When approval occurred
- Approval basis
- Scope and expiration conditions

Purpose:
Ensure accountability and governance continuity.

## Knowledge Lifecycle
Knowledge lifecycle states:
- Candidate
- Under Review
- Approved
- Active
- At Risk
- Deprecated
- Retired

Lifecycle objective:
Preserve governed knowledge quality through explicit state transitions.

## Knowledge Health
Knowledge Health summarizes:
- Validity posture
- Confidence distribution
- Staleness risk
- Evidence completeness
- Approval continuity

Knowledge health question:
Can this knowledge safely inform high-consequence decisions today?

## TTU Target
Time to Understanding (TTU) target for Knowledge Workspace:
- Executives should reach confident orientation and trust posture in under 90 seconds.
- For critical claims, explainability and confidence posture should be interpretable within 30 seconds of focus.

## Decision Success Metrics
Primary metrics:
- TTU
- Decision Confidence
- Decision Completion Rate
- Recommendation Acceptance
- Explainability Satisfaction
- Executive Trust

Knowledge-specific additions:
- Evidence Completeness Rate
- Lineage Completeness Rate
- Approval Continuity Rate
- Stale Knowledge Exposure Rate

## Experience Questions
The Knowledge Workspace should always answer:
- What evidence supports this?
- Who approved it?
- When was it changed?
- How confident are we?
- What business decisions depend on it?

## Experience Rationale
The Knowledge Workspace exists to reduce epistemic uncertainty in enterprise decisions.

It helps leaders understand not only what is recommended, but whether the recommendation is trustworthy enough to act on.
