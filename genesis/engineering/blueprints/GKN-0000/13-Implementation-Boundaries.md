# 13 Implementation Boundaries

Knowledge Platform SHALL NOT implement or own:

- Authentication ownership
- Authorization ownership
- Organization ownership
- Contact ownership
- Document storage or revision ownership
- Asset custody or retention ownership
- Notification engine ownership
- Workflow engine ownership
- Scheduling engine ownership
- Messaging transport ownership
- Mission Control ownership
- AI ownership or business-logic authority

Boundary control statements:

- All cross-platform behavior must be consumer-only and contract-first.
- Any proposed ownership expansion requires constitutional governance approval before engineering scope change.
