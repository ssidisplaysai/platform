# 01 Platform Overview

Approved platform summary:

- Platform: Genesis Knowledge Platform
- Approved business capability: Enterprise Knowledge Governance
- Approval basis: PDR-1001C final decision APPROVED

Constitutional ownership restatement:

- Knowledge Platform owns semantic knowledge entities, knowledge curation semantics, knowledge graph semantics, publication semantics, and knowledge lifecycle semantics.
- Knowledge Platform does not own document artifact custody, asset custody, identity ownership, authorization ownership, workflow ownership, scheduling ownership, notification ownership, messaging ownership, AI ownership, or Mission Control ownership.

Engineering objectives for GKN-1001:

- Build a bounded Knowledge Platform foundation aligned to one-capability ownership.
- Implement contract-first, consumer-only integrations.
- Implement deterministic lifecycle and audit-ready behavior.
- Implement Mission Control-compatible observability without ownership expansion.
- Prepare architecture and evidence pathways for future GKN-1001A certification.
