# 04 - Genesis Platform Principles

## Constitutional Platform Principles
1. Governance before implementation.
2. Architecture before code.
3. Everything versioned.
4. Everything certified.
5. Everything observable.
6. Everything auditable.
7. Everything replaceable.
8. Identity is universal.
9. AI is shared.
10. Applications own business logic.
11. Platform owns enterprise capabilities.
12. Zero duplicated infrastructure.
13. Backward compatibility is intentional.
14. Capabilities are additive.
15. Enterprise-first architecture.

## Operating Implications
- Every platform change requires explicit business and governance justification.
- Every capability has ownership, lifecycle, and certification path.
- Every cross-application service uses stable contracts and traceable evolution.
- Every autonomy feature must be explainable and auditable.

## Anti-Patterns Prohibited
- Application-local identity authorities.
- Duplicated policy engines across applications.
- Hidden runtime contracts without versioning.
- Non-auditable AI decision paths.
- Ad hoc cross-application integrations bypassing platform boundaries.
