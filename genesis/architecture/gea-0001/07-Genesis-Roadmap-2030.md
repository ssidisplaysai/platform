# 07 - Genesis Roadmap 2030

## Phase I - Certified Foundation
Mission: Establish constitutional governance and certified baseline services.
Major Programs: GCF, GPT, EAR, EHC, GMC, GLW, GPR-1.0.
Expected Outcomes: Certified immutable baseline and governance operating model.
Dependencies: Constitutional authority and certification packages.

## Phase II - Identity
Mission: Establish universal reusable identity authority.
Major Programs: GID-1001 through GID-1008.
Expected Outcomes: Shared authentication, authorization, session, federation architecture and implementation.
Dependencies: Phase I baseline and governance controls.

## Phase III - Runtime
Mission: Evolve orchestration, messaging, events, configuration, and reliability layers.
Major Programs: Runtime fabric, workflow and event backbone programs.
Expected Outcomes: Enterprise-grade execution substrate and cross-service coordination.
Dependencies: Identity foundation and core platform contracts.

## Phase IV - Enterprise Intelligence
Mission: Build shared AI, knowledge graph, planning, reasoning, and decision support.
Major Programs: AI platform, prompt registry, knowledge graph, semantic retrieval.
Expected Outcomes: Governed intelligence capabilities reusable across applications.
Dependencies: Runtime maturity, identity controls, auditable data sources.

## Phase V - Business Operating System
Mission: Compose shared business capabilities for operations, finance, CRM, commerce, and analytics.
Major Programs: Domain capability platform programs and cross-application workflows.
Expected Outcomes: Reduced duplication and accelerated enterprise application delivery.
Dependencies: Identity, runtime, and intelligence capabilities.

## Phase VI - Industry Solutions
Mission: Deliver industry-tailored application ecosystems on top of shared platform capabilities.
Major Programs: SSI ecosystem, STONER ecosystem, RJ Metal, and future vertical programs.
Expected Outcomes: Multi-enterprise, multi-domain business execution on one platform.
Dependencies: Shared business operating system capabilities.

## Phase VII - Genesis Cloud
Mission: Deliver managed cloud deployment and operations model for Genesis enterprise environments.
Major Programs: Cloud control plane, tenancy model, reliability and compliance services.
Expected Outcomes: Scalable, secure, enterprise-managed Genesis cloud platform.
Dependencies: Mature capabilities from Phases I through VI.
