# 06 - Genesis AI Strategy

## Strategy Objective
Establish AI as a shared enterprise platform capability, not an application-local implementation pattern.

## Shared AI Platform
Genesis AI services are platform-owned and consumed by applications through governed contracts. Core services include model orchestration, prompt lifecycle, retrieval, memory, planning, reasoning, and audit evidence.

## Prompt Registry
- Versioned prompt assets.
- Policy and approval controls.
- Usage telemetry and regression controls.

## Knowledge Graph
- Canonical enterprise semantic layer.
- Cross-application context and relationship modeling.
- Traceable source attribution and evidence lineage.

## Semantic Search and Retrieval
- Shared indexing and retrieval interfaces.
- Policy-aware query boundaries.
- Workspace and organization-scoped data access controls.

## Agents
- Platform-governed agent execution framework.
- Bounded capabilities and explicit tool permissions.
- Identity-backed and auditable autonomous actions.

## Planning and Reasoning
- Structured planning workflows with human override options.
- Explainable reasoning outputs with provenance trails.
- Deterministic policy checks prior to autonomous action.

## Automation and Workflow Intelligence
- AI augments workflow orchestration.
- Recommendations and autonomous steps are policy-governed.
- Operational decisions remain traceable to evidence.

## Natural Language Interfaces
- Unified conversational interfaces over governed platform context.
- Role-aware and policy-aware capability exposure.
- Enterprise-safe response policies and disclosure controls.

## Why AI Belongs to the Platform
- Avoids duplicated AI stacks across applications.
- Ensures uniform governance, security, and audit controls.
- Improves quality through shared learning and consistent evaluation.
- Reduces cost via reusable infrastructure and common tooling.
- Enables cross-application intelligence that isolated applications cannot provide.

## 2030 AI Outcome
Genesis provides a trusted enterprise intelligence layer where human and autonomous workflows are governed, explainable, and reusable across every business application.
