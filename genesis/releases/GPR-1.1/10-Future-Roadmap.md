# Future Roadmap

## Baseline Inheritance Rule

All future Genesis platform work shall identify GPR-1.1 as the certified platform baseline unless superseded by a future certified platform release.

## Immediate Post-Release Direction

1. Continue capability development only through governed work orders.
2. Preserve certified identity and quality gates as non-regression anchors.
3. Require certification closure for all baseline-impacting work.

## Supersession Rule

GPR-1.1 remains authoritative until replaced by a future release package with an explicit certified release decision.
