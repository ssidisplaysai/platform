# Known Limitations

## Certified Scope Limitations

1. GPR-1.2 is a publication release and introduces no new runtime behavior.
2. Multi-node messaging transport federation remains future work outside this release scope.
3. Workflow, notifications, and AI orchestration remain outside this release boundary.
4. Any capability without certified status remains excluded from the baseline.

## Limitation Posture

These limitations are intentional and consistent with release-only certification constraints.
