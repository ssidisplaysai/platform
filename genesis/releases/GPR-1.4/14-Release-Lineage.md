# 14 Release Lineage

Inheritance chain:
1. GPR-1.0 established initial certified baseline.
2. GPR-1.1 and GPR-1.2 progressed capability maturity and governance depth.
3. GPR-1.3 established pre-scheduling certified platform baseline.
4. GPR-1.4 inherits GPR-1.3 and adds fully certified Scheduling Platform.

Capability evolution:
1. Scheduling capability evolved from implementation (GWS-1001) to conditional certification (GWS-1001A), hardening (GWS-1001B), and final certification (GWS-1001C).

Certification progression:
1. Workflow remained certified through GWF-1001E.
2. Scheduling conditions from GWS-1001A were closed and upgraded to final CERTIFIED state in GWS-1001C.

Platform maturity progression:
1. GPR-1.4 marks transition to a timing-authoritative certified platform baseline with deterministic scheduling and certified boundary integration.
