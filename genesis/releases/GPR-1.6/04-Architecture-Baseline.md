# 04 Architecture Baseline

Baseline statement:
- GPR-1.6 inherits the certified platform baseline from GPR-1.5 and adds the certified Genesis AI Orchestration Platform.

Architecture posture:
- AI Orchestration remains provider-neutral.
- AI Orchestration remains application-neutral.
- AI Orchestration remains workflow-neutral.
- AI Orchestration remains scheduling-neutral.
- AI Orchestration remains messaging-neutral.
- AI Orchestration remains notification-neutral.
- Mission Control compatibility remains preserved.

Architecture summary:
- The platform baseline now includes certified AI orchestration alongside all previously certified platform families.
