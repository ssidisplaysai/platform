# Architecture Principles - Genesis Platform Baseline 1.0

## Permanent Principles
1. Platform never owns business logic.
2. Applications never own platform services.
3. Governance precedes implementation.
4. Certification precedes production baseline publication.
5. Platform services remain deterministic and auditable.
6. Applications remain independently deployable and autonomous.
7. Application discovery is metadata-driven.
8. Enterprise health is centrally evaluated.
9. Mission Control orchestrates but does not own applications.
10. Business domains remain autonomous.
11. Architectural boundaries are constitutional and enforceable.
12. Certified dependencies cannot be bypassed.
13. Launch policy must fail closed for unsafe states and targets.
14. Dependency chains must remain acyclic within certified scope.
15. Release baselines are additive and history-preserving.

## Engineering Law Interpretation
1. Any change that violates these principles requires constitutional review.
2. Any uncertified dependency in a certified release blocks promotion.
3. Any cross-boundary ownership transfer is a certification blocker.
