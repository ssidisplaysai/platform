# 04 Architecture Baseline

Baseline statement:
- GPR-1.5 inherits the certified platform baseline from GPR-1.4 and adds the certified Notification Platform as part of the baseline.

Architecture posture:
- Notification remains provider-neutral.
- Notification remains application-neutral.
- Notification remains transport-neutral.
- Notification remains scheduling-neutral.
- Notification remains workflow-neutral.
- Mission Control remains compatibility-preserving and observability-oriented.

Architecture summary:
- The platform baseline now includes a certified notification capability alongside the previously certified platform families.
