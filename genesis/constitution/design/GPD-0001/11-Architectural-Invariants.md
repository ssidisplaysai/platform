# 11 Architectural Invariants

Permanent constitutional invariants for all future platforms:

1. One owner per domain.
2. Contract-first communication.
3. Consumer-only integration.
4. Provider neutrality.
5. Storage neutrality.
6. Infrastructure neutrality.
7. AI neutrality.
8. Mission Control observational only.
9. Certification before release.
10. Explainability.
11. Auditability.
12. Institutional memory.
13. No circular ownership.
14. No duplicate enterprise capability ownership.

Invariant doctrine:

- Engineering patterns may evolve.
- Constitutional invariants remain binding.
