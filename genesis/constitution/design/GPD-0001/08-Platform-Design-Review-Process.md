# 08 Platform Design Review Process

Platform Design Review (PDR) is mandatory for every new platform.

Required PDR inputs:

- Problem statement
- Ownership model
- Boundary model
- Contracts
- Dependencies
- Architecture

PDR outputs:

- APPROVED
- APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS
- REJECTED

Process rules:

- Engineering may proceed only after APPROVED or APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS status.
- Conditions must be formally resolved within the constitutional governance process.
- REJECTED status blocks engineering initiation.

PDR authority:

- PDR decisions are constitutional governance decisions and must be recorded as institutional memory.
