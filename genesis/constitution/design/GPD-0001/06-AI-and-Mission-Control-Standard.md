# 06 AI and Mission Control Standard

AI standard:

- AI shall observe.
- AI shall reason.
- AI shall recommend.
- AI shall orchestrate.
- AI shall never own business logic.

Mission Control standard:

- Mission Control shall observe.
- Mission Control shall measure.
- Mission Control shall visualize.
- Mission Control shall never own business behavior.

Constitutional boundary:

- Execution authority remains with certified business platforms.
- Observability authority remains separate from business ownership.
