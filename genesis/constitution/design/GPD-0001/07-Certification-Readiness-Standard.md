# 07 Certification Readiness Standard

Before engineering begins, every proposed platform must demonstrate eventual capability to support:

- Independent certification
- Deterministic testing
- Auditability
- Explainability
- Versioned contracts
- Operational observability

Readiness doctrine:

- If a platform cannot be certified in principle, it cannot be engineered in practice.
- Certification readiness is a precondition of platform authorization.

Outcome expectation:

- Platform design shall include explicit pathways to future independent certification.
