# 01 Purpose

Genesis requires architectural governance before engineering because platform architecture decisions are constitutional decisions.

Constitutional premise:

- Architecture defines ownership, boundaries, and long-term enterprise truth.
- Engineering implements within those constitutional boundaries.
- Engineering follows architecture, never the reverse.

Purpose of this standard:

- Ensure platforms are conceived under constitutional discipline.
- Prevent avoidable ownership conflicts and boundary violations.
- Preserve long-term explainability, auditability, and institutional continuity.

This standard is therefore a mandatory constitutional gate for platform inception.
