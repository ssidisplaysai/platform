# 03 Boundary Standard

Every platform shall:

- Consume existing platforms through governed contracts.
- Never bypass contracts.
- Never duplicate responsibilities owned elsewhere.
- Never expand ownership without constitutional approval.

Consumer-only integration philosophy:

- A platform may consume another platform's certified contract.
- A platform may not assume ownership of external domain behavior.
- Boundary integrity is preserved through explicit consumer roles and contract obligations.

Boundary discipline is mandatory to preserve constitutional clarity and enterprise graph coherence.
