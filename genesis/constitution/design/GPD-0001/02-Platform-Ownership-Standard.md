# 02 Platform Ownership Standard

Ownership doctrine:

- Every platform must own exactly one enterprise business capability.
- Ownership must be singular.
- Ownership cannot overlap.
- Ownership cannot duplicate another platform.
- Every owned concept must have one canonical home.

Ownership implications:

- Platform identity is determined by business capability ownership, not by implementation convenience.
- Cross-platform collaboration shall occur through governed contracts, not shared ownership.
- Ownership expansion requires constitutional approval before any engineering action.
