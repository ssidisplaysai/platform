# 12 Governance Lifecycle

Required constitutional governance sequence:

Constitution

↓

Vision

↓

Platform Design Review

↓

Engineering

↓

Independent Certification

↓

Condition Resolution

↓

Final Certification

↓

Release

↓

Enterprise Adoption

↓

Mission Control Observation

Governance mandate:

- No platform may bypass this lifecycle.
- Each transition must preserve explainability, auditability, and institutional memory.
