# 09 Approval Criteria

A platform may proceed to engineering only if all criteria are satisfied:

1. Owns one capability.
2. Boundaries are clear.
3. Dependencies are valid.
4. No ownership conflicts exist.
5. Constitutional alignment is verified.
6. Engineering feasibility is demonstrated.
7. Certification readiness is established.

Approval interpretation:

- Approval confirms constitutional fitness to begin engineering.
- Approval does not waive future certification obligations.
