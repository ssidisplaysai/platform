# 04 Contract-First Standard

Contract-first obligations:

- Every external interaction must occur through certified contracts.
- Platforms shall not depend upon implementation details.
- Provider neutrality must be preserved.

Contract-first consequences:

- Architectural boundaries remain stable as implementations evolve.
- Platform interoperability remains governed and deterministic.
- Long-term substitution and evolution remain constitutionally possible.

Contract-first is a constitutional requirement, not a development preference.
