# 10 Rejection Criteria

A platform proposal shall be rejected if any condition is true:

1. Ownership overlaps existing platforms.
2. Architecture violates constitutional principles.
3. Dependencies bypass contracts.
4. Mission Control ownership expands into business behavior.
5. AI owns business logic.
6. Duplicate enterprise concepts exist.
7. Certification would be impossible.

Rejection interpretation:

- Rejection protects constitutional integrity.
- Rejected proposals may be resubmitted only after constitutional defects are corrected.
