# 12 Engineering Invariants

Permanent engineering invariants:

1. One engineering work order per capability objective.
2. One focused engineering commit per governed package phase unless governance explicitly allows otherwise.
3. Deterministic validation is mandatory.
4. Fail-closed behavior is mandatory for integrity-critical pathways.
5. Contract-first architecture is mandatory.
6. Consumer-only integration is mandatory.
7. No architectural ownership expansion during engineering.
8. No runtime data committed to version control.
9. Certification required before release.
10. Implementation follows blueprint and approved architecture.
11. Mixed-purpose engineering history is prohibited.
12. Boundary violations block certification handoff.

Invariant doctrine:

- Engineering practices may evolve.
- These invariants remain binding until constitutionally amended.
