# 04 Repository and Branching Standard

Repository and branching rules:

- Branch naming shall reflect work-order scope and capability intent.
- Branches shall be single-purpose and not multiplex unrelated work.
- Engineering commits on a branch shall remain focused to that branch mission.
- Tracked workspace must be clean before and after each governed milestone.
- Runtime data must remain excluded from version control.
- Worktree policy shall preserve deterministic evidence visibility and avoid hidden state.

Merge expectations:

- Merge eligibility requires lifecycle-gated completion and governance evidence.
- Mixed-purpose merge content is prohibited.
- Branch history must preserve architectural and certification traceability.
