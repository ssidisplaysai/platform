# 01 Engineering Philosophy

Genesis engineering is constitutionally sequenced and purpose-constrained.

Engineering follows:

- Constitution
- Architecture
- Blueprint

Never the reverse.

Philosophy principles:

- Engineering implements approved design.
- Engineering never defines ownership.
- Engineering preserves established boundaries.
- Engineering produces deterministic, auditable outcomes.
- Engineering is accountable to certification and release governance.

Engineering exists to realize approved architecture faithfully, not to redefine constitutional truth.
