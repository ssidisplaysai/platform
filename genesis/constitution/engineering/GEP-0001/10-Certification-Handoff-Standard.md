# 10 Certification Handoff Standard

Engineering must deliver the following before certification may begin:

- Complete engineering package manifest and required artifacts
- Full validation evidence with required command outcomes
- Boundary/dependency verification evidence
- Operational readiness evidence
- Architectural traceability evidence to approved design and blueprint
- Completion record with explicit status

Handoff rule:

- Certification may not compensate for missing engineering work.
- Incomplete engineering evidence blocks certification start.

Handoff quality objective:

- Certification begins from complete, testable, and auditable engineering outputs.
