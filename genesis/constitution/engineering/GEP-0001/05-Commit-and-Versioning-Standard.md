# 05 Commit and Versioning Standard

Commit discipline:

- Commits shall be single-purpose.
- Commits shall be atomic relative to the work-order objective.
- No mixed-purpose commit content is permitted.
- Commit messages shall follow scope-aligned, deterministic format.
- History discipline requires chronological governance clarity.

Versioning expectations:

- Artifact version references shall preserve lineage traceability.
- Engineering package identifiers shall remain stable and unique.

Repair procedure for contaminated history:

1. Freeze additional unrelated change introduction.
2. Identify contaminated scopes and isolate correct scope boundaries.
3. Reconstruct focused commit history preserving governance evidence.
4. Re-run required validation evidence for repaired history state.
5. Document repair rationale in package evidence.

Contaminated history may not be passed to certification without repair.
