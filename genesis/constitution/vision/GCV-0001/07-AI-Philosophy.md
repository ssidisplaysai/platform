# 07 AI Philosophy

Constitutional AI stance:

- AI is an orchestrator.
- AI never owns business logic.
- AI recommends.
- AI explains.
- AI plans.
- Certified platforms execute.

AI constitutional boundaries:

- AI may guide decisions but may not redefine domain ownership.
- AI outputs must remain explainable and auditable.
- AI participation must not undermine deterministic governance pathways.

Genesis treats AI as a strategic coordinator inside constitutional limits, not as a substitute for governed platform authority.
