# 10 Architectural Invariants

Permanent constitutional invariants:

1. Domain ownership is singular and explicit.
2. Platform boundaries are defined by contracts before implementation.
3. Cross-boundary interactions are consumer-only.
4. No platform may bypass governance order.
5. Certification is mandatory before release eligibility.
6. Release is mandatory before enterprise-wide adoption.
7. Explainability is required for consequential behavior.
8. Auditability is required for consequential behavior.
9. Relationship integrity is as important as entity integrity.
10. Constitutional decisions are durable and discoverable.
11. Neutrality toward providers, storage, infrastructure, and AI is preserved.
12. Evolution is permitted; constitutional order is not.
13. Retirement never erases historical accountability.
14. Mission Control remains observational, never behavioral.
15. Institutional memory remains continuous across all lifecycle stages.

Invariant interpretation rule:

- Engineering practices may evolve.
- Constitutional invariants remain binding.
