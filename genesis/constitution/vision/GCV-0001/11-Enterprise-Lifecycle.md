# 11 Enterprise Lifecycle

Constitutional lifecycle of a business capability:

1. Concept
2. Constitutional framing
3. Engineering
4. Certification
5. Condition resolution
6. Final certification
7. Release
8. Adoption
9. Operation
10. Improvement
11. Retirement

Lifecycle commitments:

- Each stage must preserve audit history.
- Transitions must remain explainable.
- Governance evidence must remain durable.
- Retirement must preserve institutional memory and relationship history.

Enterprise progress is therefore cumulative and accountable, not disposable.
