# 06 Constitutional Governance

Immutable governance lifecycle:

Engineering

↓

Certification

↓

Condition Resolution

↓

Final Certification

↓

Platform Release

↓

Enterprise Adoption

Constitutional governance rules:

- No platform bypasses governance.
- Governance order is mandatory and non-circumventable.
- Certification determines release eligibility.
- Conditions must be resolved before final certification.
- Adoption follows release, not the reverse.

Governance is not administrative overhead; it is the constitutional mechanism that preserves enterprise trust and system integrity.
