# 01 Why Genesis Exists

Genesis exists to become the operating system of the enterprise.

Its purpose is to unify the enterprise reality that is often fragmented across disconnected systems:

- People
- Organizations
- Knowledge
- Documents
- Assets
- Products
- Processes
- Automation
- AI
- Governance

Genesis unifies these domains into one governed enterprise graph where relationships, accountability, and institutional continuity are first-class.

Genesis therefore exists not to model isolated software concerns, but to make enterprise truth coherent, governable, explainable, and durable over time.
