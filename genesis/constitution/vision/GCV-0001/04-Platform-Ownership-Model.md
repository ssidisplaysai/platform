# 04 Platform Ownership Model

Ownership philosophy:

- Each platform owns exactly one business capability.
- Ownership is singular, explicit, and non-overlapping.
- No platform may claim duplicate ownership of another platform's core capability.

Illustrative platform ownership domains:

- Organization
- Contact
- Asset
- Document
- Knowledge
- Product
- Manufacturing
- Inventory
- Finance
- Commerce
- Analytics

Future inheritance rule:

- Every future platform shall inherit the same ownership constraints.
- New capabilities must declare constitutional ownership boundaries before engineering begins.
