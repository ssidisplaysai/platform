# 12 Long-Term Roadmap

Roadmap purpose:

- Provide directional guidance for enterprise capability evolution.
- Preserve constitutional continuity while allowing strategic adaptation.

Directional capability roadmap:

- Knowledge
- Product
- CRM
- Manufacturing
- Inventory
- Finance
- Commerce
- Analytics
- Additional future domains as enterprise realities evolve

Roadmap doctrine:

- The roadmap is directional rather than prescriptive.
- Capability sequencing may adapt.
- Constitutional invariants do not.
