# 08 Mission Control Philosophy

Mission Control constitutional role:

- Mission Control observes.
- Mission Control visualizes.
- Mission Control measures.
- Mission Control never owns business behavior.

Mission Control exists to strengthen enterprise awareness while preserving platform ownership boundaries.

Constitutional implications:

- Observation must not mutate governed domain behavior.
- Measurement must remain attributable and auditable.
- Visibility serves governance; it does not replace governance.
