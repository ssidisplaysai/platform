# 05 The Enterprise Graph

Genesis defines the enterprise as one governed graph.

Constitutional graph model:

- Every platform is a node.
- Relationships are first-class.
- Nothing owns everything.
- Everything is connected through governed contracts.

Graph consequences:

- Enterprise meaning is formed through connected relationships, not isolated records.
- Cross-platform interactions are lawful only through explicit consumer contracts.
- The graph remains coherent because ownership and contracts are constitutionally enforced.

The enterprise graph is therefore the canonical representation of enterprise reality over time.
