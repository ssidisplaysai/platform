# 02 Enterprise Expansion Transition

Transition declaration:

- Enterprise Expansion Era is established as the primary Genesis operating mode.

Operating-mode shift:

1. Foundation construction transitions to foundation reuse.
2. Constitutional documentation growth transitions to enterprise capability execution.
3. Governance effort transitions from framework creation to framework application quality.

Strategic operating posture:

1. Future engineering shall inherit this foundation.
2. Future engineering shall validate this foundation through implementation outcomes.
3. Future engineering shall improve enterprise capability, consistency, and scale.
4. Future engineering shall not routinely expand constitutional governance.

Scope guardrail:

- This transition changes strategic operating philosophy only.
- No authority revocation and no lifecycle rewrites are introduced by this decision.
