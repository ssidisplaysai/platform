# 05 Future Constitutional Evolution

Future constitutional evolution policy:

1. Constitutional evolution remains constitutionally permitted.
2. Constitutional evolution is exceptional rather than routine.
3. Proposed evolution must include explicit architectural-deficiency evidence.

Required justification package for constitutional amendments:

1. Deficiency statement with affected authority scope.
2. Demonstration that interpretation and clarification are insufficient.
3. Demonstration that existing standards cannot reasonably resolve the issue.
4. Demonstration that implementation guidance or reusable patterns cannot resolve the issue.
5. Impact analysis across governance, engineering, certification, and release lineage.
6. Proposed amendment with supersession and historical-preservation mapping.

Approval path:

- Governance Architecture Board constitutional decision approval is mandatory.

Continuity requirements:

1. Existing authority continuity must be preserved unless explicitly superseded.
2. Derivative artifacts must be updated to reflect approved constitutional changes.
3. Historical constitutional records remain immutable and permanently addressable.
