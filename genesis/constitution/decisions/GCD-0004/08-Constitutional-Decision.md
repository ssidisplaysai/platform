# 08 Constitutional Decision

Decision:

- GCD-0004 is adopted.
- Genesis Foundation Era is formally concluded.
- Enterprise Expansion Era is established as the primary Genesis operating mode.

Constitutional holdings:

1. Constitutional, governance, architectural, engineering, certification, release, and audit foundations are sufficient for enterprise-scale platform engineering.
2. Constitutional baseline freeze is established as a stability policy.
3. Constitutional evolution remains permitted but requires exceptional justification.
4. Enterprise capability implementation becomes the primary strategic focus.

Non-revocation and non-modification holdings:

1. No constitutional authority is revoked.
2. No governance lifecycle is changed.
3. No engineering lifecycle is changed.
4. No certification lifecycle or policy is changed.
5. No release lifecycle or policy is changed.

Scope holding:

- This decision changes strategic operating philosophy only.
- No runtime implementation is authorized by this decision.
- No platform engineering execution is authorized by this decision.

Alignment verification:

- Aligned with GCV-0001, GPD-0001, GEP-0001, GSR-0001, and GCD-0003.
