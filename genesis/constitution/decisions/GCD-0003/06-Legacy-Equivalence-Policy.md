# 06 Legacy Equivalence Policy

Policy objective:

- Preserve constitutional continuity without retroactively invalidating earlier certified platforms created before full framework finalization.

Legacy treatment rules:

1. Pre-framework or partial-framework platforms may satisfy governance intent through equivalent evidence.
2. Equivalent evidence must show bounded ownership, contract-first integration, dependency clarity, certification lineage, and release anchoring.
3. Legacy packages are not required to mimic later artifact naming exactly if equivalent control evidence exists.
4. Future modifications to legacy capabilities must use current governance standards unless constitutional exemption is explicitly approved.

Non-retroactive validity rule:

- Previously certified and released capabilities remain valid when historical evidence is equivalent to the required governance control intent at time of approval.
