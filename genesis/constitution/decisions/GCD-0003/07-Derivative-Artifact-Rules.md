# 07 Derivative Artifact Rules

Derivative artifact classification:

- Closeout packages, audits, remediation packages, and summary documents are derivative governance artifacts.

Binding rules:

1. Derivative artifacts must reference constitutional authorities when stating lifecycle sequence.
2. Derivative artifacts may summarize but may not redefine or reorder canonical lifecycle authority.
3. If simplified lifecycle shorthand is used, scope limitations must be explicitly stated.
4. Historical audit findings remain immutable records; post-remediation status must be appended as a resolution reference, not rewritten.

Future lifecycle statement requirement:

- New lifecycle statements must reference GCD-0003 for authority-versus-execution interpretation and must remain consistent with GPD-0001 and GEP-0001 scope boundaries.
