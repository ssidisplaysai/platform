# 03 Authority Hierarchy

Authoritative hierarchy for governance lifecycle interpretation:

1. Genesis Constitution and first principles
- Highest authority.
- Defines permanent doctrine and interpretive constraints.

2. Constitutional vision standard (GCV-0001)
- Defines constitutional intent and enterprise governance philosophy.
- Cannot contradict constitutional doctrine.

3. Constitutional design standard (GPD-0001)
- Defines platform design governance standards.
- Governs design-stage requirements and lifecycle framing.

4. Constitutional engineering standard (GEP-0001)
- Governs engineering-stage lifecycle, evidence, and handoff controls.
- Scope begins at blueprint-to-release execution pathway.

5. Constitutional decisions (including GCD-0003)
- Resolve ambiguity and establish binding interpretation without weakening higher authority.

6. Governance closeouts, audits, and remediation packages
- Derivative records and summaries.
- May interpret, report, and certify adherence.
- May not redefine constitutional authority.

Interpretive rule:

- If a derivative lifecycle statement conflicts with constitutional standards, constitutional standards control.
