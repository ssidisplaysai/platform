# 04 Canonical Execution Lifecycle

Canonical platform execution lifecycle gates:

1. Constitutional framing
- Platform intent is bounded by constitutional doctrine and vision.

2. Platform design review
- Initial architecture and ownership/boundary posture is independently assessed.

3. Design condition resolution, where required
- Mandatory when design review issues conditions.

4. Final design approval
- Required before blueprinting and implementation planning.

5. Engineering blueprint
- Architecture is translated into implementation-neutral execution guidance.

6. Engineering implementation under GEP-0001
- Runtime engineering occurs only from approved blueprint lineage.

7. Independent certification
- Engineering output is independently assessed.

8. Engineering hardening and certification condition closure, where required
- Mandatory when certification returns conditions.

9. Final certification
- Certification closure decision is issued.

10. Platform release
- Release package and certificate are issued from certified anchors only.

11. Enterprise adoption
- Certified capability is adopted into operating baseline.

12. Mission Control observation
- Post-adoption observation, measurement, and visualization without ownership transfer.

Clarification:

- Platform Design Standard and Engineering Process Standard are governance authorities applied across gates, not standalone chronological runtime-implementation activities.
