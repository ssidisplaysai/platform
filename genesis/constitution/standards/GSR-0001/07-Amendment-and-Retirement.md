# 07 Amendment and Retirement

Amendment policy:

1. Amendment trigger conditions
- Constitutional conflict correction.
- Subject scope expansion.
- Governance lifecycle clarification.
- Numbering or registry policy gap.

2. Amendment request authority
- Domain Standard Owner.
- Constitutional Custodian.
- Governance Audit Authority via formal finding.

3. Amendment workflow
- Submit amendment proposal with rationale and impacted standards.
- Perform conflict and overlap assessment.
- Conduct constitutional alignment review against Constitution, GCV, GPD, GEP, and relevant GCD decisions.
- Obtain Governance Architecture Board approval.
- Publish successor or revised standard with full traceability.

4. Amendment constraints
- No amendment may produce contradictory active authorities.
- No amendment may erase prior historical decisions or records.

Retirement policy:

1. Retirement eligibility
- Subject no longer operationally relevant.
- Subject fully absorbed by a successor authority.
- Constitutional deprecation decision approved.

2. Retirement workflow
- Publish retirement rationale.
- Verify successor mapping or deprecation disposition.
- Approve retirement via Governance Architecture Board.
- Transition state to Retired and then Historical.

3. Retirement constraints
- Retired identifiers are never reused.
- Retired packages remain permanently discoverable.
- Audit references to retired standards remain valid.

Recordkeeping requirements:

1. Amendment decision record.
2. Retirement decision record.
3. Supersession map update.
4. Registry index update and timestamp.
