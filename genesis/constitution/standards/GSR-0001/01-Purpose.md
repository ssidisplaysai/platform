# 01 Purpose

The Genesis Standards Registry defines the constitutional operating contract for standards governance.

Purpose statements:

1. Maintain one authoritative catalog of standards families and authority relationships.
2. Define the required identity, approval, lifecycle, and historical-preservation model for every standard.
3. Prevent governance drift by requiring one authoritative standard per subject domain.
4. Preserve constitutional continuity through explicit supersession and retirement rules.
5. Keep all standards permanently addressable and auditable.

Non-purpose statements:

- This registry does not define runtime behavior.
- This registry does not authorize implementation.
- This registry does not replace family-specific standards content.

Governance outcome:

- All present and future Genesis standards must register under this constitutional registry model.
