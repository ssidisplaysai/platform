# 04 Identifier and Numbering Policy

Policy goals:

1. Permanent addressability.
2. Unique identifier per standard artifact.
3. Lineage-preserving successors.
4. No cross-family collision.

Numbering conventions:

1. GCV
- Format: GCV-0001 and above.
- Use: Constitutional vision baselines.
- Reserve: GCV-9000 to GCV-9999 for constitutional emergency or continuity baselines.

2. GCD
- Format: GCD-0001 and above.
- Use: Constitutional decisions.
- Reserve: GCD-8000 to GCD-8999 for conflict-arbitration decisions.

3. GPD
- Format: GPD-0001 and above.
- Use: Platform design standards.
- Reserve: GPD-9000 to GPD-9999 for foundational redesign standards.

4. GEP
- Format: GEP-0001 and above.
- Use: Engineering process standards.
- Reserve: GEP-9000 to GEP-9999 for enterprise engineering model transitions.

5. PDR
- Format: PDR-1001 with lineage letters where needed, for example PDR-1001A.
- Use: Design review lineage.
- Reserve: PDR-9000 to PDR-9999 for enterprise-wide cross-platform reviews.

6. Blueprints
- Format: Gxx-0000 where xx is the platform code, for example GKN-0000.
- Use: Pre-implementation blueprint baselines.
- Reserve: 0000 fixed as blueprint baseline marker within each platform code family.

7. Engineering packages
- Format: Gxx-1001 baseline, successor letters optional, for example GWF-1001B.
- Use: Platform engineering governance packages.
- Reserve: 1000 to 1999 for primary platform engineering baselines.

8. Certification packages
- Format: Gxx-1001A style lineage tied to engineering baseline.
- Use: Certification states and closures.
- Reserve: Suffix letters A through Z for certification progression states.

9. Releases
- Format: GPR-Major.Minor, for example GPR-2.0.
- Use: Enterprise release governance baselines.
- Reserve: Major versions 90 to 99 reserved for extraordinary constitutional release transitions.

10. Audits
- Format: GSA-0001 baseline with optional lettered dispositions, for example GSA-0001B.
- Use: Governance audits and follow-on dispositions.
- Reserve: GSA-9000 to GSA-9999 for enterprise emergency audit actions.

11. Remediation
- Format: GSA-R001 baseline with optional verification suffix, for example GSA-R001A.
- Use: Governance remediation and independent verification chains.
- Reserve: GSA-R900 to GSA-R999 for emergency constitutional remediation.

12. Future constitutional programs
- Format: GCP-0001 and above, or family-specific reserved prefix approved by registry amendment.
- Use: New constitutional program families not yet established.
- Reserve: GCP-8000 to GCP-8999 for constitutional pilot standards; GCP-9000 to GCP-9999 for constitutional continuity standards.

Collision prevention rules:

1. One identifier may map to only one canonical artifact.
2. Retired or superseded identifiers are never reused.
3. New family prefix creation requires registry amendment approval.
4. Derivative documents may not claim authoritative identifier namespaces.
