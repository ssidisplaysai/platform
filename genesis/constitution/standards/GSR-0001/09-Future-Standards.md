# 09 Future Standards

Purpose:

- Govern admission of future constitutional standard families while preserving non-conflict and single-authority constraints.

Future family onboarding requirements:

1. Family charter
- Define purpose, normative subject boundary, and owner.

2. Authority mapping
- Show relationship to Constitution, GCV, GPD, GEP, and applicable GCD decisions.

3. Identifier namespace reservation
- Allocate unique prefix and numbering range through registry amendment approval.

4. Lifecycle and version policy
- Declare lifecycle states, transition approvals, and supersession model.

5. Evidence and approval model
- Define mandatory artifacts and approval authority.

6. Conflict pre-check
- Demonstrate no overlap conflict with active standards.

Reserved future namespaces:

1. GCP family
- Constitutional program standards and frameworks.

2. GSR family expansions
- Additional registry catalog standards.

3. Additional prefixes
- Allowed only through approved registry amendment with collision scan.

Future constitutional standards rule:

- No new family becomes authoritative until registered in the active GSR baseline.
