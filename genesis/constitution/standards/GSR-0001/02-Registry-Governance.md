# 02 Registry Governance

Constitutional authority model:

1. Genesis Constitution
2. GCV-0001 Constitutional Vision
3. GPD-0001 Platform Design Standard
4. GEP-0001 Engineering Process Standard
5. Constitutional decisions, including GCD-0003
6. GSR-0001 Registry as constitutional catalog policy
7. Derivative governance catalogs and summaries

Registry owner:

- Governance Architecture Board

Stewarding roles:

1. Constitutional Custodian
- Maintains authority hierarchy integrity.

2. Registry Maintainer
- Maintains family records, numbering reserves, and lifecycle state records.

3. Domain Standard Owners
- Own family-specific content quality and amendment evidence.

Registry governance controls:

1. Single authority per subject
- Each normative subject has one active authoritative standard at a time.

2. Reference integrity
- Derivative documents may reference standards but may not redefine authority.

3. Auditable traceability
- Every standard entry must record owner, status, version lineage, and supersession links.

4. Constitutional conflict handling
- Any conflict claim triggers constitutional review under GCD authority process.

Approval authority:

- Governance Architecture Board approval is required for:
1. New family registration.
2. Registry policy amendment.
3. Supersession activation.
4. Retirement activation.
