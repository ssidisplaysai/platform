# 08 Constitutional Rules

Constitutional registry rules:

1. Standards never conflict.
- Active authoritative standards must remain non-contradictory.
- Conflicts trigger constitutional decision review.

2. One authoritative standard per subject.
- Only one active standard may govern a normative subject at a time.

3. Derivative documents reference standards.
- Derivative and summary artifacts may cite standards but may not redefine authority.

4. Standards supersede prior standards.
- Authority transition requires explicit supersession metadata.

5. Historical standards remain preserved.
- Superseded and retired standards remain immutable and retrievable.

6. Standards remain permanently addressable.
- Identifiers are durable and non-reusable.

7. Standards remain auditable.
- Every lifecycle transition requires evidence and recorded approval.

8. Authority and execution are distinct.
- Standards define authority constraints; lifecycle artifacts define chronological execution gates.

9. Registry is constitutional table of contents.
- The registry catalogs authority; it does not replace family standards.

10. No implementation leakage.
- Registry standards governance artifacts may not contain runtime implementation directives.
