# Compiler Terminology

## Normative Requirements
- Terms defined in this document SHALL be used consistently across GCS-0001.
- Where a term conflict exists, the definition in this document SHALL prevail for GCS-0001.

## Core Terms
- Compiler: A transformation system conforming to GCS-0001.
- Canonical Entity: A governed identity-resolved business entity.
- Canonical Relationship: A governed relationship-resolved entity connection.
- Validated Fact: A fact derived from governed evidence and valid under rule contracts.
- IBR: Intermediate Business Representation used for deterministic pass interaction.
- Compiler Manifest: Normative record of compiler execution inputs, versions, and outputs.
- Replay Manifest: Normative record sufficient to replay outputs deterministically.
- Certification Record: Normative certification decision and attestations for a compiler run.
- Business Genome Snapshot: Complete immutable canonical state output.
- Business Genome Delta: Immutable governed change set between snapshots.
