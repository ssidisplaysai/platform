# Glossary

## Normative Requirement
Glossary entries SHALL align with Compiler-Terminology.md.

## Terms
- Conforming Compiler: An implementation that satisfies all mandatory GCS-0001 requirements.
- Determinism: Required property that identical normative inputs yield identical normative outputs.
- Replay: Re-execution that reproduces normative outputs under declared replay inputs.
- Certification Record: Immutable record of certification decision and evidence references.
- Manifest: Immutable metadata artifact describing inputs, outputs, versions, and lineage.
- Snapshot: Full canonical Business Genome state.
- Delta: Canonical change set between two snapshots.
- Provenance: Traceability linking outputs to source evidence and governed decisions.
