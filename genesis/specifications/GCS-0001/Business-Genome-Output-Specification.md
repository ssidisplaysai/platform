# Business Genome Output Specification

## Normative Output Set
A conforming compiler MUST produce, at minimum:
- Business Genome Snapshot
- Business Genome Delta
- Canonical Entity Graph
- Canonical Relationship Graph
- Knowledge Graph
- Evidence Trace Graph
- Identity Ledger
- Relationship Ledger
- Rule Ledger
- Assembly Manifest
- Publication Manifest
- Certification Record

## Normative Output Guarantees
- Output identifiers MUST be deterministic.
- Output provenance MUST be complete.
- Output references MUST be internally consistent.
- Output artifacts MUST be immutable once published.

## Informative Guidance
Output storage or serialization format is implementation-defined and out of scope.
