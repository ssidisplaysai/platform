# GX-003 Executive Decision Validation Plan v1.0

Project: Genesis Enterprise Operating System  
Studio: Genesis Experience Studio

Version: v1.0

## Mission
Validate whether Genesis helps executives understand enterprise state and make confident decisions.

This is not usability testing.

This is not UI testing.

This is not engineering validation.

This is executive decision validation.

## Objective
Measure whether Genesis reduces executive uncertainty.

Primary validation question:
Can executives understand their enterprise and make confident decisions?

## Genesis Experience KPI
### Time to Understanding (TTU)
Definition:
Elapsed time from opening Genesis until an executive can confidently answer all of the following:
- Where am I?
- Is the enterprise healthy?
- What changed?
- What requires my attention?
- What should I do next?

TTU is recorded in every scenario.

## Scope Boundaries
In scope:
- Executive interpretation quality
- Decision confidence
- Explanation trust
- Action clarity
- TTU measurement and cross-scenario comparison

Out of scope:
- Interface usability mechanics
- Pixel-level design critique
- Engineering implementation review
- Backend, API, performance, and architecture testing

## Validation Design
Session flow:
1. Brief participant on purpose and confidentiality.
2. Start each scenario with clean context.
3. Record TTU start and stop timestamps.
4. Capture scenario outcomes and confidence.
5. Ask standard post-scenario questionnaire.
6. Log observations and improvement opportunities.

Evidence sources:
- Observer checklist
- Participant questionnaire
- TTU measurement sheet
- Findings template
- Improvement log

## Roles
Moderator:
- Reads scenario prompts consistently.
- Ensures neutrality and no leading guidance.

Observer:
- Records TTU timestamps, behavior evidence, and quotes.
- Completes observer checklist and findings template.

Participant (Executive):
- Thinks aloud when comfortable.
- Makes decisions using Genesis prototype content.

## TTU Protocol
TTU Start:
- Timestamp the moment the participant first sees the active scenario in Genesis.

TTU Stop:
- Timestamp when the participant clearly and confidently answers all five TTU criteria.

Confidence confirmation prompt:
"Are you confident in your understanding and recommended next decision?"

If confidence is not present, TTU continues.

## Success Criteria
Genesis succeeds when executives consistently report:
- I understood my business quickly.
- I trusted the recommendations.
- I knew what decision mattered.
- I would use this every morning.
