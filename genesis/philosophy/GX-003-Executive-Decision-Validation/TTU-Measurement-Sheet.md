# TTU Measurement Sheet

Program: GX-003 Executive Decision Validation  
KPI: Time to Understanding (TTU)  
Version: v1.0

TTU Definition:
Elapsed time from opening Genesis until the participant can confidently answer:
- Where am I?
- Is the enterprise healthy?
- What changed?
- What requires my attention?
- What should I do next?

## Interpretation Guidance
Use TTU with qualitative findings.

Lower TTU is meaningful only when explanation trust and decision confidence are high.

A low TTU with low confidence is not success.

A moderate TTU with high confidence can still indicate strong executive value.
