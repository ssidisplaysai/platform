# Scenario Scripts

Program: GX-003 Executive Decision Validation  
Version: v1.0

## Scenario 001: Morning Executive Brief
Goal:
Measure first-impression clarity.

Moderator script:
1. Open Genesis Executive Workspace prototype.
2. Say: "You are beginning your morning executive brief. Use Genesis to understand current enterprise posture."
3. Ask participant to answer:
- Is the enterprise healthy?
- What are the top three priorities?
- Which recommendation should be reviewed first?

Expected output evidence:
- Health summary with rationale
- Ranked top three priorities
- First recommendation selection with reason

## Scenario 002: Revenue Decline
Goal:
Determine whether Genesis clearly explains declining revenue.

Moderator script:
1. Set context: "Revenue has declined over the last reporting cycle."
2. Ask participant to identify:
- Root cause
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence level
- Recommended action

Expected output evidence:
- Coherent cause hypothesis
- Evidence references from Genesis context
- Explicit confidence statement
- Action recommendation with owner

## Scenario 003: Manufacturing Delay
Goal:
Identify supplier, product impact, customer impact, financial impact, and recommended action.

Moderator script:
1. Set context: "A manufacturing delay is affecting current commitments."
2. Ask participant to identify:
- Supplier involved
- Affected products
- Customer impact
- Financial impact
- Recommended action

Expected output evidence:
- Specific impacted entities
- Relationship explanation across operations and customers
- Financial consequence framing
- Clear response plan

## Scenario 004: Inventory Risk
Goal:
Determine constrained inventory, cause, business impact, and recommended response.

Moderator script:
1. Set context: "Inventory risk is rising in a critical area."
2. Ask participant to determine:
- What inventory is constrained
- Why the constraint exists
- Business impact
- Recommended response

Expected output evidence:
- Identified constrained area
- Causal explanation
- Impact statement tied to outcomes
- Prioritized response

## Scenario 005: Executive Inquiry
Goal:
Evaluate whether Genesis naturally guides understanding from executive search inquiry.

Moderator script:
1. Ask participant to type exactly:
"Why is margin decreasing?"
2. Ask participant to narrate how Genesis guides understanding.

Expected output evidence:
- Interpretation path from inquiry to understanding
- Explanation trust statement
- Next decision recommendation

## Post-Scenario Standard Questions
Ask after every scenario:
1. Did Genesis help you understand the problem?
2. Did you trust the explanation?
3. Did you understand why the recommendation was made?
4. Did you know what to do next?
5. Did anything create confusion?

## Moderator Consistency Rules
- Keep wording stable across participants.
- Avoid hints, coaching, or interpretation assistance.
- Record participant language exactly when possible.
- Preserve neutral tone and pacing.
