# 01 Introduction

## Why this playbook exists

GPG-0001 captures the delivery operating model proven through the first Enterprise Expansion platform delivery (GKN-1001). The goal is repeatability: future teams should be able to deliver new platforms with consistent quality, independent evidence, and controlled scope.

## What this playbook is

- A practical, stage-based operating guide.
- A checklist for entry criteria, exit criteria, and required evidence.
- A method for separating platform defects from shared-foundation defects.

## What this playbook is not

- Not constitutional text.
- Not governance policy.
- Not an engineering specification.
- Not a certification decision artifact.

## Operating principles

1. Governance defines authority; engineering executes within that authority.
2. Every stage has explicit artifacts and independent evidence.
3. Condition handling is explicit, traceable, and isolated by ownership.
4. Validation and certification must remain independent from implementation.
5. Release follows final certification, never precedes it.
