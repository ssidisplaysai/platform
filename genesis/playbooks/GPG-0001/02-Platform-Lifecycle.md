# 02 Platform Lifecycle

## Proven lifecycle sequence

Platform Design Review

Condition Resolution

Final Design Approval

Engineering Blueprint

Engineering

Engineering Validation

Independent Certification

Foundation Maintenance (conditional)

Platform Condition Closure (conditional)

Final Certification

Publication Verification

Release

Enterprise Adoption

Mission Control Observation

## Stage classification

Mandatory stages:

1. Platform Design Review
2. Final Design Approval
3. Engineering Blueprint
4. Engineering
5. Engineering Validation
6. Independent Certification
7. Final Certification
8. Publication Verification
9. Release
10. Enterprise Adoption
11. Mission Control Observation

Conditional stages:

1. Condition Resolution
- Required only when design review issues block approval.

2. Foundation Maintenance
- Required only when independent validation/certification identifies shared baseline defects outside platform ownership.

3. Platform Condition Closure
- Required only when certification conditions remain open for platform-owned behavior.

## Stage controls

For every stage, teams must define and execute:

1. Entry criteria
2. Exit criteria
3. Expected artifacts
4. Typical evidence
5. Success criteria

## Control intent

This lifecycle enforces sequence discipline. Later stages cannot compensate for skipped earlier controls.
