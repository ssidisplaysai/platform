# 08 Publication and Release

## Purpose

Define the controlled transition from final certification to public availability and operational deployment.

## Publication Verification entry criteria

1. Final certification decision issued.
2. All certification conditions closed.
3. Publication artifacts prepared and validated.

## Publication Verification expected artifacts

1. Publication readiness checklist.
2. Immutable references to final certified commits and packages.
3. Approval record for publication.

## Release entry criteria

1. Publication verification complete.
2. Release scope, rollout, and rollback plans approved.
3. Operational owners assigned.

## Release expected artifacts

1. Release notes.
2. Version/tag records.
3. Deployment verification evidence.

## Enterprise Adoption entry criteria

1. Release completed successfully.
2. User enablement and runbooks available.
3. Support and incident paths activated.

## Mission Control Observation entry criteria

1. Production telemetry and authorization posture active.
2. Health and metrics endpoints available.
3. Observation policy and escalation thresholds defined.

## Success criteria

1. No release before final certification.
2. Traceable lineage from certification to release.
3. Operational stability monitored from day one.
