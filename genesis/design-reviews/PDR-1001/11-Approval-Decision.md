# 11 Approval Decision

Decision: APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

Decision rationale:

- The proposed Knowledge Platform can satisfy the one-capability ownership rule when constrained to Enterprise Knowledge Governance.
- Constitutional principles of contract-first architecture, consumer-only integration, and governance-first progression are satisfiable.
- No mandatory rejection condition is intrinsic to the proposal if explicit boundary controls are established before engineering.

Mandatory blocking conditions:

1. Publish a canonical ownership matrix for all proposed Knowledge concepts, including explicit non-ownership declarations for Document and Asset custody concerns.
2. Publish a formal boundary charter that defines Knowledge vs Document semantic boundaries, including policy/procedure/standard treatment rules.
3. Publish required, optional, and forbidden dependency sets with dependency direction and anti-circularity guarantees.
4. Publish conceptual external contract catalog with versioning intent and consumer-only integration declarations.
5. Publish AI and Mission Control boundary statement confirming non-ownership of business logic and behavior.

Engineering authorization rule:

- Engineering SHALL NOT begin until all mandatory blocking conditions are resolved and constitutionally accepted.
