# 03 Ownership Analysis

Knowledge Platform ownership (proposed):

- Knowledge Articles
- Policies as knowledge entities
- Procedures as knowledge entities
- Standards as knowledge entities
- Playbooks
- FAQs
- Decision Records as knowledge entities
- Training Material as knowledge entities
- Semantic Relationships
- Knowledge Categories
- Knowledge Tags
- Knowledge Publication state
- Knowledge Lifecycle
- Knowledge Graph

Knowledge Platform does not own:

- Document artifact custody, file persistence, or document revision authority (Document Platform)
- Asset custody, asset integrity custody, or retention custody (Asset Platform)
- Organization identity or organization hierarchy ownership (Organization Platform)
- Contact identity or contact profile ownership (Contact Platform)
- AI decision authority or business logic ownership (AI Orchestration Platform)
- Observability ownership of business behavior (Mission Control)
- Workflow/scheduling/notification/message ownership (respective platforms)

Constitutional ownership finding:

- Proposed ownership is constitutionally valid if policy/procedure/standard entries are treated as knowledge-domain semantic entities and not as duplicate document ownership.
