# 07 AI and Mission Control Analysis

AI posture verification:

- AI remains consumer-only.
- AI may reason, recommend, summarize, and orchestrate.
- AI does not own knowledge-domain business logic.

Mission Control posture verification:

- Mission Control remains observational.
- Mission Control may measure and visualize platform health and metrics.
- Mission Control does not own knowledge-domain behavior.

Constitutional finding:

- Proposed architecture is compliant when AI and Mission Control remain bounded to non-ownership roles.
