# 06 Contract Analysis

Conceptual external contracts required:

- Identity and access contract
- Organization reference contract
- Contact reference contract
- Document reference and publication-link contract
- Workflow progression contract
- Notification dispatch contract
- Messaging event contract
- Scheduling request contract
- Asset reference contract
- AI recommendation/orchestration contract
- Mission Control observability contract

Contract-first findings:

- Proposed architecture is compatible with contract-first governance when all external interactions are defined as versioned contracts.
- Implementation-specific coupling is avoidable and constitutionally disallowed.

Contract governance requirements:

- External interactions must remain certified contract surfaces.
- Knowledge platform behavior must not rely on non-contract internal details of any platform.
