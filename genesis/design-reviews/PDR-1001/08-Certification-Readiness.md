# 08 Certification Readiness

Readiness evaluation:

- Independent certification: achievable through explicit ownership, boundary, and contract declarations.
- Deterministic testing: achievable when lifecycle and approval transitions are defined as deterministic state transitions.
- Explainability: achievable when knowledge lifecycle decisions require attributable rationale.
- Auditability: achievable when publication, approval, and lifecycle transitions are recorded as immutable audit events.
- Operational observability: achievable via Mission Control-compatible health and metrics contracts.
- Versioned contracts: achievable by requiring versioned external contract surfaces.
- Platform neutrality: achievable by preserving provider/storage/infrastructure/AI neutrality.

Readiness determination:

- Certification readiness is established in principle, subject to conditions that formalize boundaries and forbidden dependencies before engineering starts.
