# 02 Business Capability Definition

Proposed single business capability:

- Enterprise Knowledge Governance

Capability determination:

- The proposed Knowledge Platform represents one constitutional business capability when defined as governance of enterprise knowledge semantics, publication state, and lifecycle.

Detected multi-capability risk:

- Risk emerges if scope expands into document authoring, CRM ownership, or AI decision authority.

Restructuring guidance if scope drifts:

- Keep knowledge semantics, curation, and knowledge graph ownership in Knowledge Platform.
- Keep document artifact ownership in Document Platform.
- Keep customer-domain ownership in future CRM platform.
- Keep orchestration-only authority in AI platform.

Board conclusion:

- Single-capability criterion is satisfiable, subject to strict boundary conditions.
