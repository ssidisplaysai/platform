# 01 Problem Statement

Enterprise problem:

- Critical enterprise knowledge is often fragmented, implicit, and inconsistently governed across teams and tools.
- The enterprise lacks a canonical platform for governed knowledge semantics, publication state, decision lineage, and cross-domain discoverability.

Knowledge distinction boundaries:

- Knowledge is not Documents:
  - Document Platform governs document artifacts and document lifecycle.
  - Knowledge Platform governs semantic meaning, curation, and knowledge-level relationships that may reference documents.
- Knowledge is not Assets:
  - Asset Platform governs asset identity, storage references, and retention posture.
  - Knowledge Platform governs interpretive and operational meaning, not asset custody.
- Knowledge is not CRM:
  - CRM capabilities govern customer-facing relationship and commercial interaction domains.
  - Knowledge Platform governs institutional knowledge applicable across enterprise domains.
- Knowledge is not AI:
  - AI orchestrates, reasons, and recommends.
  - Knowledge Platform owns governed knowledge structures and lifecycle; AI remains a consumer.

Constitutional objective:

- Introduce a singularly owned Knowledge capability that strengthens enterprise memory, explainability, and governed decision continuity.
