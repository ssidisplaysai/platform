# 08 Residual Risk Assessment

Residual architectural risk profile:

1. Knowledge-to-Document semantic drift
- Classification: Medium
- Risk: future proposals may blur semantic ownership and document artifact ownership.
- Recommendation: enforce boundary charter review during final approval and future design updates.

2. Dependency scope inflation
- Classification: Medium
- Risk: optional dependencies may become implicit requirements over time.
- Recommendation: require periodic dependency posture reviews in design governance.

3. Contract taxonomy inconsistency across domains
- Classification: Low
- Risk: conceptual contracts may diverge in naming or lifecycle expectations.
- Recommendation: establish cross-platform contract taxonomy governance in future constitutional updates.

4. AI recommendation authority creep
- Classification: Medium
- Risk: AI orchestration requests may gradually imply business decision ownership.
- Recommendation: require explicit non-ownership checks in all AI-related design changes.

5. Mission Control behavioral creep
- Classification: Low
- Risk: observability surfaces could be asked to mutate behavior.
- Recommendation: preserve observational-only constitutional invariant enforcement.

Residual risk conclusion:

- No High residual risks identified.
- Remaining risks are governable through ongoing constitutional review controls.
