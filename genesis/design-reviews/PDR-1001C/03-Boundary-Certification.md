# 03 Boundary Certification

Boundary verification scope:

- Knowledge vs Document boundary charter from PDR-1001B reviewed for semantic and artifact ownership separation.

Certification findings:

- Knowledge semantic ownership is explicitly distinct from Document artifact ownership.
- Shared interactions are contract-defined reference relationships.
- Consumer-only relationship is explicit and non-bypass.
- Non-duplication rules are explicit and enforceable.

Boundary certification decision:

- Boundary architecture is constitutionally certified for final design approval.
