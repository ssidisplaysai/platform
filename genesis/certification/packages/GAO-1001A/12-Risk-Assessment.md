# 12 Risk Assessment

Top risks:
1. Policy enforcement risk: timeout/cancellation declared but not enforced.
2. Budget control risk: token/cost policies declared but not used for hard execution gating.
3. Authorization boundary risk: execution trust boundary accepts caller-provided permission lists.

Risk severity:
- C1: High
- C2: High
- C3: Medium

Risk conclusion:
- Risks are manageable under explicit conditions but do not support unconditional certification at this stage.
