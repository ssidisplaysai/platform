# Genesis Platform Change Justification Standard

Standard ID: GPCS-0001
Status: ACTIVE
Work Order: GPT-0001
Date: 2026-07-30

## Purpose
Require rigorous justification for any post-baseline platform change proposal.

## Mandatory Proposal Content
Every future platform change proposal must document:
- Business problem.
- Affected applications.
- Why application-local implementation is insufficient.
- Cross-application reuse.
- Constitutional impact.
- Certified dependency impact.
- Contract impact.
- Data impact.
- Security impact.
- Migration impact.
- Testing requirements.
- Certification requirements.
- Rollback requirements.
- Versioning requirements.

## Permitted Justification Classes
1. Enterprise reuse.
2. Production necessity.
3. Security.
4. Compliance.
5. Certified defect.
6. Compatibility.
7. Operational resilience.
8. Measurable cost reduction.
9. Measurable revenue enablement.

## Automatic Rejection Basis
Reject proposals based only on:
- Preference.
- Speculative future use.
- Cosmetic consistency.
- Convenience.
- Premature abstraction.
- Technology novelty.
- Unproven reuse.

## Review Gate
No platform change proposal may proceed to implementation planning unless this standard is fully satisfied.

## Baseline Protection Rule
All accepted proposals must inherit from GPR-1.0 and preserve additive governance and certification history.
