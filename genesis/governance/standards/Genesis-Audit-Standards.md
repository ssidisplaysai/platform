# Genesis Audit Standards Family

## Purpose
Define permanent audit governance standards that GAR and future audit programs must implement.

## Standards
1. Genesis Audit Framework Standard (GAFS)
2. Genesis Evidence Standard (GEVS)
3. Genesis Baseline Standard (GBLS)
4. Genesis Determinism Standard (GDS)
5. Genesis Mutation Protection Standard (GMPS)
6. Genesis Reporting Standard (GRS)
7. Genesis Governance Traceability Standard (GGTS)
8. Genesis Decision Recording Standard (GDRS)
9. Genesis Supersession Standard (GSS)

## Governance Rule
Audit packages implement these standards and never redefine them.
