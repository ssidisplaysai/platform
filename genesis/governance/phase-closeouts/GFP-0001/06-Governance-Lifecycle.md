# 06 Governance Lifecycle

Closeout classification:

- This artifact is a Foundation Phase I closeout summary.
- Constitutional lifecycle authority is defined by GPD-0001 and GCD-0003.

Authoritative lifecycle summary used by this closeout:

1. Constitutional framing
2. Platform Design Review
3. Design condition resolution, where required
4. Final design approval
5. Engineering blueprint
6. Engineering implementation under GEP-0001
7. Independent certification
8. Engineering hardening and certification condition closure, where required
9. Final certification
10. Platform release
11. Enterprise adoption
12. Mission Control observation

Authority clarification:

- Platform Design Standard and Engineering Process Standard are governance authorities applied across lifecycle stages.
- This closeout summary does not redefine constitutional authority.

Lifecycle mandate:

- No future platform may bypass this lifecycle unless authorized by constitutional amendment.
