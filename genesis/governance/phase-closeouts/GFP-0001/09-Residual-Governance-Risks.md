# 09 Residual Governance Risks

Residual governance risk profile:

1. Standards drift risk
- Risk: future work may under-apply established constitutional/engineering standards.
- Mitigation: enforce governance gate checks at each lifecycle stage.

2. Boundary erosion risk
- Risk: cross-platform ownership boundaries may erode under delivery pressure.
- Mitigation: mandatory PDR and certification boundary evidence enforcement.

3. Evidence incompleteness risk
- Risk: package evidence quality may vary across future domains.
- Mitigation: strict adherence to GEP-0001 evidence and validation standards.

Residual risk conclusion:

- Risks are manageable within the established governance framework.
