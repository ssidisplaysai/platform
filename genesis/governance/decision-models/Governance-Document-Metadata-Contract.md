# Governance Document Metadata Contract

Every governance artifact in this library shall define or inherit:
- authority
- dependencies
- consumers
- successors
- supersessionPolicy

Additional traceability requirements:
- authoritySource
- constitutionReferences
- relatedStandards
- relatedProcedures
- implementationConsumers
- certificationConsumers
- auditConsumers
- evidenceConsumers
