# Genesis Audit Family Overview

## Governance Role
Defines the audit governance family as a permanent branch of the Governance Library.

## Audit Family Members
- GAR (Repository and architecture audit implementations)
- Future domain-specific audit programs

## Binding Rule
All audit members must conform to the audit standards defined in [standards/Genesis-Audit-Standards.md](../standards/Genesis-Audit-Standards.md).

## Scope Boundary
Audit implementations evaluate conformance and produce evidence.
Audit implementations do not redefine governance standards.
