# Genesis Audit Reporting Standard

## Required Report Structure
1. Executive Summary
2. Scope
3. Authority
4. Methodology
5. Inputs
6. Evidence
7. Analysis
8. Findings
9. Exceptions
10. Recommendations
11. Validation
12. Traceability
13. Appendices

## Reporting Rules
- Required sections are mandatory for every audit report.
- Findings, inherited findings, and environmental findings must be separated.
- Report must include determinism and mutation validation statements.
- Report must include machine identifier references for evidence and findings.
