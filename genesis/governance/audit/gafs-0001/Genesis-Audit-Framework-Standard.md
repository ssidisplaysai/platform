## 2. Mission and Scope
This standard defines exactly how audits are planned, executed, validated, documented, and concluded.

This standard governs audit execution and does not define constitutional certification decisions.

## 3. Primary Objectives
This standard defines:
- Audit lifecycle and phases
- Audit inputs and outputs
- Evidence collection and validation methodology
- Baseline handling
- Determinism requirements
- Mutation protection
- Scope definition
- Risk recording
- Finding and observation recording
- Exception handling
- Completion criteria
- Reproducibility and independence
- Audit quality assurance

## 4. Audit Independence
Audit execution authority shall be independent from implementation ownership for audited targets.

## 5. Audit Completion Criteria
An audit is complete only when:
1. Required lifecycle phases are executed.
2. Required outputs are produced.
3. Determinism and mutation controls are validated.
4. QA checks pass.
5. Traceability records are complete.

## 6. GAR Binding Rule
GAR packages implement this framework and do not redefine its methodology.
