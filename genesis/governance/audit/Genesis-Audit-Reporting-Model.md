# Genesis Audit Reporting Model

## Mandatory Report Sections
1. Scope and authority
2. Standards and procedures consumed
3. Baseline identity
4. Determinism verification
5. Mutation verification
6. Findings and severity model
7. Unresolved items
8. Certification and readiness recommendation

## Reporting Constraints
- Report output must distinguish VERIFIED, INFERRED, OPINION, RECOMMENDATION, UNRESOLVED.
- Inherited baseline conditions must be separated from package-specific limitations.
- Audit reports must never redefine audit standards.
