# Genesis Milestone Registration Standard

Standard ID: GMS-0001
Status: ACTIVE
Work Order: GPT-0001
Date: 2026-07-30

## Purpose
Define when a milestone may be registered in the Genesis Hall of Milestones.

## Milestone Qualification Criteria
A milestone must be:
- Historically significant.
- Evidence-backed.
- Enterprise-relevant.
- Distinct from routine engineering completion.
- Durable over time.
- Formally approved.

## Non-Qualifying Events
A milestone shall not be created for:
- Individual commits.
- Routine bug fixes.
- Minor releases.
- Documentation-only updates without historical significance.
- Ordinary feature completion.
- Internal task completion.

## Lifecycle States
- RESERVED.
- PROPOSED.
- VERIFIED.
- RECORDED.
- SUPERSEDED.

## State Definitions
- RESERVED: Placeholder milestone identifier and intent are recorded; no completion claim.
- PROPOSED: Candidate milestone has draft evidence and sponsor request.
- VERIFIED: Evidence and governance review confirm milestone criteria are satisfied.
- RECORDED: Milestone is formally accepted into permanent historical registry.
- SUPERSEDED: Milestone remains historical but is expanded or replaced by successor context.

## Registration Controls
- Each milestone entry must include required fields from the Hall of Milestones schema.
- Evidence references must be immutable and traceable.
- Milestone registration must preserve additive history and must not rewrite prior records.
