## Purpose
Maintain the permanent historical record of major Genesis achievements.

## Distinction Rule
The Hall of Milestones is distinct from:
- Hall of Decisions.
- Hall of Releases.
- Certification Registry.
- Engineering Work Orders.

A milestone records a historically significant achievement rather than a governance or engineering artifact.

## Required Fields
- Milestone ID.
- Title.
- Date.
- Status.
- Classification.
- Description.
- Evidence.
- Related decisions.
- Related work orders.
- Related release.
- Repository commit.
- Git tag.
- Historical significance.
- Superseded or expanded by.
