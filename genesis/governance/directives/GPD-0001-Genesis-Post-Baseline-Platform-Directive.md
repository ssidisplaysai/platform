# GPD-0001: Genesis Platform Baseline Freeze and Enterprise Delivery Directive

Status: ACTIVE
Work Order: GPT-0001
Date: 2026-07-30
Authority: GPR-1.0 (CERTIFIED), GCF-0001, GCF-0001A, GCD-0001..GCD-0005, GPE-0001

## Directive Mission
Formally transition Genesis from foundational platform construction into enterprise capability delivery after certification and publication of Genesis Platform Baseline 1.0.

## Governing Statement
"Genesis Platform Baseline 1.0 is complete and frozen. Future work shall prioritize enterprise capability delivery over platform expansion. Platform changes require demonstrated business justification, production necessity, or reusable cross-application value."

## Directive Declarations
- GPR-1.0 is complete.
- GPR-1.0 is certified.
- GPR-1.0 is frozen.
- Future work shall inherit from GPR-1.0.
- Platform expansion requires explicit justification.
- Business capability delivery takes precedence over abstract platform expansion.
- Certified boundaries shall not be bypassed.
- Certified services shall not be duplicated inside applications.
- Runtime changes to frozen baseline components require a new governed work order.
- Architectural refactoring requires evidence of necessity.
- New platform services require demonstrated cross-application value.
- Application-specific needs remain application-owned unless reusable enterprise value is proven.

## Post-Baseline Priority Tests
Future platform work must demonstrate one or more of the following:
- Measurable enterprise capability.
- Cross-application platform value.
- Production readiness.
- Business operational value.
- Security or compliance necessity.
- Certified dependency remediation.
- Reusable capability required by multiple applications.

## Prohibition Rule
Platform expansion without demonstrated enterprise need is prohibited.

## Compliance Boundaries
- No runtime implementation is authorized by this directive.
- No architecture refactoring is authorized by this directive.
- No new platform service implementation is authorized by this directive.
- No application implementation is authorized by this directive.

## Inheritance and Traceability
All post-baseline work orders must include:
- Baseline inheritance declaration referencing GPR-1.0.
- Certified dependency chain declaration.
- Boundary preservation statement.
- Additive certification evidence when change affects certified components.

## Effectivity
This directive is effective immediately and remains active until constitutionally superseded.
