# Genesis Governance Authority Model

## Authority Chain
Constitution -> Governance Library -> Standards -> Procedures -> Implementations -> Evidence -> Certification -> Operational Decisions

## Authority Rules
1. Constitutional authority is supreme.
2. Governance Library derives authority from the Constitution.
3. Standards are authoritative policy constraints.
4. Procedures operationalize standards.
5. Implementations must declare standards and procedures they consume.
6. Evidence must be traceable to implementation and governance sources.
7. Certification decisions are valid only with traceable evidence.
8. Supersession cannot violate constitutional constraints.

## Required Metadata For Every Governance Artifact
- Authority
- Dependencies
- Consumers
- Successors
- Supersession policy
