# GCD-0002 Constitutional Decision

## Constitutional Determination
The Genesis Governance Library is constitutionally adopted as the permanent governance institution immediately subordinate to the Genesis Constitution.

## Authority Chain Ratified
Genesis Constitution -> Genesis Governance Library -> Governance Standards -> Procedures -> Implementations -> Evidence -> Certification -> Operational Decisions

2. GCCS-0001 is the governing Constitutional Certification Standard.
3. GAFS-0001 is the governing Audit Framework Standard.
