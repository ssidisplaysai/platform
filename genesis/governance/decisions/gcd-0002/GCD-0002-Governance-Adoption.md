# GCD-0002 Governance Adoption

## Adoption Statement
The Genesis Governance Library (GGL-0001) is adopted as a permanent constitutional institution of Genesis, immediately subordinate to the Constitution.

## Operating Rule
All future governance standards, procedures, and governance-controlled implementation programs derive authority from the Governance Library.

## Standards Recognition
- GCCS-0001 governs constitutional certification methodology.
- GAFS-0001 governs audit execution methodology.

## Implementation Rule
Implementation packages shall not redefine governance standards.

## GAR Rule
Future GAR packages shall implement GAFS-0001 and GCCS-0001 as applicable, without redefining either.
