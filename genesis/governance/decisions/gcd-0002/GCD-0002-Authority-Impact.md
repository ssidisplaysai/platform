# GCD-0002 Authority Impact

## Constitutional Impact
No constitutional text changes.

## Governance Impact
Formal constitutional adoption of Governance Library as permanent institution below the Constitution.

## Authority Grants
1. Governance Library gains constitutional recognition as root governance institution below Constitution.
2. Governance standards derive authority through Governance Library.
3. GCCS-0001 and GAFS-0001 are recognized as governing standards in their domains.

## Boundary Safeguards
- Governance Library cannot supersede constitutional authority.
- Audit and certification implementations must apply standards and cannot redefine methodology.
