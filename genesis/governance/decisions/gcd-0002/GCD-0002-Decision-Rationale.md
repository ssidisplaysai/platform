# GCD-0002 Decision Rationale

## Evaluation Questions and Answers
- Does the Governance Library contradict the Constitution?
  - No contradictions identified in reviewed artifacts.
- Does it preserve constitutional supremacy?
  - Yes. Governance authority model explicitly states constitutional supremacy.
- Does it establish clear authority boundaries?
  - Yes. Authority chain and metadata requirements are explicit.
- Does it separate Constitution, Standards, Procedures, Implementations, Evidence, Certification, Audit, and Operational Decisions?
  - Yes. Separation is explicitly defined and modeled.
- Does it avoid redefining constitutional authority?
  - Yes. Governance Library is subordinate and derived.
- Does it establish reusable framework?
  - Yes. It includes standards, procedures, lifecycle, traceability, and machine registries.
- Does it improve maintainability?
  - Yes. Permanent volume model and traceable governance metadata improve maintainability.
- Is it implementation-neutral and production-neutral?
  - Yes. Artifacts are governance-only and non-executing.

## Constitutional Decision Basis
APPROVAL is granted because governance architecture is constitutionally compatible, structured, and reusable without constitutional override or production mutation.
