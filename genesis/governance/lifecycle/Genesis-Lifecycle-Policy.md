# Genesis Governance Lifecycle Policy

## Lifecycle Domains
- Evidence lifecycle
- Certification lifecycle
- Baseline lifecycle
- Governance lifecycle
- Decision lifecycle
- Artifact lifecycle
- Release certification lifecycle
- Re-certification lifecycle
- Appeals and reassessment lifecycle
- Versioning lifecycle
- Supersession lifecycle

## Lifecycle Controls
- State transitions must be explicit and recorded.
- Authority and dependency links must be preserved through lifecycle transitions.
- Supersession must include successor mapping and migration notes.

## Compliance
Lifecycle conformance is assessed through audit standards and certification standards.
