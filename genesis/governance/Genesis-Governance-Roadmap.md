# Genesis Governance Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation (Completed by GGL-0001)
- Establish governance volume model
- Establish standards/procedures/certification/audit families
- Establish authority and traceability model
- Establish machine-readable governance registry

## Phase 2: Standard Adoption
- Adopt GCCS and GATS in upcoming certification programs
- Bind GAR packages to audit standards family
- Require governance metadata in new program artifacts

## Phase 3: Procedure Operationalization
- Bind implementation packages to governance lifecycle procedures
- Introduce periodic governance conformance checks

## Phase 4: Evolution Control
- Operate supersession model with successor mapping
- Run governance appeals and reassessment protocol

## Guardrails
- No governance phase may redefine constitutional authority.
- Governance changes must remain additive and traceable.
