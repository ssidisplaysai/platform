# Genesis Constitutional Certification Roadmap

## Phase 1: Standard Activation
- Publish GCCS-0001 as active governance standard.
- Register schemas and traceability models.

## Phase 2: Procedure Binding
- Bind governance and audit procedures to GCCS-0001 requirements.
- Require clause and decision registry adoption in constitutional assessment workflows.

## Phase 3: Ecosystem Adoption
- Apply GCCS-0001 to runtime, genome, agent, platform, and release constitutional certifications.
- Enforce reporting and traceability requirements as mandatory gates.

## Phase 4: Evolution Governance
- Operate re-certification, appeal, and supersession lifecycle.
- Maintain backward-compatible registry and schema evolution.

## Guardrails
- Constitution remains supreme.
- Audit packages implement this standard and do not redefine it.
- No certification output can omit machine traceability fields.
