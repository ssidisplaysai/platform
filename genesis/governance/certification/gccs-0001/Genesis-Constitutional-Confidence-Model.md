# Genesis Constitutional Confidence Model

## Confidence Ratings
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Verified
- Deterministic

## Threshold Rules
- Certified requires at least High confidence for every required clause.
- Certified With Conditions allows bounded Medium confidence only with explicit compensating conditions.
- Deterministic requires reproducibility evidence for repeated evaluation.
- Verified requires direct, admissible, traceable evidence.
- Low confidence on required clauses triggers Certification Deferred or Not Certified based on severity and missing evidence cause.

## Evidence Threshold Matrix
- Low: Contextual or partial secondary evidence only.
- Medium: Secondary evidence with partial primary corroboration.
- High: Primary evidence with integrity and traceability checks.
- Verified: High plus independent verification path.
- Deterministic: Verified plus reproducible repeated outcomes.
