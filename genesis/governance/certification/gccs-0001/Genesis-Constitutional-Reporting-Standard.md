# Genesis Constitutional Reporting Standard

## Required Sections For Constitutional Certification Reports
1. Executive Summary
2. Authority
3. Scope
4. Inputs
5. Evidence
6. Analysis
7. Clause Evaluation
8. Findings
9. Exceptions
10. Conditions
11. Decision
12. Recommendations
13. Traceability
14. Appendices

## Reporting Rules
- Report must distinguish fact from interpretation.
- Evidence class and confidence must be explicit.
- Clause identifiers and decision identifiers are mandatory.
- Inherited baseline conditions must be separated from package-specific limitations.

## Scope Clarification
This standard defines reporting requirements and does not produce certification reports by itself.
