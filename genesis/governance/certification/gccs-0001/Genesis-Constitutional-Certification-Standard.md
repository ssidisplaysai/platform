## 3. Constitutional Scope
This standard defines:
- Constitutional authority and interpretation rules
- Clause evaluation methodology
- Evidence admissibility and hierarchy
- Compliance determination and decision protocol
- Certification, re-certification, and reassessment protocols
- Traceability and reporting requirements

This standard does not itself certify targets.

## 5. Constitutional Interpretation Rules
1. Literal clause meaning applies unless explicit constitutional cross-clause precedence states otherwise.
2. Constitutional clause dependencies must be evaluated before dependent clauses.
3. Superseded clauses cannot govern active decisions unless explicitly retained.
4. Conflict between subordinate artifacts is resolved by constitutional precedence and traceable governance hierarchy.

## 6. Clause Evaluation Methodology
1. Identify applicable constitutional clauses.
2. Bind evidence set to each clause.
3. Assess evidence admissibility and confidence.
4. Determine per-clause compliance state.
5. Aggregate states using decision protocol.
6. Issue certification decision and conditions.

## 7. Compliance Meaning
- Constitutional compliance means all applicable clauses satisfy required evidence and confidence thresholds.
- Constitutional non-compliance means one or more applicable clauses fail required thresholds.
