# Genesis Constitutional Certification Lifecycle

## Lifecycle States
- Draft
- Proposed
- Ratified
- Active
- Certified
- Re-certification Pending
- Superseded
- Withdrawn
- Revoked
- Archived

## Certification Protocol
1. Register target and jurisdiction.
2. Bind applicable clauses.
3. Validate evidence admissibility.
4. Evaluate clauses and aggregate compliance.
5. Issue decision and conditions.
6. Register lifecycle transition and traceability links.

## Re-certification Protocol
Re-certification is triggered by:
- Expiry window
- Constitutional clause change
- Material implementation or governance change
- Evidence supersession
- Appeals outcome

## Appeals and Reassessment
1. Register appeal with authority and reason.
2. Reassess clause-evidence mapping.
3. Update decision state with full traceability.
4. Preserve prior decision lineage.

## Supersession and Revocation
- Superseded decisions require successor decision identifiers.
- Revoked decisions require explicit constitutional basis and effective time.
