# Genesis Constitutional Severity Model

## Severity Levels
- Informational
- Minor
- Moderate
- Major
- Critical
- Constitutional Blocker

## Decision Rules By Severity
- Informational: No direct compliance impact.
- Minor: Can contribute to COMPLIANT WITH CONDITIONS.
- Moderate: Requires remediation condition and due date.
- Major: Can prevent Certified unless explicitly conditioned by governing authority.
- Critical: Prevents Certified; typically yields Not Certified.
- Constitutional Blocker: Immediate Not Certified unless clause is NOT APPLICABLE.

## Severity Aggregation
Highest applicable severity across required clauses governs maximum achievable decision state.
