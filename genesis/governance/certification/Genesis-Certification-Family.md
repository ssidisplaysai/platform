# Genesis Certification Family Overview

## Scope
Permanent certification standards defined by governance authority.

## Certification Standards
- GCCS: Genesis Constitutional Certification Standard
- GATS: Genesis Architecture Certification Standard
- GBAS: Genesis Business Agent Certification Standard
- GEAS: Genesis Enterprise Agent Certification Standard
- GBGS: Genesis Business Genome Certification Standard
- GRTS: Genesis Runtime Certification Standard
- GAPS: Genesis Application Platform Certification Standard
- GECS: Genesis Executive Certification Standard

## Governance Constraints
- Certification standards define conformance requirements.
- Certification implementations are out of scope for this library.
- Certification outcomes require traceable evidence and governing standards references.
