# Genesis Certification Standards

## GCCS
Genesis Constitutional Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify conformance to constitutional principles and authority constraints.

## GATS
Genesis Architecture Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify architecture conformance, boundary integrity, and approved decision lineage.

## GBAS
Genesis Business Agent Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify Business Agent governance conformance and dependency integrity.

## GEAS
Genesis Enterprise Agent Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify Enterprise Agent governance conformance and authority alignment.

## GBGS
Genesis Business Genome Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify Business Genome model and compiler governance conformance.

## GRTS
Genesis Runtime Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify runtime governance conformance and operational safety constraints.

## GAPS
Genesis Application Platform Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify application platform conformance to governance, architecture, and evidence policies.

## GECS
Genesis Executive Certification Standard

Purpose: Certify executive governance, decision accountability, and traceability compliance.

## Common Certification Requirements
- Authority reference
- Standards and procedures consumed
- Evidence set and provenance
- Decision outcome and lifecycle state
- Supersession and re-certification policy
