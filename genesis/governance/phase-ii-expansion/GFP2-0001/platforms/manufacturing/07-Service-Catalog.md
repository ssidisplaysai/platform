# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Manufacturing Platform:

- ProductionPlanService, ProcessStateService, TraceabilityService, QualityGovernanceService, ManufacturingReferenceService, ManufacturingAuditService, ManufacturingMetricsService, ManufacturingHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
