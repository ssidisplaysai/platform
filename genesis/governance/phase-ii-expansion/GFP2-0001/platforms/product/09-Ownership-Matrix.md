# 09 Ownership Matrix

Owned by Product Platform:

- Product Definitions, Product Hierarchies, Product Variants, Product Lifecycle States, Product Classification Semantics

Consumed from other platforms:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Workflow, Document, Asset, Messaging
- Optional: Scheduling, Notification, AI Orchestration, Analytics, Mission Control

Explicitly NOT owned:

- Inventory quantities, Commerce transactions, Manufacturing execution, Finance ledger ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
