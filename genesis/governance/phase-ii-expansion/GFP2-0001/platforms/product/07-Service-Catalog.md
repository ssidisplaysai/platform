# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Product Platform:

- ProductRegistryService, VariantHierarchyService, ProductClassificationService, ProductLifecycleService, ProductPolicyService, ProductReferenceService, ProductAuditService, ProductMetricsService, ProductHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
