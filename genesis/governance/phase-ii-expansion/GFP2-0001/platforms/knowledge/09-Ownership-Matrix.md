# 09 Ownership Matrix

Owned by Knowledge Platform:

- Knowledge Articles, Knowledge Taxonomy, Knowledge Relationships, Knowledge Publication Semantics, Knowledge Lifecycle Semantics

Consumed from other platforms:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Contact, Document, Workflow, Messaging, Notification
- Optional: Scheduling, Asset, AI Orchestration, Mission Control

Explicitly NOT owned:

- Document artifact custody, Asset custody, Identity ownership, Authorization ownership, Workflow ownership, Mission Control ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
