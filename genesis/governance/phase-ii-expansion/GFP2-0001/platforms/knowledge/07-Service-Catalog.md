# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Knowledge Platform:

- KnowledgeRegistryService, TaxonomyService, KnowledgeGraphService, PublicationGovernanceService, LifecyclePolicyService, CitationService, KnowledgeAuditService, KnowledgeMetricsService, KnowledgeHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
