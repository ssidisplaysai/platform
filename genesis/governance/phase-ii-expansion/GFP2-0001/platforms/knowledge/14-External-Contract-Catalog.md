# 14 External Contract Catalog

Platform-specific contract catalog for Knowledge Platform:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Contact, Document, Workflow, Messaging, Notification required; Scheduling, Asset, AI, Mission Control optional

Contract catalog controls:

- Contracts are conceptual, version-intent, and implementation-neutral.
- Commands, queries, events, references, and observations remain distinct.
- Contracts do not transfer ownership.
