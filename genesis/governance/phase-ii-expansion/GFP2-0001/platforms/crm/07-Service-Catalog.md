# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for CRM Platform:

- AccountRelationshipService, EngagementTimelineService, OpportunityLifecycleService, SegmentationPolicyService, CRMReferenceService, CRMAuditService, CRMMetricsService, CRMHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
