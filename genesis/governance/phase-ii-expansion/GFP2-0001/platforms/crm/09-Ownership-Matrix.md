# 09 Ownership Matrix

Owned by CRM Platform:

- Account Relationships, Opportunity Semantics, Engagement Timeline, Customer Interaction Governance

Consumed from other platforms:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Contact, Messaging, Notification, Workflow
- Optional: Scheduling, AI Orchestration, Analytics, Mission Control, Document

Explicitly NOT owned:

- Commerce order ownership, Finance receivables ownership, Contact identity ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
