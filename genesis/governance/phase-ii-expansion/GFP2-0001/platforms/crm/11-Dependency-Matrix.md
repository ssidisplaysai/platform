# 11 Dependency Matrix

Required dependencies:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Contact, Messaging, Notification, Workflow

Optional dependencies:

- Scheduling, AI Orchestration, Analytics, Mission Control, Document

Forbidden dependencies:

- Customer master identity duplication and autonomous messaging transport ownership

Dependency direction:

- Inbound consumer posture from this platform to certified upstream contracts.

Anti-circular guarantee:

- Circular dependency and circular ownership patterns are prohibited.
