# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Analytics Platform:

- MetricDefinitionService, InsightCatalogService, AnalyticalModelGovernanceService, LineageService, AnalyticsReferenceService, AnalyticsAuditService, AnalyticsMetricsService, AnalyticsHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
