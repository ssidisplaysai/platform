# 09 Ownership Matrix

Owned by Analytics Platform:

- Analytical Models Semantics, Metric Definitions, Insight Catalog, Decision-Intelligence Governance

Consumed from other platforms:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Messaging, Document
- Optional: Knowledge, Product, CRM, Manufacturing, Inventory, Finance, Commerce, AI Orchestration, Mission Control

Explicitly NOT owned:

- Source platform ownership, transactional execution ownership, Mission Control ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
