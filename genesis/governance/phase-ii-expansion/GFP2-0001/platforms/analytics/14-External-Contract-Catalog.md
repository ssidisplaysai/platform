# 14 External Contract Catalog

Platform-specific contract catalog for Analytics Platform:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Messaging, Document required; Knowledge, Product, CRM, Manufacturing, Inventory, Finance, Commerce, AI, Mission Control optional

Contract catalog controls:

- Contracts are conceptual, version-intent, and implementation-neutral.
- Commands, queries, events, references, and observations remain distinct.
- Contracts do not transfer ownership.
