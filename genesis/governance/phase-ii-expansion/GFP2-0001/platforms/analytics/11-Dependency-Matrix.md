# 11 Dependency Matrix

Required dependencies:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Messaging, Document

Optional dependencies:

- Knowledge, Product, CRM, Manufacturing, Inventory, Finance, Commerce, AI Orchestration, Mission Control

Forbidden dependencies:

- Mutation ownership over source-domain business behavior

Dependency direction:

- Inbound consumer posture from this platform to certified upstream contracts.

Anti-circular guarantee:

- Circular dependency and circular ownership patterns are prohibited.
