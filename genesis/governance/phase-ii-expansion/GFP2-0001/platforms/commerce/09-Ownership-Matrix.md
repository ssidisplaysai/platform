- Commercial Offers, Order Semantics, Pricing Policy Semantics, Fulfillment State Semantics

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Contact, Product, Inventory, Workflow, Messaging, Notification
- Optional: Scheduling, AI Orchestration, Analytics, Mission Control, Document

- Payment rail implementation ownership, Inventory ownership, Finance ledger ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
