# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Commerce Platform:

- OfferService, OrderGovernanceService, PricingIntentService, FulfillmentStateService, CommerceReferenceService, CommerceAuditService, CommerceMetricsService, CommerceHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
