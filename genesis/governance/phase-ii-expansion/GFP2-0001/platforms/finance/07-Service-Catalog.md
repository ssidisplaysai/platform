# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Finance Platform:

- FinancialEventService, LedgerSemanticsService, ComplianceControlService, FiscalPolicyService, FinanceReferenceService, FinanceAuditService, FinanceMetricsService, FinanceHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
