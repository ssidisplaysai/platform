# 09 Ownership Matrix

Owned by Finance Platform:

- Ledger Semantics, Financial Events Classification, Fiscal Controls, Compliance Traceability

Consumed from other platforms:

- Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Organization, Workflow, Document, Messaging
- Optional: Notification, Scheduling, AI Orchestration, Analytics, Mission Control

Explicitly NOT owned:

- Commerce checkout ownership, Inventory movement ownership, Product lifecycle ownership

Ownership assertion:

- Every concept in this platform dossier has exactly one canonical owner.
