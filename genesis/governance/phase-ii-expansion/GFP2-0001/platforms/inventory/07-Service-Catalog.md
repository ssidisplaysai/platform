# 07 Service Catalog

Platform-specific conceptual services for Inventory Platform:

- StockStateService, MovementGovernanceService, AllocationPolicyService, ReservationService, InventoryReferenceService, InventoryAuditService, InventoryMetricsService, InventoryHealthService

Service-catalog requirements:

- Each service must remain ownership-bounded.
- Service inputs and outputs are contract-governed.
- Service behavior remains conceptual at this stage.
