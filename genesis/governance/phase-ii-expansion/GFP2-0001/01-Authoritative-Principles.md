# 01 Authoritative Principles

Authoritative enterprise principles for Phase II:

1. The Genesis Compiler is the constitutional producer of canonical business knowledge.
2. The Business Genome is the canonical enterprise knowledge model.
3. Enterprise Platforms consume canonical truth and never redefine canonical truth.
4. One owner per enterprise capability.
5. Contract-first integration.
6. Consumer-only dependencies.
7. Mission Control remains observational only.
8. AI may reason, recommend, summarize, and orchestrate.
9. AI may never own business capability.
10. No duplicate ownership.
11. No circular ownership.
12. No implementation decisions in constitutional planning.
13. No provider-specific, storage-specific, or infrastructure-specific commitments.
