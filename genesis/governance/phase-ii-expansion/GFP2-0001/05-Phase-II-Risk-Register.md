# 05 Phase II Risk Register

Portfolio-level risks:

1. Cross-platform ownership drift
- Severity: Medium
- Mitigation: enforce ownership matrix and constitutional review checkpoints.

2. Contract inconsistency across platforms
- Severity: Medium
- Mitigation: maintain shared contract fabric and review uniformity.

3. Dependency inflation and hidden coupling
- Severity: Medium
- Mitigation: explicit forbidden dependencies and anti-circular checks.

4. AI and Mission Control scope creep
- Severity: Medium
- Mitigation: permanent non-ownership declarations and certification checks.

5. Portfolio fragmentation risk
- Severity: Medium
- Mitigation: common package standards and recurring architecture-board convergence reviews.
