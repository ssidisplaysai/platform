# 08 Legacy Equivalence Assessment

Assessment objective:

- Confirm lifecycle authority reconciliation does not retroactively invalidate previously certified platforms.

Findings:

1. Legacy package generations use non-uniform naming and metadata conventions.
2. Equivalent governance evidence remains present for certified baseline capabilities.
3. GCD-0003 explicitly codifies non-retroactive legacy equivalence policy.

Conclusion:

- Historical certified and released platforms remain valid.
- Future platform work remains bound to reconciled lifecycle authority model.
