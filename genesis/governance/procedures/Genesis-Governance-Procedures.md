# Genesis Governance Procedures

Authority: [../Genesis-Governance-Authority-Model.md](../Genesis-Governance-Authority-Model.md)

## 1. Evidence Lifecycle Procedure
- Register evidence artifact with identifier, producer, and governance links.
- Validate deterministic reproduction metadata.
- Lock evidence state and hash references.
- Bind evidence to certification and audit consumers.

## 2. Certification Lifecycle Procedure
- Declare target certification standard.
- Validate prerequisite evidence set.
- Execute standard-specific conformance checks.
- Record certification decision and validity window.

## 3. Baseline Lifecycle Procedure
- Establish approved baseline with canonical hashes.
- Record baseline authority source and scope.
- Require explicit disposition for baseline replacement.
- Preserve baseline lineage through supersession metadata.

## 4. Governance Lifecycle Procedure
- Draft governance artifact.
- Propose with authority and dependency declarations.
- Ratify through governance authority path.
- Activate and publish in governance registry.

## 5. Decision Lifecycle Procedure
- Register governance decision identifier and scope.
- Record decision authority and affected standards.
- Capture rationale and alternatives.
- Publish successor and supersession links when replaced.

## 6. Artifact Lifecycle Procedure
- Create artifact metadata envelope.
- Validate required traceability fields.
- Publish artifact in registry.
- Archive with successor mapping when superseded.

## 7. Release Certification Lifecycle Procedure
- Bind release candidate to required certification standards.
- Confirm all required evidence and audit outputs.
- Issue release certification decision with scope and validity.

## 8. Re-certification Lifecycle Procedure
- Trigger on expiry, critical change, or failed conformance signal.
- Re-run certification controls with updated evidence.
- Preserve prior certification lineage.

## 9. Appeals and Reassessment Procedure
- Register appeal request with governing authority.
- Reassess evidence and decision record.
- Publish decision reaffirmation or revision.

## 10. Versioning Procedure
- Assign version and status transition.
- Record compatibility and migration notes.
- Update governance registry and cross-reference graph.

## 11. Supersession Procedure
- Declare superseded and successor artifacts.
- Provide rationale and transition constraints.
- Preserve historical traceability and authority chain.
