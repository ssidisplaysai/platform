# 03 Governance Lifecycle Audit

Target lifecycle under audit:

Constitution -> Constitutional Vision -> Platform Design Standard -> Platform Design Review -> Condition Resolution -> Final Design Approval -> Engineering Blueprint -> Engineering Process Standard -> Engineering -> Independent Certification -> Engineering Hardening if required -> Final Certification -> Platform Release -> Enterprise Adoption -> Mission Control Observation

Evidence reviewed:

- GFP-0001 06-Governance-Lifecycle defines the full sequence above.
- GEP-0001 Engineering lifecycle and release handoff sections align to blueprint-to-release stages.
- PDR-1001 through PDR-1001C and GKN-0000 provide explicit Knowledge lifecycle evidence through design approval and blueprint.

Historical classification:

1. Platforms created before finalized Governance Framework:
- Early legacy engineering and release packages (pre-GCV/GPD/GEP finalization) rely on equivalent evidence patterns.

2. Platforms created under partial governance:
- Mid-lineage capabilities that required hardening or recertification packages to meet later standards.

3. Platforms designed under complete governance lifecycle:
- Knowledge design lifecycle (PDR-1001 -> PDR-1001B -> PDR-1001C -> GKN-0000).
- Phase II constitutional designs (GFP2-0001) and independent integrity review (GFP2-0001A).

Lifecycle audit result:

- PASS WITH GOVERNANCE ALIGNMENT FINDING

Reason:

- Repository lifecycle evidence is broadly coherent, but constitutional lifecycle wording is not yet perfectly unified across all constitutional sources.

Historical resolution reference:

- GSA-R001 and GCD-0003 subsequently resolved lifecycle authority inconsistency and established authority-versus-execution interpretation.
