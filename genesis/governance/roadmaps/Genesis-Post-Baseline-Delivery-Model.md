# Genesis Post-Baseline Delivery Model

Status: ACTIVE
Work Order: GPT-0001
Date: 2026-07-30
Baseline: GPR-1.0 (CERTIFIED, FROZEN)

## Purpose
Define the post-GPR-1.0 delivery phases that transition Genesis from foundational platform construction into enterprise capability delivery.

## PHASE I - CERTIFIED PLATFORM
Status: COMPLETE

Contents:
- Constitution.
- Governance.
- Registry.
- Health.
- Mission Control.
- GLW integration.
- GPR-1.0.

## PHASE II - ENTERPRISE IDENTITY
Mission:
Create one reusable identity and access layer for every Genesis application.

Planned packages:
- GID-1001: Identity Foundation.
- GID-1002: Authentication.
- GID-1003: Authorization.
- GID-1004: Workspace Federation.
- GID-1005: Session Management.
- GID-1006: Enterprise Permissions.
- GID-1007: Single Sign-On.

Required principles:
- Identity is platform-owned.
- Applications do not implement independent authentication authority.
- Permissions are centrally defined and application-consumed.
- Session and workspace identity remain reusable.
- Cross-application access remains policy-governed.

## PHASE III - SSI ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION
Mission:
Integrate SSI as the flagship operational enterprise application ecosystem.

Expected domains:
- CRM.
- Quotes.
- Sales Orders.
- Projects.
- Manufacturing.
- Inventory.
- Purchasing.
- Shipping.
- Installations.
- Service.
- Finance.
- Reporting.
- Operational dashboards.

Required principles:
- SSI retains business-domain ownership.
- Genesis owns shared platform services.
- SSI modules consume certified platform capabilities.
- No platform capability shall be duplicated inside SSI.

## PHASE IV - STONER ENTERPRISE INTEGRATION
Mission:
Validate Genesis against a retail, ecommerce, licensing, membership, community, and collector business model.

Expected domains:
- Ecommerce.
- Retail.
- Memberships.
- STONER Passport.
- Collector Ecosystem.
- Limited drops.
- Licensing.
- Events.
- Community.
- Influencer programs.
- Affiliate and partner attribution.
- Product registration.
- Marketing.

Required principles:
- STONER business logic remains STONER-owned.
- Shared platform services remain Genesis-owned.
- Customer identity shall consume Genesis Identity.
- Collector and Passport capabilities shall not be generalized prematurely.

## PHASE V - SHARED ENTERPRISE CAPABILITIES
Expected areas:
- Finance platform.
- Manufacturing platform.
- CRM platform.
- Partner platform.
- Affiliate platform.
- Commission and payout platform.
- Shared document services.
- Shared notification services.
- Enterprise search.
- Reporting and analytics.

Rule:
Cross-application reuse must be demonstrated before promotion into the platform.

## PHASE VI - ENTERPRISE INTELLIGENCE
Mission:
Introduce intelligence only after multiple applications generate trustworthy, governed enterprise data.

Expected capabilities:
- AI agents.
- Enterprise knowledge graph.
- Cross-company analytics.
- Workflow optimization.
- Predictive operations.
- Sales intelligence.
- Manufacturing optimization.
- Autonomous workflows.
- Digital twins.
- Executive intelligence.
- Enterprise simulation.

Required principles:
- Intelligence consumes governed platform data.
- Agents do not bypass application or platform authority.
- Autonomous decisions remain auditable.
- Intelligence recommendations remain traceable to evidence.

## Execution Constraint
This model authorizes planning and governance sequencing only. Implementation requires separately approved governed work orders.
