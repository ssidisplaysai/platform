# Genesis Governance Lifecycle

## Lifecycle Stages
1. Draft
2. Proposed
3. Ratified
4. Active
5. Certified
6. Superseded
7. Archived

## Lifecycle Rules
- No artifact may skip Ratified before Active.
- Superseded artifacts require explicit successor linkage.
- Archived artifacts must retain traceability metadata.
- Re-certification follows procedure-defined intervals or trigger events.

- Evidence lifecycle
