# Business Genome Foundation Baseline

Baseline Name: Business Genome Foundation
Baseline Version: 1.0
Baseline State: Frozen
Baseline Package: BGF-0001

## Baseline Scope
This baseline includes the canonical object, evidence, and relationship authorities established by BGP-0001, BGP-0002, and BGP-0003.

## Baseline Invariants
- Identity remains deterministic and canonical.
- Evidence remains traceable to source authority.
- Relationships remain governed by semantic contract.
- Validation remains a governance concern before any engineering implementation.

## Baseline Exclusions
- No ingestion implementation.
- No storage implementation.
- No connector implementation.
- No runtime implementation.
- No inference engine implementation.

## Baseline Consumption Rule
All future Business Genome engineering packages SHALL reference this baseline as the authoritative starting point.
